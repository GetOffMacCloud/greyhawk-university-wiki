# 📌 Contract #0010 — The Hall of the Honored
**Posted by:** Kethra Tims, for the Chair of Flan Antiquities | **Date:** 0576-09-03 C.Y.
**Danger Rating:** 3 / 5 | **Mission Type:** 📜 Field Thesis | **Arc:** The Bones of Tharuun, session 2 of 17

---

> [!abstract] The Posting
> *SILBERTAL — CONTINUATION. Students posted to the Silbertal Field Station are advised that yesterday's investigation of the Königsruh mausoleum found the missing family and both sentinels, one of the family alive. The disturbance is the work of tomb robbers who entered from* beneath *the graveyard through stone older than the town. Their trail leads down. The town council of Königsruh has posted a bounty of 250 marks a head for the robbers' two leaders, a sorcerer called Xeron and a priest of Hextor called Krootad, the latter of whom animated the dead of the family concerned.*
>
> *Students are wanted to follow the trail into the older complex, record what they find, and — the Chair's words — bring back the script before the sword. Board at the Krone und Kohl continues. Miss Tims will accompany the expedition, and asks that nobody tell her not to.*
>
> *— K. Tims, Silbertal Field Station, for I. Brantwald, Magistra*

> **500 Credits per student upon successful audit**, plus Discovery Credits for the older complex. The Königsruh council's bounty (250 marks a head, in Perrenland marks) is separate and belongs to the students, as does any civic reward for the return of the town's property.

> [!warning] Field Conditions
> The complex beneath the mausoleum is of pre-Migration Flan construction and was sealed deliberately. Students should expect wards, guardians, and the tomb robbers' rear guard. The University reminds students that the honored dead of another people are to be treated as one would wish one's own treated.
