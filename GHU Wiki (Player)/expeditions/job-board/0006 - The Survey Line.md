# 📌 Contract #0006 — The Survey Line
**Posted by:** [[people/townsfolk/Sigmund Holt]] | **Date:** 0576-11-19 C.Y.
**Danger Rating:** 3 / 5 | **Mission Type:** ⚔️ Frontier Vigil

---

> [!abstract] The Posting
> *A herder off the autumn migration reports that the University's survey markers on the Ochre Escarpment have been interfered with — each peg ringed in soot, deliberately, and a raven on the rim that does not flush. Those markers were placed by a University party in the spring. Someone has read them, and wanted us to know it.*
>
> *I need a field team on the survey line: confirm the condition of markers R1 through R3, document the interference, and identify — at range, if you have any sense — who or what is keeping watch up there. This is a verification climb, not a punitive expedition. Come back. That is the assignment.*
>
> *— S. Holt, Research Officer, Ritterstadt Field Station*
