# 📌 Contract #0011 — The Labyrinth of Friends
**Posted by:** Kethra Tims, for the Chair of Flan Antiquities | **Date:** 0576-09-04 C.Y.
**Danger Rating:** 3 / 5 | **Mission Type:** 📜 Field Thesis | **Arc:** The Bones of Tharuun, session 3 of 17

---

> [!abstract] The Posting
> *SILBERTAL — THIRD POSTING. The priest of Hextor who raised the dead of Königsruh has been dealt with. The king's four companions have been found in their vault, and their belongings recovered to the University's keeping. A paper taken from the priest bears a word:* Vanguard. *The Chair is informed.*
>
> *Below the companions' vault the robbers descended into a natural cavern and beyond it into a labyrinth which, by the account of a prisoner, the tomb's builders made to turn thieves back and to help the honest through. The sorcerer Xeron is beyond it, a day ahead, with the king's bones and the king's sword. Students are wanted to follow. The Chair's instruction stands: the script before the sword — but the Chair has not seen the sword.*
>
> *— K. Tims, Silbertal Field Station, for I. Brantwald, Magistra*

**Party Size:** six scholars, about 4th level

> [!info] University Credit Reward
> **500 Credits per student upon successful audit**, plus Discovery Credits for the labyrinth's workings and whatever the sorcerer's crew left behind. The Königsruh council's bounty on Xeron (250 marks a head, in Perrenland marks) stands and belongs to the students.

---

> [!warning] Field Conditions
> The labyrinth is warded and, by report, summons. Rope is advised. The University notes, without comment, that the tomb's makers built the way for the honest, and that students of good conscience may find the going easier than others.
