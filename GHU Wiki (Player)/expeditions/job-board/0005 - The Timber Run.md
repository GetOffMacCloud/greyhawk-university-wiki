> [!abstract] The Posting
> *The Field Station expansion is a season behind because no caravan will carry a full timber load up the south road. Goric Ashendelf has agreed to run twelve cartloads from the Verbobonc yards in one convoy — on the condition the University puts armed scholars on the wagons. Every carrier who has run that road short-handed this summer has come back lighter than they left, and one of Goric's outriders never came back at all.*

> *I want the timber in the yard before the frost. I am past caring what has to be moved out of the way to get it here.*
