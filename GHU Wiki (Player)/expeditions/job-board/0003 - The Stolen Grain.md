# 📌 Contract #0003 — The Stolen Grain
**Posted by:** [[people/townsfolk/Vogt Ruprecht Ackermann]] | **Date:** 0576-10-14 C.Y.
**Danger Rating:** 1 / 5 | **Mission Type:** ⚒️ Civic Project

---

> [!abstract] The Posting
> *The Vogt's ledger does not balance. Over three months, grain has been leaving the village surplus store faster than the harvest accounts for — enough to feed two or three people not listed on the census. It is subtle. It is deliberate. And entering deep winter, Ritterstadt cannot afford to lose a single bushel.*
>
> *Vogt Ackermann is not looking for a public reckoning. He wants to know who, and why, and he wants it handled without turning neighbour against neighbour. Speak to him privately at the Vogt's office if you are willing to work with discretion.*
