# 📌 Contract #0004 — The Man from the South
**Posted by:** [[people/townsfolk/Lotte Kesselring]] | **Date:** 0576-10-16 C.Y.
**Danger Rating:** 2 / 5 | **Mission Type:** 🕵️ Civic Investigation

---

> [!abstract] The Posting
> *A man arrived in Ritterstadt three days ago. Southern accent. New coin. Staying at the Grey Wolf and asking questions — about the road north, about the old stone tower, about whether anything "unusual" has happened in the village lately. He is pleasant about it. That is what worries me.*
>
> *His name is Vethkar. I don't know who sent him. I don't know what he wants. I know that Konrad has gone very quiet since the formal introduction, and that Hildegard is watching him and won't say why. Someone needs to find out what this man is actually doing here — carefully, before he finishes doing it.*
>
> *— L.K.*
