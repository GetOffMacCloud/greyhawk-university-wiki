# 📌 Contract #0009 — The Mourners Who Didn't Return
**Posted by:** Magistra Ilse Brantwald, Chair of Flan Antiquities | **Date:** 0576-09-02 C.Y.
**Danger Rating:** 3 / 5 | **Mission Type:** 📜 Field Thesis | **Arc:** The Bones of Tharuun, session 1 of 17

---

> [!abstract] The Posting
> *FIELD POSTING — SILBERTAL. The Chair of Flan Antiquities requires students for a term at the Silbertal Field Station, Canton Silbertal, Perrenland, under the direction of Miss K. Tims, graduate assistant. Duties: survey and measurement of a pre-Migration monument; test pits; drawing; carrying of chains. Board and lodging at the Krone und Kohl, Königsruh, at the University's expense.*
>
> *Amended, by hand, in a different ink:* Miss Tims writes that the town has stopped talking to her. In a Perrenland canton that means the town is frightened. Students on this posting should expect to find out of what.
>
> *— I. Brantwald*

---

**University Credit Reward:** 500 Credits per student upon verified resolution (partial 250–375 each, DM's call; APL 5 × 100)
**Contact:** Kethra Tims at the Silbertal Field Station, Königsruh — or, from Ritterstadt, the Chair's office
**Party Size:** six scholars, about 4th level
**Estimated Duration:** one field session (3½ hours). This is the first of a series; students may take any session of the arc as it is posted

---

> [!info] University Credit Reward
> **500 Credits per student upon successful audit**, plus Discovery Credits for anything the survey plates don't already hold. A civic bounty from the Königsruh council, paid in Perrenland marks, is separate and belongs to the students.

---

> [!warning] Field Conditions
> Perrenland clan law applies in Silbertal: bands on the doorposts, the dress-law, lawful feud. The University's students are guests of the canton. Wolves have been seen in the hills above the town this autumn, which the town's farmers consider normal and the town's council does not.
