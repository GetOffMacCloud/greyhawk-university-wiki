# 📌 Contract #0012 — The Betrayer's Watch
**Posted by:** Kethra Tims, for the Chair of Flan Antiquities | **Date:** 0576-12-04 C.Y.
**Danger Rating:** 3 / 5 | **Mission Type:** 📜 Field Thesis | **Arc:** The Bones of Tharuun, session 4 of 17 — **the last of the barrow**

---

> [!abstract] The Posting
> *SILBERTAL — FOURTH POSTING. The labyrinth has been crossed. A survivor of the robbers' company has been recovered and has given an account: the sorcerer Xeron carries a map of the tomb, is not human, and has abandoned his own hirelings to go on alone. A pendant in the shape of three heads has been taken from the robbers' camp and is the subject of a separate report.*
>
> *Beyond the robbers' camp a waterfall falls past a ledge, and beyond the ledge lies what the sorcerer told his company was the king's tomb. The University is advised that it may not be. Students are wanted to follow the ledge to its end, to record what they find, and to recover the king's remains before they are carried further. The Chair's instruction stands: the script before the sword.*
>
> *— K. Tims, Silbertal Field Station, for I. Brantwald, Magistra*

> **500 Credits per student upon successful audit**, plus Discovery Credits for the tomb's record. The Königsruh council's bounty on Xeron stands (**150 marks a head**, the balance of Ratsherr Turbrandt's 250) and belongs to the students.

> [!warning] Field Conditions
> The ledge behind the falls is iced in Sunsebb; rope and cold-weather gear are required, not advised. A resident of the falls is reported. **Students are asked to be courteous to it.** The road to Exag is closed for the winter; there is no help nearer than Königsruh.
