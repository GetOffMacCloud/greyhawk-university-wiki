## The Expedition
At dawn on the twenty-fourth of Patchwall, the Field Office woke six of us and put the [[expeditions/session-reports/2026-11-13 - The Pilgrims of the Hollow Bell (Kalbach)|first team's report]] in front of us: grey pilgrims who bless wells, and dead who walk five nights later.

We rode east past Kalbach to the courier relay at **Hollen Ford**, where the dead had beaten us to it. We cleared the relay, and found a sister's notes, a page listing old graves, and more of the skull coins.

On the road to the Tor we found a camp of orc deserters from Iuz's war, holding the path for the pilgrims. [[students/Friday Night Field Trips/Draven Ironskull|Draven]] challenged their chief, **Grukka One-Tusk**, to single combat and beat him. The band chose to come with us rather than die for the pilgrims.

We didn't climb the hill. Grukka and three of his band walked back to Ritterstadt with us, on our word that there's work for them here and a better life than the one they left. Nobody fell.

**The team:** [[students/Friday Night Field Trips/Draven Ironskull|Draven]], [[students/Friday Night Field Trips/Krate|Krate]], [[students/Friday Night Field Trips/Henry McCoy|Hank]], [[students/Friday Night Field Trips/Zephyrine Winzig|Zephyrine]], [[students/Friday Night Field Trips/Abina|Abina]] and [[students/Friday Night Field Trips/Uriel Sonnenbrecht|Uriel]].

## What We Found
- **A list of old graves**, copied in a fair hand. The first name is struck through. Carn Aedh was next.
- **Sister Halse's notes**: the pilgrims' own record.
- More **skull coins**.
- The orcs were paid to hold the path "until the dark of the moon, on the hill."

## Loot & Rewards
- **Credits:** 225 each (a partial: the relay and the orcs, but not the bell). Draven earned +15 more for the duel with Grukka.
- **Grukka's band** owes us a favour, and they're our friends now.
- More credit is waiting for whoever writes up the list, the orcs and the relay in a field report.
