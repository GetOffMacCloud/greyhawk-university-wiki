## The Expedition
The Field Office sent six of us east to shadow a procession of grey-robed pilgrims who call themselves mendicants of Wee Jas. They walk the drove roads behind a handcart, offering to bless wells and pray for the dead. Our orders were to watch and report, not to fight.

We followed them to the hamlet of **Kalbach**, where they had blessed the well. On the fifth night the dead rose: three of Kalbach's own, and the bones of beasts with them. [[students/Friday Night Field Trips/Elric Embersmile|Elric]] had shored up the hamlet's defences, and we fought them in the churchyard and the lanes and put them down.

Afterwards we reburied the dead in the churchyard and blessed the ground. We stayed the night and rode back to the University in the morning.

**The team:** [[students/Friday Night Field Trips/Elric Embersmile|Elric]], [[students/Friday Night Field Trips/Aeliana|Aeliana]], [[students/Friday Night Field Trips/Tu'ree|Tu'ree]], [[students/Friday Night Field Trips/Henry McCoy|Hank]], [[students/Friday Night Field Trips/Vaelin Saelanth|Vaelin]] and [[students/Friday Night Field Trips/Angus Thistlebottom IX|Angus]].

## What We Found
- Wherever the pilgrims bless a well, the weak die within three days and rise on the fifth night.
- **Two lead coins** stamped with a skull and a scythe, pulled from the mud.

## Loot & Rewards
- **Credits:** 75 each (a quarter share, since the second team finished the job). Elric and Angus each earned a roleplay bonus.
- The coins went to the **Department of Arcane Studies**, where the staff are analysing them.
