## The Expedition
In the first week of Brewfest, a Perrender merchant named **Ottokar Brandmeier** hired six of us to lift an iron "heirloom" from the Archive's sealed stacks: 25 marks each, half up front.

We did the legwork first. [[students/Friday Night Field Trips/Uriel Sonnenbrecht|Uriel]] attended Professor Vogelsang's lecture. [[students/Friday Night Field Trips/Zephyrine Winzig|Zephyrine]], [[students/Friday Night Field Trips/Sybil|Sybil]] and [[students/Friday Night Field Trips/Morana Thrym|Morana]] studied in the Archive's reading room. [[students/Friday Night Field Trips/Elric Embersmile|Elric]] and [[students/Friday Night Field Trips/Felix Fleetfoot|Felix]] went to the Registrar's counting-room.

On the night of the Warden's Brewfest dinner we went in, with Elric drawing off the curious guards while the rest of us slipped into the sealed stacks. At the handover on the quay before dawn, Brandmeier woke from an enchantment he hadn't known he was under, and the job turned. His "clerk," Vesharan, went over the rail and away into the fog.

## What We Found
- The "heirloom" was the **Link**, a relic the City sent north under seal two years ago.
- Brandmeier had been tethered by someone else's magic since spring.
- Vesharan is still out there.

## Loot & Rewards
- **Credits:** 300 base, plus extra credit for freeing Brandmeier and for a clean job. Uriel and Elric each earned a roleplay bonus.
- Brandmeier's 25 marks each, and his friendship in Schwartzenbruin.
- Professor Vogelsang owes us a favour.
- Lecturer Tock gave each of us a **Rubbing of the Six**.
