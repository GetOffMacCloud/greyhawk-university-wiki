# Brunhilde Hammerschlag

![[Brunhilde_Hammerschlag.png|300]]

*Human · Blacksmith · Hammerschlag Forge, Ritterstadt*

Bruni runs the Hammerschlag Forge — Ritterstadt's full-service smithy — and has since her husband Dietrich died in the northern pass three winters ago. She is the hamlet's industrial backbone: weapons, armor, and structural metalwork that no one else in the village can produce.

A powerfully built woman, work-hardened from years at the forge, with hands so calloused she doesn't feel heat the way others do. She doesn't fidget. She speaks in spare, controlled sentences, asks precise questions, and waits for precise answers. Her prices are fair and she doesn't haggle — that's the only warmth she extends until she's decided she trusts you.
