# Drunik Runevault

![[Drunik_Runevault.png|300]]

*Dwarf · Mining Consortium Liaison · University Field Station, Ritterstadt*

Drunik is the Ritterstadt Mining Consortium's man on the frontier — the commercial counterpart to the University's academic office. He commissions field expeditions, assays returned specimens, and pays in University credit vouchers the same day, drawn on the Consortium liaison office. Many of the job-board postings at the Fiend's Rest are his.

Broad and unhurried, with stone-grey in his beard and the permanent squint of a man who reads mineral grain in poor light. A loupe on a cord and an assayer's pick are always within reach. He speaks in short, exact sentences and corrects sloppy terminology by reflex — learn the difference between *ferrotholite* and "rust-red" and he'll like you better. Real business happens in the back room of the Fiend's Rest, behind the hearth.

## Dealings

| Matter | Notes |
| :--- | :--- |
| Field commissions | Clear requirements, fair pay, same-day settlement — *"Bring it back sealed and intact."* |
| Assay & appraisal | Mineral specimens graded on the spot |
| Mineral lore | Will talk grades and method longer than you'd like |

## Known Connections

Commercial counterpart to [[people/townsfolk/Sigmund Holt]] — they cooperate, and openly disagree about what matters. His commissions have made him the Ritterstadt Questers' most regular employer.
