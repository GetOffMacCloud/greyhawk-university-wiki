# Hanz Korngut

![[Hanz_Korngut.png|300]]

*Human · Miller · Die Mühle am Bach, Ritterstadt*

Hanz operates the village's only mill. Without him, Ritterstadt cannot process its grain surplus — and beyond the millstones, he is quietly the settlement's most reliable keeper of uplands folklore and old family memory. He won't volunteer it; it comes out in conversation.

An aging man, always flour-dusted, with deeply calloused hands and a considering look that suggests he's heard more than he says. He moves slowly and deliberately, like a man who has spent decades working with heavy machinery that punishes carelessness. Slow to speak, careful when he does, and he refers to old stories as plain facts.

## Known Connections

His ledger is overseen by [[people/townsfolk/Vogt Ruprecht Ackermann]]. Shares a quiet mutual respect with [[people/townsfolk/Vater Sigmund Wolfherz]]. Buys from and sells to every farming family in the hamlet, including the Beckenhaus longhouse.
