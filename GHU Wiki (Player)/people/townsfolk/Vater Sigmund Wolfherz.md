# Vater Sigmund Wolfherz

![[Sigmund_Wolfherz.png|300]]

*Human · Village Priest & Healer · St. Erika's Chapel, Ritterstadt*

Vater Sigmund is the spiritual and medical center of Ritterstadt. He buries the dead, heals the sick, settles disputes with quiet authority, and serves as the closest thing the hamlet has to a living institutional memory.

A gentle elder with careful, clean hands and simple robes. His quarterstaff is worn smooth from decades of use, and a healer's kit is always at his side. He speaks quietly and at length, rarely in a hurry, and prefers to share something and wait for your response rather than ask a direct question.

Not to be confused with **Sigmund Holt**, the University's Field Research Officer — a different Sigmund entirely, a coincidence that regularly confuses newcomers.
