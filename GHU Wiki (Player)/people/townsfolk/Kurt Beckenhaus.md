# Kurt Beckenhaus

![[Kurt_Beckenhaus.png|300]]

*Human · Farmer · The Beckenhaus Longhouse, Ritterstadt farmsteads*

Kurt is one of Ritterstadt's farming residents, arrived with his family from Edgerton — a small village two days east — after three bad harvests made staying untenable. He works the family's longhouse field, pulls his weight in communal labor, and has a reputation as a reliable if quiet neighbor.

Late twenties but looks older, with the particular exhaustion of a man who hasn't slept cleanly in months: his daughter Marta, six years old, has had a wasting illness since midsummer, and nothing the family has tried — the priest's remedies, prayer, an expensive herb packet from a Traft trader — has turned it. He is polite to the point of stiffness with strangers and relaxes only in increments.

The household is four adults and two children: Kurt, his wife Marta-elder, his father Gottfried, and his mother Ilse, who cares for little Marta while Kurt works. The second child, a boy of seven, is healthy and cheerful.

## Known Connections

[[people/townsfolk/Vater Sigmund Wolfherz]] has treated little Marta and remains the family's trusted healer. The family trades grain through [[people/townsfolk/Hanz Korngut]]'s mill like every farmstead in the hamlet.
