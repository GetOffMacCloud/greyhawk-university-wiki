# Lotte Kesselring

![[Lotte_Kesselring.png|300]]

*Human · Innkeeper · The Grey Wolf, Ritterstadt*

Lotte has run the Grey Wolf for twelve years, and the Grey Wolf is where Ritterstadt's social life happens. If something is going on in the village, she usually knows about it before anyone official does.

Practical working clothes, hair pinned back efficiently, always moving — never idle behind the bar. Her grey dog, **Grauer**, follows her like a shadow, and his opinion of a newcomer is a more reliable gauge of hers than anything she'll say. She asks exactly one apparently-innocent question of every new arrival, and refills your drink without being asked when she wants you to keep talking.
