# Hogar Irongrip

![[Hogar_Irongrip.png|300]]

*Mountain Dwarf · Blacksmith's Apprentice (acting) · The Iron Anvil, Ritterstadt*

Hogar is currently the only person operating The Iron Anvil, and everyone in the village knows why: his master, Brakken Hammer-Fall, went missing three weeks ago. Hogar keeps the fires lit and handles the hamlet's basic tool repair, sharpening, and simple metalwork — competently, if nervously.

A young dwarf with a thin, poorly-braided beard and a leather apron several sizes too large (it was Brakken's). His hands are steadier when he's working than when he isn't. He mumbles the steps of smithing out loud to remember them — *"heat to orange, not yellow — Brakken said orange"* — and quotes his master like scripture.

## Services

| Service | Notes |
| :--- | :--- |
| Tool repair & sharpening | Standard rate; he's good at this |
| Basic fabrication | Nails, brackets, hinges |
| Edge sharpening (weapons) | He can do it; he just won't say so confidently |
| Weapon or armor fabrication | Not without supervision — he will refuse |

## Known Connections

Apprentice (and indentured) to the missing Brakken Hammer-Fall. Slightly in awe of [[people/townsfolk/Brunhilde Hammerschlag]], the village's full smith, and tries not to show it.
