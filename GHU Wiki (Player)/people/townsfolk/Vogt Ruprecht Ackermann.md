# Vogt Ruprecht Ackermann

![[Ruprecht_Ackermann.png|300]]

*Human · Reeve & Administrator · The Ritter's Hall, Ritterstadt*

Ruprecht is the reeve of Ritterstadt — the Ritter's appointed administrator, tax collector, and record-keeper, and the person who ensures the lord's decisions actually get implemented. He has held the position for nineteen years, through the end of the old Ritter's tenure and all of Konrad's, and knows where every coin in the village has come from and where it has gone.

Fifty-five, thin in a papery way, with ink-stained fingers and quick eyes that inventory a room in the first second and spend the rest of the conversation checking the math. Efficient, organized, and quietly indispensable, with a smooth, pleasant smile that never quite reaches warm.
