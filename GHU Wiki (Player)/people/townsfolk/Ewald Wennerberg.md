# Ewald Wennerberg

![[Ewald_Wennerberg.png|300]]

*Human · Garrison Soldier · The Guardhouse, Ritterstadt*

> [!warning] Died 12 Harvester, 576 CY
> Killed by a gorgon on the descent from the northern pass, travelling under the Questers' escort. He was the first to fall in that fight. The party carried the news, and what remained of him, back to the garrison.

Ewald was the garrison's second watch leader — a competent, reliable soldier who pulled difficult rotations without complaint and handled equipment maintenance with real care. He volunteered for the northern watches more often than most.

Average height, average build, brown hair kept short in the manner of military habit, clean-shaven when most of the garrison isn't. His uniform was always regulation-correct. He was friendly in conversation and readier with a question than an answer — the sort of easy small talk that makes a watch pass quickly.

He said he was from Traft originally, with a stint in a private company in the south before signing on here.

## The Northern Pass

He was met on the road well off his patrol line, moving north, and asked to travel with the party from that point on. He gave a good account of himself and was a steady hand for four days. He did not come back down.
