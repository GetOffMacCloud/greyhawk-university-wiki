# Ritter Konrad von Eisfeld

![[Konrad_von_Eisfeld.png|300]]

*Human · Feudal Lord · The Ritter's Hall, Ritterstadt*

Konrad is the hamlet's feudal lord — the legal, military, and social authority of Ritterstadt. He commissions the garrison, approves contracts, and sets the tone for how the village presents itself to outsiders.

An older man with a soldier's bearing that age hasn't entirely taken from him. He receives guests in fine clothes worn over chain mail, his longsword always at his hip. He speaks in measured, deliberate sentences and never wastes words. Direct and candid about the dangers of the uplands; considerably more reserved about everything else.

## Dealings

| Matter | Notes |
| :--- | :--- |
| Quest commissions (civic contracts) | Pays promptly and clearly from his own funds |
| Garrison coordination | Through [[people/townsfolk/Hauptmann Hildegard Stahlwulf]], with his endorsement |
| Village records | By request through the Vogt's office |
| Formal introductions | Available to a party he's judged capable |

Co-custodian, with the University's [[people/townsfolk/Sigmund Holt]], of the Academic Registry in the Ritter's Hall annex.
