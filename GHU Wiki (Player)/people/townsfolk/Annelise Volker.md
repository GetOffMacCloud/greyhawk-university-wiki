# Annelise Volker

![[Annelise_Volker.png|300]]

*Human (Flan) · Exchequer (Schat-Rat) · Klauberstadt*

Annelise Volker is the Exchequer of Klauberstadt, called "The Iron Ledger" behind her back. A native of the Sepia Uplands with strong Flan roots, she possesses an uncanny ability to sniff out hidden mineral wealth and fiscal discrepancies alike. While the rest of the council worries about walls and steel, Annelise focuses on what she calls the lifeblood of the Reach: coin.

Sharp, analytical features and a cool, measuring gaze; austere, well-kept frontier dress; a coin-scale, an abacus, and requisition papers always at hand. She is known for her cold demeanor and her refusal to approve even a single copper for the militia without a signed requisition form.

## Dealings

| Matter | Notes |
| :--- | :--- |
| Requisitions & disbursements | Signed forms only. No exceptions. Truly none. |
| Assay & valuation disputes | Her ledger is generally treated as final |

## Known Connections

Sits on the Klauberstadt Council of Trust. Works closely — in their shared, wordless way — with High Constable [[people/townsfolk/Gunter Hellsing]].
