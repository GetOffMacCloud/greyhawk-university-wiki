# Sigmund Holt

![[Sigmund_Holt.png|300]]

*Human · University Field Research Officer · Ritterstadt Satellite Station*

Sigmund Holt is the Field Research Officer of the Greyhawk University Ritterstadt Satellite Station — the University's senior academic authority on site. He commissions and reviews field work, distributes survey instruments, signs off student papers and research filings, and is co-custodian (with the Ritter) of the Academic Registry lockbox in the annex of the Ritter's Hall.

Middle-aged and lean, with ink-stained fingers and the slight forward stoop of a man who reads by candlelight more than he sleeps. He carries a tri-compass and a field ledger everywhere, speaks in complete sentences precise to the point of dryness, and asks for things *"in writing, with notice."* Examination requests go to him in writing with at least seven days' notice; credentialed Questers he answers personally.

Not to be confused with **Vater Sigmund Wolfherz**, the village priest — a different Sigmund entirely.

## Dealings

| Matter | Notes |
| :--- | :--- |
| Academic commissions & credit awards | Correct, procedural, reliable |
| Survey instruments & method | Genuinely enthusiastic — will talk method longer than you want |
| Registry examination requests | In writing, seven days' notice, both keyholders required |

## Known Connections

Co-keyholder of the Academic Registry with [[people/townsfolk/Ritter Konrad von Eisfeld]]. Academic counterpart to the Consortium's [[people/townsfolk/Drunik Runevault]] — they cooperate, with visible friction over priorities.
