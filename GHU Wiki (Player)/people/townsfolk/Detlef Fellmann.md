# Detlef Fellmann

![[Detlef_Fellmann.png|300]]

*Human · Furrier & Licensed Field-Materials Buyer · The Drying Shed, Ritterstadt*

Fellmann runs The Drying Shed, the racks-and-tanning yard at the edge of Ritterstadt where pelts are stretched, scraped, and cured. He is the village's only proper furrier and holds a University Requisition charter as a licensed buyer: bring him skins from a sanctioned hunt and he weighs, grades, and posts **University Credits** straight to your ledger. He handles no gold with students — the University settles his account.

A broad, stooped elder in a much-mended fur coat that is its own advertisement. Grey stubble, a face like cured leather, and a left hand missing the last two fingers to old frostbite — he'll tell you the story for free. He talks to the hides, not the hunter: turns a skin over, clucks his tongue, and names the flaw before he names the price. Never haggles up; states a fair number once and means it. Calls every student "pup" regardless of age or ancestry.

## The Pelt Board — buy rates

**Value = Base (creature) × Quality (condition).** He grades condition on sight; a clean kill skinned with care is worth far more than a thing burned, dissolved, or hacked apart.

| Creature tier (examples) | Base Credits |
| :--- | :---: |
| Tiny game — hare, marten, fox | 3 |
| Wolf, boar, giant rat | 8 |
| Dire wolf, black bear, giant badger | 20 |
| Brown bear, great elk | 50 |
| **Winter wolf** *(prized: cold-warding fur)* | **75** |
| Large or exotic predators | 120 |
| Rare or clearly magical hide | By appraisal — usually routed to the University |

| Grade | Condition | ×Base |
| :--- | :--- | :---: |
| Pristine | Clean kill, skinned with care | ×1.0 |
| Serviceable | Ordinary kill, rough skinning | ×0.6 |
| Damaged | Fire / acid / lightning / over-chopped | ×0.25 |
| Ruined | Charred, dissolved, shredded | no value |

Skinning takes about ten minutes per beast and a blade — a proper skinning kit helps. Fire and acid ruin pelts; consider your tactics before the fight, not after.

> He can pay out roughly **300 Credits per tenday** from his University float. Beyond that he writes an honest chit — *"redeem when the wagon comes up from Leukish, pup"* — and the Credits post, just delayed.

## The Wanted Board — standing bounties

A slate nailed beside the Drying Shed door. Big, dangerous quarry he holds special orders for, at a premium over the Pelt Board — values are for pristine hides, and most exceed his float, so expect a chit for the balance.

| Wanted | Ranges in | Bounty *(Pristine)* |

| Bulette *("landshark")* | Uplands scree & broken moor | 200 cr |
| Gorgon *(iron-scaled bull)* | The high stony moors | 225 cr |
| Giant Crocodile | The Quag Mire fringes | 175 cr |

*"These I'll pay dear for, pup. Bring 'em in whole if you can. Burn 'em or boil 'em and you've cost us both."*
