# Hauptmann Hildegard Stahlwulf

![[Hildegard_Stahlwulf.png|300]]

*Human · Garrison Commander · The Guardhouse, Ritterstadt*

Hildegard commands Ritterstadt's garrison — the hamlet's only organized military force. She sets patrol routes, responds to threats, and is the first line of defense against gnoll incursions, wolf raids, and unwanted visitors.

Always in chain mail when in public, no exceptions, with a longsword at her hip and sometimes a hand crossbow. Military bearing, hair kept short and practical, back to the wall when she can manage it. She speaks in direct, economical sentences, answers exactly what was asked and nothing more, and states her limits plainly rather than pretend to authority she doesn't have.

## Dealings

| Matter | Notes |
| :--- | :--- |
| Threat assessment / briefing | Available to any party she's judged capable — direct and specific, with maps |
| Garrison coordination | Sanctioned missions only; she cooperates when the Ritter has commissioned the work |
| Patrol intelligence | Earned — she shares routes with parties she's come to rely on |

## Known Connections

Loyal to [[people/townsfolk/Ritter Konrad von Eisfeld]] and his vision for the hamlet. Works alongside [[people/townsfolk/Vogt Ruprecht Ackermann]] within the same authority structure. Commands the garrison's soldiers, including watch leader [[people/townsfolk/Ewald Wennerberg]].
