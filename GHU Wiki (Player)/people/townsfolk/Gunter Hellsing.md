# Gunter Hellsing

![[Gunter_Hellsing.png|300]]

*Human · High Constable (Rat-Konstabler) · Klauberstadt*

Gunter Hellsing is the High Constable of Klauberstadt. A veteran of the brutal skirmishes along the Perrenland border, he carries the scars of nomadic raids and the pragmatic weight of frontier justice. He is a man of few words, preferring the sharp snap of a bowstring to the long-winded debates of the council.

Hard-bitten, close-cropped hair, an old raid-scar across a grim weathered face. Boiled leather and mail, a hunting bow over one shoulder. He views the Sepia Uplands as a land that must be tamed with iron and vigilance, and serves as the iron fist that protects the town's budding commerce.
