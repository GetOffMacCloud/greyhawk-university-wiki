# Professor Wilhelmina Brandt

![[Wilhelmina_Brandt.png|300]]

*Human · Professor of Antiquarian Wardcraft · Visiting Faculty, from the City of Greyhawk*

Professor Brandt holds the Greyhawk University chair in Antiquarian Wardcraft and is the University's foremost living authority on pre-Migration Flan script and artifacts. She traveled to Ritterstadt in Wealsun 576 on a supervised examination request — the frontier finally produced something worth the journey.

Tall, straight-backed, past sixty and utterly unbothered by it. Iron-grey hair pinned severely, expensive city tailoring worn without apology, a jeweler's loupe on a chain and a roll of bronze reading-styli she never lets out of reach. She speaks in declaratives, does not soften findings, and interrupts hedging — say "it might be" and she'll answer *"it is, or it isn't — which."* She thinks by lecturing and expects you to keep up.

Professional rival and reluctant ally of [[people/townsfolk/Sigmund Holt]], who has been working from her published lexicon for months. Works around [[people/townsfolk/Ritter Konrad von Eisfeld]]'s custody rules with visible impatience. Has decided the Ritterstadt Questers are competent — high praise, from her.
