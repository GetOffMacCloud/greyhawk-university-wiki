# Student Organizations

Greyhawk University's student organizations are as much a part of an education as any Major. Each is *hosted* by one of the [[factions/00 — Department Index|Eight Departments]] — that's who lends the room and signs for the equipment — but **membership is open to students of every Major**. Clubs are where cross-department friendships (and expedition parties) are made.

---

## Joining an Organization

- **You may join one organization.** Time is finite; the University expects full commitment to the club you choose.
- **Membership is open** to any student in good standing, regardless of Major, unless an entry says otherwise.
- **Most clubs have no application process** — show up, participate, demonstrate commitment. Some have a brief **initiation task** noted in their entry.
- Benefits that refresh "once per semester" reset at semester turnover (see the [[factions/00 — Department Index|academic calendar]]).
