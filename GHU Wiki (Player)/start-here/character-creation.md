In the cold frontier of the Northern Sepia, characters are as much the product of geography and survival as of lineage and training. The full list of what you may build from — every permitted race, class, subclass, background and house rule — lives in the **Character Gazetteer**, which is shared by every table in the setting. This section covers only what is different about Ritterstadt: who is common here, who is rare, and which callings the frontier actually has work for.

> [!important] Rules reminder
> 2014 rules only. No feats. If a subclass is not in the Character Gazetteer, it is not in play, and the options marked ⚠ there need the DM's approval before you bring them north.

### Callings the Frontier Has Work For

Magic is rare, dangerous, and viewed with bone-deep suspicion by the common folk of the Sepia Uplands, whose grandparents grew up in the shadow of what magic can do to a continent. Steel, woodcraft and a strong back are understood. Every permitted class is playable here; these are the ones the Sepia itself seems to produce.

- **Barbarians** come out of Wolf Nomad warrior culture or the brutal survival logic of the Quag Mire margin. The Uirtag Badger and the Thillonrian Berserker are the local traditions; Zealots are sometimes consecrated by the Old Faith druids as living embodiments of Ehlonna's or Beory's wrath against whatever is fouling the north.
- **Fighters** of the Battle Master and Champion kind are the frontier's backbone: pragmatic, tactical, and trained to survive ambushes on open moor. A Cavalier or a Banneret of Holy Shielding is a Shield Lands exile who followed trouble north. The Furyondian Crusader shield-wall is the other tradition the dwarves respect.
- **Rangers** are the single most useful thing Ritterstadt can recruit. Gloom Stalkers for the Bloom-darkened margin, Monster Slayers with undead experience, and the Wolf Sentinels and Mist Walkers of the northern forests.
- **Druids** are rooted in the Old Faith of the Flan, the living theological tradition of the Sepia Uplands. Land druids here are Moor or Arctic specialists; Moon druids serve the enclave at Ehlonna's Mount as shape-shifting scouts. The Circle of Winter has a professional interest in the Bloom.
- **Clerics** of St. Cuthbert (Order) are the pragmatic spine of Perrenlander civic faith; Grave clerics of Wee Jas study what the Quag Mires produce; Life clerics of Pelor or Ehlonna keep students alive; Knowledge clerics of Boccob are University-aligned almost by definition.
- **Paladins** of the Watchers oath are uniquely at home here: they are mandated to guard against planar leaks and fiendish incursions, and the Quag Mires are exactly that kind of thin-boundary country. Of the Greyhawk oaths, St. Cuthbert, Pelor and Beory are the ones the frontier recognizes.
- **Rogues** of the Inquisitive kind serve as agents for the Cantons or, more quietly, as the University's intelligence arm in the field. Masterminds in this region are frequently former Scarlet Brotherhood-adjacent operatives who have changed allegiances at least once.
- **Monks** are foreign here. A Shadow monk in the Northern Frontier is almost certainly connected to the Scarlet Brotherhood's intelligence network; an Open Hand monk sometimes emerges from the druid enclave's ascetic traditions. The Wayfarers pass through on the roads.
- **Wizards** are Guild of Wizardry graduates almost without exception. Divination is the most immediately practical discipline where the terrain actively resists mapping; Abjuration is the appropriate response to a region that generates undead. The Bloom Crystals are the reason the University is here, and the reason a wizard is welcome.
- **Sorcerers** are distrusted. Wild Magic in the northern Sepia is often attributed to exposure to Bloom Crystals rather than bloodline; Storm Sorcerers come off Lake Quag's seasonal gales; an Aberrant Mind is, almost universally, very bad news.
- **Warlocks** are not chosen; they are made. Nobody enrolls at Ritterstadt as a warlock. If the bog's collective dead, an aspect of Iuz, or whatever is interested in the Fiend's Embrace decides to make an offer to a student in the field, the DM will make it, and the price will be part of the story.
- **Bards** of the College of Lore serve as living archives of a region with very few written records; Valor bards sometimes travel with the nomad bands as memory-keepers for cultures that distrust writing.

### Permitted Classes & Subclasses

Eleven classes may be chosen at character creation; the Artificer is not part of our Greyhawk, and the **Warlock is a DM-given class** that only enters play when the DM offers a pact. Every subclass below is the **2014** version. Options marked ⚠ exist in the world but need the DM's approval before you bring one to the table. Anything not listed here is not in play. The master copy, with the full reasoning, is Permitted Classes & Subclasses.

### Backgrounds

All 2014 Player's Handbook backgrounds are permitted. Five Greyhawk backgrounds tie you to a corner of the Flanaess: Baklunish Caravaneer (Ket, Tusmit), Marcher Scout (Gran March, Keoland, Geoff), Sentinel of the Velverdyva (Furyondy, Veluna), Ulek Trade-Smith (the Ulek States) and Yatil Mountaineer (Perrenland, the Yatils). The last is the natural choice for a Ritterstadt student.
