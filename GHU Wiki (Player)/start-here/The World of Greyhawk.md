# Welcome to the Flanaess

The year is **576 CY (Common Year)**. You stand in the eastern portion of the continent of **Oerik**, a land known as the **Flanaess**. This is a world of ancient mysteries, crumbling empires, and a sky lit by two moons: **Celene** (The Handmaiden) and **Luna** (The Mistress).

Greyhawk is a world of grey. It is grittier and more dangerous than most. Magic is rare and feared — most commoners have never seen a fireball. The Great Kingdom to the east is rot-stricken and mad. To the north, the demigod **Iuz** carves a kingdom of blood. You live in the space between collapsing giants, in a frontier hamlet called **Ritterstadt**, at the edge of everything.

## Peoples of the Flanaess

![[00. Start Here - Peoples of the Flanaess.png|1000]]

The Flanaess is home to five human ethnicities and a wide variety of demi-human peoples. Your character's ancestry shapes how others read them at a glance — in a frontier hamlet like Ritterstadt, where outsiders are rare and trust is earned, it matters.

### Human Ethnicities

Five waves of migration, ancient catastrophe, and centuries of displacement have produced the ethnic patchwork of the Flanaess. Most people carry some mixture of these bloodlines, but cultural identity often runs deeper than appearance.

| Ethnicity | Common Appearance | Background |
|---|---|---|
| **[[library/peoples/Human/The Baklunish\|Baklunish]]** | Golden-hued skin, dark hair | Masters of trade and ancient elemental magic from the west |
| **[[library/peoples/Human/The Flannae\|Flan]]** | Bronze/copper skin, wavy dark hair | The first people; deeply connected to the earth and the Old Faith |
| **[[library/peoples/Human/The Oerdians\|Oeridian]]** | Tan/olive skin, athletic build | The conquerors who brought current law, knighthoods, and organized religion |
| **[[library/peoples/Human/The Suloise\|Suel]]** | Pale skin, platinum or white hair | Survivors of a magical apocalypse; often scholarly, reclusive, or cold |
| **[[library/peoples/Human/The Rhennee\|Rhennee]]** | Small, dark, wiry build | The River People; live on barges and keep to their own secret laws |

### Demi-Human Peoples

Humans are the dominant population of the Sepia Uplands, but they are far from the only people here. The dwarven clanholds of the Clatspur Mountains, gnome prospectors in the eastern valleys, and reclusive elven watchers on the Vesve border are all part of the region's fabric — and all view Ritterstadt with varying degrees of wariness.

| Race | Presence in the Region | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **[[library/peoples/Elves/Elven Race\|Elves]]** — [[library/peoples/Elves/High Elves\|High]], [[library/peoples/Elves/Sylvan Elves\|Sylvan]], [[library/peoples/Elves/Grey Elves (Faerie)\|Grey]], [[library/peoples/Elves/Valley Elves\|Valley]] | Rare | Reclusive scholars and Vesve border guardians; long memories and longer grudges |
| **[[library/peoples/Dwarves/Mountain Dwarves\|Dwarves]]** — [[library/peoples/Dwarves/Mountain Dwarves\|Mountain]], [[library/peoples/Dwarves/Hill Dwarves\|Hill]] | Significant (regional) | The Clatspur clanholds; forge-masters of the western Teeth; autonomous and proud |
| **[[library/peoples/Gnomes/Rock Gnomes\|Gnomes]]** — [[library/peoples/Gnomes/Rock Gnomes\|Rock]], [[library/peoples/Gnomes/Deep Gnomes (Svirfneblin)\|Svirfneblin]] | Significant (regional) | Masters of the eastern valleys; engineers, prospectors, and natural illusionists |
| **[[library/peoples/Halflings/Lightfoot\|Halflings]]** — [[library/peoples/Halflings/Lightfoot]], [[library/peoples/Halflings/Stout]], [[library/peoples/Halflings/Tallfellow]] | Uncommon | Valley communities; scouts and traders with impeccable local knowledge |

## The Calendar

The year is **364 days long**, divided into twelve months of 28 days each. Between seasons, four **7-day Festival Weeks** mark the turning of the year:

| Festival | Season | Character |
|---|---|---|
| **Needfest** | Midwinter | Indoor celebration; warding off the cold. The darkest time of year. |
| **Growfest** | Early Spring | Planting season. Special prayers to **Beory** (The Earth Mother). |
| **Richfest** | Midsummer | The longest days; dedicated to **Pelor** (The Sun). |
| **Brewfest** | Harvest | The most raucous festival; testing the year's ale. A time of communal relief. |

The current date is the **12th of Planting, 576 CY** — early summer by the calendar, but the Blackfrost Bloom has pushed temperatures three weeks behind seasonal norms. Expect frost at night.

See **[[library/calendar|The Calendar and Festivals]]** for the full twelve-month structure and month names.

## Faith of the Common Folk

![[00. Start Here - Faiths of the Flanaess.png|1000]]

You don't have to be a cleric to know who to call upon. In the Sepia Uplands, three powers dominate daily life:

**[[library/deities/Pelor]]** *(The Sun Father)* — For light, healing, and strength against the dark. The most widely venerated deity on the northern frontier.

**[[library/deities/Beory]]** *(The Oerth Mother)* — For the crops, the land, and the safety of the lake. The Old Faith druids at Ehlonna's Mount serve her alongside Ehlonna herself.

**[[library/deities/St. Cuthbert]]** *(The Saint of Common Sense)* — For law, community, and the truth of the cudgel. The faith of working people who distrust ornamentation.

In Ritterstadt specifically, **St. Erika's Chapel** venerates **Saint Erika of the Long Night**, a localized aspect of [[library/deities/Pholtus]] particular to the uplands frontier — the guardian of travelers in darkness and those who endure winter.

See **[[library/Faith of the Common Folk]]** for the full pantheon and their relevance to the campaign.
