## University Departments

*Every student at Greyhawk University declares a department. This determines your specialisation, your access to advanced resources, and your field assignment priorities.*

| Department | Focus | Bonus |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **Arcane Studies** | Magic theory, spell research | Access to higher-tier spell scrolls |
| **Natural Philosophy** | Wilderness, ecology, survival | Advantage on Survival in the Sepia Uplands |
| **Military History** | Tactics, weapons, fortifications | Proficiency with one martial weapon group |
| **Theology & Rites** | Deities, faith, undead lore | Channel Divinity recharge on short rest (1/day) |
| **Commerce & Logistics** | Trade, negotiation, supply chains | Discount at University Exchange |

*Department mechanics are finalized at Level 2 (Sophomore Year).*

> [!info] Bannerets of Holy Shielding
> Our version of the *Purple Dragon Knight* (SCAG). The rules are unchanged, but the knights belong to the Knights of Holy Shielding of the occupied Shield Lands. A proven Banneret may enter the order's prestige class at level 10. See Permitted Classes & Subclasses.
