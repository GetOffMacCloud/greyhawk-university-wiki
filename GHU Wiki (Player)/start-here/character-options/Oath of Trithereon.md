# ⚔️ Sacred Oath — The Oath of Trithereon

*"You were not born owing anyone your obedience. Whoever told you otherwise was selling something."*

The Oath of Trithereon is sworn by paladins who serve the Summoner — the god of individual liberty, self-defense, and retribution. Where other oaths bind a paladin **to** something — an order, a code, a crown — this one binds them **against**: against the collar, the cell, the compelled tongue, the hand that takes what is not offered.

Trithereon's paladins are called **Summoners**, the same title the faith gives its clergy, because the work is the same: calling people to their own liberation, and calling oppressors to answer. They keep no chapterhouses and hold no ranks. A Summoner who finds another Summoner defers to experience and nothing else.

> [!warning] This oath is not comfortable company
> Its adherents free prisoners without asking whose prisoners they were, and they pursue oppressors past the point where lawful faiths would call the matter settled. Paladins of **Heironeous** and **St. Cuthbert** consider the Oath of Trithereon reckless; the Summoners consider both of them collaborators who have not noticed yet. They fight side by side constantly and argue about it every time.

---

## Tenets of the Summoner

*Drawn from the Three Tenets in [[library/deities/Trithereon|Trithereon]]'s dogma, with the fourth added by long practice.*

**Break the Chain.** No being may rightfully own another. Slavery, bondage, the compelled mind, the debt written to be unpayable — every one is a chain, and every chain you find is a chain you break. Every person freed is an act of worship.

**The Right to Answer.** The right to self-defense is absolute. A person attacked may answer with whatever force stops the attack. Never counsel a victim to endure. Never disarm someone for the comfort of the people hurting them.

**Retribution Is Justice.** Oppressors who face no consequence continue. Making them answer is not vengeance dressed as virtue — it is the work.

**Never Build the Cage.** The one that breaks Summoners. You will be tempted to hold someone "for their own good," to keep a prisoner because releasing them is inconvenient, to take command because you would use it better. The moment you do, you are the thing you swore against.

## Channel Divinity

At 3rd level, you gain the following two Channel Divinity options.

### Sunder the Chain
As an action, you touch one willing creature (which can be yourself) and speak the Summoner's call. Choose one condition currently affecting it: **charmed, frightened, grappled, paralyzed, or restrained.** That condition ends, and for the next minute the creature has **advantage on saving throws and ability checks made to resist or escape that same condition.**

You may instead use this Channel Divinity to instantly **break one set of mundane bonds** you touch — manacles, rope, a cell door's lock, a slaver's collar — regardless of its construction.

### The Reckoning
As a bonus action, you name one creature you can see within 30 feet and mark it for the Reckoning for 1 minute, or until you are incapacitated.

The first time each turn you hit the marked creature with a melee weapon attack, it takes an extra **1d6 radiant damage.** If that creature has **reduced a creature other than you to 0 hit points within the last minute**, the extra damage is **1d10** instead.

> *Speak the accounting aloud when you mark them. The faith holds that an unspoken Reckoning is only a grudge.*

## Aura of the Unbound *(7th level)*

You and friendly creatures within **10 feet** of you can't be **charmed** while you are conscious.

In addition, a creature in the aura that begins its turn **grappled, restrained, or paralyzed** may use its **reaction** to immediately make one saving throw or ability check to end that condition, if the condition normally allows one.

At **18th level**, the range increases to **30 feet.**

---

## Answer for Answer *(15th level)*

The right to self-defense is absolute, and you have made it literal. When a creature you can see within 5 feet of you **hits you with an attack**, you can use your **reaction** to make one melee weapon attack against it.

---

## Aspect of the Summoner *(20th level)*

As an action, you take on the aspect of the god who does not kneel. For **1 minute**:

- **The triskelion burns** above you in bright light out to 30 feet, dim light 30 feet beyond.
- You and friendly creatures within 30 feet are **immune** to the **charmed, frightened, grappled, paralyzed, and restrained** conditions.
- Whenever a hostile creature within 30 feet **reduces a creature other than you to 0 hit points**, you can use your **reaction** to teleport up to 30 feet to an unoccupied space you can see and make one melee weapon attack against that creature.

Once you use this feature, you can't use it again until you finish a **long rest.**

## Breaking This Oath

Most oaths break through cruelty or cowardice. This one usually breaks through **Never Build the Cage** — and it breaks the same way every time, which is to say *reasonably.*

A Summoner falls when they hold a person they could release and choose not to: a prisoner kept past necessity, a confession compelled, an ally "protected" from a decision that was theirs to make, an authority accepted because they would wield it more wisely than the last holder.

They rarely notice. Their allies usually do first.
