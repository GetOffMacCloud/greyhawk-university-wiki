*How to find your way around, and how to add to it.*

## Finding things
- Use the sidebar, search, or the tag links at the top of each page.
- Every page has one type tag: `student`, `npc`, `location`, `faction`, `session`, `herald`, `rumor`, `map`, `lore` or `guide`.

## Adding a page
1. Copy the matching template from `templates/` into the right folder.
2. Fill in the frontmatter and the infobox.
3. Images go in `attachments/` (portraits, maps, handouts).
4. Add `draft: true` to the frontmatter to keep a page off the site until it's ready.

| You're adding | Folder | Template |
|---|---|---|
| A news story | `herald/` | Event |
| A session write-up | `expeditions/session-reports/` | Session Report |
| A rumour or lead | `expeditions/rumor-board.md` | Rumor |
| A player character | `students/` | Student |
| A professor or townsperson | `people/faculty/` or `people/townsfolk/` | NPC |
| A place | `gazetteer/<region>/` | Location |
| A college, house or guild | `factions/` | Faction |
