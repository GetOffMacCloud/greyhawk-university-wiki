# The Antlered Circlet

> [!info] Quick Reference
> **Type:** Wondrous item (circlet) | **Rarity:** Rare | **Attunement:** Yes
> **Origin:** F6 - The Antler Throne — kept by the glade
> **Status:** 🟢 At the throne; given by the Hart, or stolen at peril

A circlet of silver-grey antler, shed and re-grown into a seamless band, light as bone and warm as a living thing. It has rested at the foot of the Antler Throne for longer than anyone can say, the gift of some forgotten supplicant, kept by the glade against the day a worthy one comes to receive it. How it comes to you decides everything it will be.

---

## Properties — If Given by the Hart

A circlet *gifted* by the Hart of Thorns to one the glade has judged worthy (see F6 - The Antler Throne):

- **Wild Step.** Difficult terrain of the natural world (bramble, bog, scree, deep snow) costs you no extra movement, and you leave no trail unless you choose to.
- **Speech of the Green.** You can *speak with animals* at will, and have advantage on Charisma checks with fey and with the druids of J8 - Ehlonna's Mount, who recognize the glade's favour.
- **The Hart's Watch (1/long rest).** As a reaction when you would be hit by an attack in a natural setting, antlers of shadow turn the blow: gain +4 AC against it. If it still hits, you may *fey step* up to 30 ft. to safety in a scatter of white blossom.
- **Obad-Hai's Regard.** While worn with honour, the wild itself is slow to harm you — natural beasts will not attack unless attacked or starving.

---

## The Curse — If Taken

> [!danger] Stolen From the Glade
> A circlet *taken* — grabbed from the throne, seized by force, claimed without the Hart's gift — fuses to the wearer's skull the moment it is donned and cannot be removed. Antler begins, slowly, to grow *into* them: each long rest the wearer makes a DC 13 Constitution save or gains a level of exhaustion that does not heal normally, as the wild reclaims a thief's body for the glade. The curse ends only with *remove curse* **and** a sincere act of restitution at the Antler Throne — returning, unarmed, to beg the Hart's pardon. The Hart remembers thieves. It is not quick to forgive, but it is not incapable of it.
