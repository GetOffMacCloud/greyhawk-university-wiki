# The Mire-Glass Lens

> [!info] Quick Reference
> **Type:** Wondrous item (lens / arcane focus) | **Rarity:** Rare (perilous) | **Attunement:** Yes (spellcaster)
> **Origin:** H4 - The Glassed Hollow — sealed beneath the fused glass
> **Status:** 🔵 Sealed; freeing it has a cost

A faceted lens of dark, bottle-green glass the size of two fists, throwing back light in colours the eye cannot quite hold. It is the focus of a *failed binding* — someone, a century ago, tried to fashion a new anchor-node by fusing an influence-fragment into glass rather than binding it properly to stone. It half-worked. The Lens holds a sliver of bound cold, and that makes it a potent arcane focus and a slow, sealed danger both at once.
