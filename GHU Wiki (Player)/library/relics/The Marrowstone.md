# The Marrowstone

> [!info] Quick Reference
> **Type:** Wondrous item (anchor-stone core) | **Rarity:** Artifact (arc-critical) | **Attunement:** No
> **Origin:** I6 - The Marrowstone Reach (Node 5)
> **Status:** 🟡 In situ, guarded by the Hollow Vigil

A core of pale, dense stone the size of two fists, warm to the touch in any weather, cut from the broken socket-end of the great fallen anchor-pillar at the Marrowstone Reach. It hums, just at the edge of hearing, the same low note the whole binding network sings. It is the only loose piece of *sound* anchor-material on the frontier — and it may be the single most important object the party can carry out of the wild.
