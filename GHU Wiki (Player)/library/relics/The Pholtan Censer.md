# The Pholtan Censer

> [!info] Quick Reference
> **Type:** Wondrous item (censer) | **Rarity:** Uncommon | **Attunement:** Yes (non-evil)
> **Origin:** B2 - The Mire-Sunk Chapel — the drowned altar
> **Status:** 🔵 Lost beneath the chapel waters

A hanging censer of tarnished brass, pierced in the sunburst pattern of **Pholtus**, the Blinding Light. It was cast to carry a coal blessed at a mother-temple up and down a pilgrim road that the marsh has since swallowed. The coal within has not gone cold in three hundred years. It will not.

---

## Properties

While attuned and carried lit:

- **Undying Coal.** The censer holds a coal of blessed flame that never dies and needs no fuel. It sheds *bright light* in a 20-ft. radius and *dim light* for 20 ft. beyond. This light cannot be extinguished by wind, water, or the dampening of cold-seep sites — it is the one flame that burns true in the Mire-Sunk Chapel's gloom.
- **Against the Cold.** You and allies within the bright light have resistance to the cold-exhaustion effects of regional seep-sites, and advantage on saves against the *Cold Aura* and chill effects of marsh undead such as the Mire-Wight.
- **Lay to Rest (1/day).** As an action, swing the censer and speak Pholtus's litany over an undead within 30 ft. It makes a DC 14 Wisdom save or is *turned* for 1 minute. A Mire-Wight reduced to 0 HP while turned by the censer **does not reform** — it is laid to rest for good.

---

## The Right Use

> [!tip] Closing the Chapel
> The censer is the key to ending the Mire-Sunk Chapel haunting cleanly. Recovered from the drowned altar and returned to *any* consecrated altar of Pholtus (the nearest is days away, toward Perrenland), it lays the drowned priest to rest, severs that site's minor seep, and earns the lasting goodwill of the Pholtan faith — a rare commodity on a frontier this far from the Blinding Light's reach.
