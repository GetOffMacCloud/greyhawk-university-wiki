# The Sevenfold Key

> [!info] Quick Reference
> **Type:** Wondrous item (tuning-rod) | **Rarity:** Artifact (arc-critical) | **Attunement:** No
> **Origin:** C1 - The Sevenfold Hush (Node 6)
> **Status:** ⚪ Resting at the vault's silent heart

A slender rod of dark marrowstone, banded at seven points with rings of cold iron, each ring inscribed in Ur-Flan with one of the network's seven tuning-notes. It is the master key of the damping node beneath the Sevenfold Hush — the instrument by which the Flan tuned how much resonance the whole network absorbed and bled. In the right hands and the right sequence, it re-balances a restored binding network. In the wrong hands, or the right hands at the wrong time, it is how the frontier ends.
