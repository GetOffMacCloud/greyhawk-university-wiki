# The World at a Glance

The Flanaess is large. Most of it will not affect your first session. The following is the minimum political and geographic literacy required to understand why Ritterstadt exists, who has opinions about it, and what is going on beyond the palisade.

For full detail on the Northwestern Flanaess, the Sepia Uplands, and campaign-specific context, read the **Player Gazetteer**. This page is the overview that makes the Gazetteer's details land correctly.

---

## Where You Are

The continent of **Oerik** occupies most of the known world. Its eastern half — the **Flanaess** — is where all of this takes place. The Flanaess is roughly the size of Europe, home to dozens of nations, and currently experiencing what scholars call "an era of precarious stability," which means several large things are slowly collapsing and have not yet fallen on each other.

**Ritterstadt** sits in the **Sepia Uplands**, a lawless frontier territory at the northwestern edge of the Flanaess — as far north and west as permanent human settlement meaningfully exists. It is beyond the reach of any national government. It is several days from the nearest market town. It is directly adjacent to one of the most dangerous wilderness regions on the continent.

This was not an accident. The founders chose this location deliberately, for reasons that are documented in the Academic Registry and not fully public.

## The Powers That Shape Your World

### The City of Greyhawk — *The Axis of the World*
**Distance from Ritterstadt:** Far. Very far. Weeks of travel south and east.
**Relevance:** The University's sponsoring institution is based here. The city is the effective center of Flanaess commerce, scholarship, and political intrigue. When faculty talk about "the University" as a large institution with resources and prestige, they mean the relationship between this field station and the main institution in the City of Greyhawk.

You will probably not go there. Its influence reaches you through mission briefs, funding decisions, and the occasional visiting scholar who arrives with interesting questions and too little field experience.

---

### The Concatenated Cantons of Perrenland — *The Distant Authority*
**Distance from Ritterstadt:** Four days south to the nearest canton town of Traft.
**Relevance:** Perrenland is the nominally governing nation over the Sepia Uplands, in the sense that if you drew lines on a map, the Sepia falls within their claimed territory. Their tax collectors reach Traft and no further. Their garrison reaches Traft and no further. Their interest in Ritterstadt is purely retrospective — they will express concern about any disaster here after the fact, once it is politically convenient to do so.

The Cantons are a loose confederation of semi-autonomous city-states united by elected leadership and a strong tradition of not letting any one authority get too powerful. This works well for them in their heartlands. It means the frontier has no effective government at all.

**Practical implication:** You cannot appeal to Perrenland for help. You cannot invoke Perrenlander law. You cannot call for a garrison. What you have is Ritterstadt's eleven-soldier guardhouse, and whatever you bring to the problem yourself.

---

### Iuz the Evil — *The Threat in the Northeast*
**Distance from Ritterstadt:** The northeastern Sepia Uplands edge the territory of Iuz's domain. His agents have been documented in the eastern uplands.
**Relevance:** Iuz is a cambion demigod — the offspring of a demon lord and a mortal — who carved a kingdom of atrocity in the northern Flanaess before being imprisoned beneath Castle Greyhawk in 505 CY by the archmage Zagig. "Imprisoned" should be understood as a temporary administrative condition. His cults continued operating. His generals held his territories. In 576 CY, his return is considered a matter of when, not if.

His agents are not here in force. They are here quietly: mapping, watching, looking for anything with the theological signature of a sealed demonic relic. The **Fiend's Embrace**, secured in Ritterstadt since its founding, is exactly the kind of object that would interest them.

**Practical implication:** Confirmed sightings of Iuz's agents should be reported to University leadership immediately. Do not freelance a response.

---

### The Great Kingdom — *The Rotting East*
**Distance from Ritterstadt:** Extremely far east. Irrelevant to daily life.
**Relevance:** The Great Kingdom was once the most powerful human empire in the Flanaess. It is currently governed by a hereditary ruler of comprehensive moral depravity, staffed by a nobility that has normalized cruelty as a form of refinement, and approaching the terminal condition of a civilization that has forgotten what it was for. It will probably collapse within a generation. When it does, the political shock will be felt across the Flanaess.

You are far enough from the Great Kingdom that its internal politics do not touch you directly. What it does is serve as a reminder of the alternative: what happens to civilizations that lose their purpose and their conscience simultaneously. Ritterstadt's founders understood this, which is part of why they went north.

---

### The Wolf Nomads — *The Watchers on the Moor*
**Distance from Ritterstadt:** Present. Their outriders have been observed watching the hamlet from the high moor.
**Relevance:** Flan-descended nomadic herders who follow seasonal migration routes through the eastern Sepia Uplands. They regard the standing stones as sacred and the uplands as ancestral territory. They have not attacked Ritterstadt. This is a deliberate choice and should not be mistaken for disinterest or weakness.

For a longer treatment, see the Player Gazetteer.

---

### The Clatspur Dwarven Clans — *The Autonomous West*
**Distance from Ritterstadt:** Two to three days west. Clan Irongrip holds Bitter Pass; Clan Brock holds the western approaches.
**Relevance:** The dwarven clanholds of the Clatspur Mountains predate human settlement in the region by centuries. They trade in metal and information. They maintain their autonomy from Perrenland through a combination of geographic advantage, military readiness, and a demonstrated willingness to make difficult situations more difficult for anyone who pushes them. They are not hostile to Ritterstadt. They are not friends of Ritterstadt. They are watching, as they always watch, to see whether this settlement is worth the administrative complication of a formal relationship.

## The Cosmic Stakes

The Flanaess in 576 CY is not simply a collection of political disputes. There are forces in play that predate the current nations and will outlast them.

**Tharizdun**, the Chained God — imprisoned at the dawn of the world by a coalition of divine powers who recognized what eternal entropy looked like up close — cannot act directly on the Oerth. He influences. He reaches. His followers, the Doomdreamers, believe his liberation is possible through the patient accumulation of suffering and cold. Whether the Quag Mires are connected to his imprisonment, his power, or simply resonate with his nature by coincidence, the druids at Ehlonna's Mount have opinions they share carefully and only with people they trust.

This is background context, not an immediate problem. It is, however, the reason "the northern frontier" means something more than "the edge of civilization" in 576 CY.

## The Short Version

You are at the northwestern edge of the known world. The nearest government doesn't govern here. An evil demigod's agents move through the territory. The druids are worried about the land itself. The dwarves are watching. The Wolf Nomads are watching. The standing stones predate everyone currently alive.

Ritterstadt exists because four people decided to stay somewhere difficult, and everything that has been built since is built on that decision.
