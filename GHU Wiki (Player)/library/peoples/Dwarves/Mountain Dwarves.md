# **The History of the Hollar**

The mountain dwarves are a reclusive and exceptionally hardy people who dwell in the highest peaks and deepest subterranean vaults of the Flanaess. Their history is defined by the **Twin Cataclysms**; after the Invoked Devastation cast down the great halls of the Hellfurnaces, the Hollar migrated east.

Guided by the goddess Berronar, they established the legendary hall of **Gilmorack** in the Lortmils around -201 CY. They have spent generations in a state of perpetual, bloody war against the "One-Eyed" hordes of Gruumsh. Today, they are the "Stone Wall" of the Flanaess, holding the high passes against Iuz and the giants of the Crystalmists.

### **Playing a Mountain Dwarf**

##### **The Hollar Mindset**

- **A Legacy of Grudges:** You do not forgive. Whether it is an orc tribe that raided a mine 200 years ago or a human merchant who overcharged you last week, it is recorded in your memory.
    
- **Ancestral Honor:** You guard your "True Name" closely. Casual use of your name by a human is a minor insult; you expect to be addressed by your title or clan until a bond is forged in blood.
    
- **Heavy Infantry:** You favor heavy chain mail, shields, and the weight of a battle axe. You don't "skirmish"—you hold the line until the mountain falls.

##### **Mountain Dwarf (Hollar) Traits**

- **Ability Score Increase.** Your **Constitution** score increases by 2, and your **Strength** score increases by 1. Your **Charisma** decreases by 1, reflecting the notoriously blunt and antisocial temperament of the deep-mountain clans.

- **Age.** Dwarves mature at the same rate as humans but are considered young until they reach 50. They live about 350 years.
    
- **Size.** You are Medium, standing about 4 feet tall and weighing nearly 150 lbs.
    
- **Speed.** Your base walking speed is **25 feet**. Your speed is not reduced by wearing heavy armor.
    
- **Darkvision.** Accustomed to life in the deep halls and high passes, you have superior vision in dim and dark conditions. You can see in dim light within 60 feet as if it were bright light, and in darkness as if it were dim light. You can't discern color in darkness, only shades of gray.
    
- **Dwarven Resilience.** You have **Advantage** on saving throws against poison and resistance against poison damage.
    
- **Stonecunning.** You have Advantage on Intelligence (History) and Wisdom (Perception) checks related to the origin, age, or structural integrity of stonework.
    
- **Giant Dodger.** Generations of warfare against the enemies of the deep peaks have honed an instinctive survival reflex. Giants, Ogres, and Trolls have **Disadvantage** on melee and ranged weapon attack rolls against you.

- **Languages.** You can speak, read, and write **Common** and **Dwarvish**.

---

**Hollar Heritage.** Choose one of the following lineages based on your clan's tradition:

- **Hollar War-Proven:** Your clan forged its name in the bloody underground wars against the goblinoid hordes. You gain proficiency with the **Battleaxe, Handaxe, Light Hammer, and War Hammer**. You also gain a **+1 bonus to attack rolls** against orcs, goblins, and hobgoblins, the ancient enemies of your kin.

- **Glorvardum Stoneholder:** Trained in the shield-wall traditions of the Glorioles, you are the living definition of an immovable object. You gain proficiency with **Heavy Armor**. Additionally, you have **Advantage** on Strength saving throws made to resist being **knocked prone** or **forcibly moved** against your will.

- **Dumadan Seer-Kin:** You hail from the reclusive, mystical Clan of Dumadan, where insight and spiritual awareness are prized above brute force. Your **Wisdom** score increases by 1. You gain proficiency in the **Insight** skill and learn one extra **language** of your choice.
