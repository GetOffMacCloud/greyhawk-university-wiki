# The History and Origins of the Oeridians
Some sages believe that the Oeridians originally hailed from a western land known as Orcreich, located far beyond the borders of the ancient Baklunish Empire. According to legends, they were driven from this homeland by a massive invasion of orcs and goblins. What history does record with certainty is that the Oeridians first appeared in the Baklunish Basin, where they served as mercenaries before the Twin Cataclysms devastated the region.

Following the Rain of Colorless Fire and the Invoked Devastation, the Oeridians were pushed eastward by the surviving Baklunish and Suloise (who heavily utilized orc mercenaries). Guided by prophecies of greatness, the fierce Oeridian tribes were the first to enter the Flanaess, fighting both the native Flan and the migrating Suel. Unlike the Suel, who were largely despised for their cruelty, the Oeridians successfully allied with scattered demi-human enclaves (dwarves, gnomes, and halflings) and native Flan tribes to defeat the Suel and humanoid hordes.

The Oeridians quickly established themselves as the preeminent conquerors of the Flanaess. The Aerdi, the most powerful of the Oeridian tribes, marched all the way to the Solnor Ocean and founded the Kingdom of Aerdy, which eventually expanded into the continent-spanning Great Kingdom. Meanwhile, in the west, Oeridian tribes allied with the Suel Houses of Rhola and Neheli to found the peaceful and prosperous Kingdom of Keoland. Today, Oeridian culture, language (Old Oeridian/Common), and military tradition serve as the foundation for the civilized Flanaess.

>> [!note|wide-3]- Culture and Personality
>> As a people, Oeridians are aggressive, determined, pragmatic, and straightforward. They are conquerors by nature who are accustomed to thinking of themselves as the best humanity has to offer, destined to rule. They have a profound, culturally ingrained respect for authority, hierarchy, and martial prowess. Oeridians are prone to taking charge in difficult situations, obeying their superiors, and sacrificing themselves for the good of their cause.
>> 
>> They are notoriously short-tempered and prone to direct, sometimes violent action when faced with a problem. (In the Flanaess, a drastic, confrontational solution to a minor problem—such as burning down a building because the door is locked—is colloquially called an **"Oeridian key"**). However, those who learn to master this temper and channel their boundless energy into productive crafts are highly regarded.
>> 
>> Oeridians love songs, celebrations, and physical games. They view combat as a sacred craft and practice acrobatics, tumbling, unarmed fighting, and even dance to improve their battlefield nimbleness. Conversely, they are highly practical and generally not inclined toward intellectual pursuits; they are the human race least likely to pursue arcane magic, though the few who do typically specialize in combat or protective magics.
>> 
>> A strict Oeridian custom dictates that **a gift must be offered to a host or hostess when staying overnight**; traditionally, this gesture assures the host that the heavily armed Oeridian guest has no designs on conquering their land or property.

### **Playing an Oeridian Character**
##### The Oeridian Mindset and Roleplaying Hooks
Playing an Oeridian character means embodying a relentless and pragmatic conqueror who values martial discipline and ancestral hierarchy above all, even as they struggle to master a notoriously explosive temper.

* **The Oeridian Key:** You are prone to direct, sometimes violent solutions to minor problems. When faced with a locked door, your first instinct may be to burn down the building rather than find a locksmith. Roleplay this "short-fused" pragmatism when negotiations stall.
* **The Sacred Gift:** You adhere strictly to the custom of offering a gift to a host when staying overnight. This is not just politeness; it is a vital diplomatic signal to your host that you—a heavily armed and capable warrior—have no designs on conquering their property.
* **Hierarchy and Discipline:** You have a profound respect for authority. You are likely the first to look for a leader in a crisis, or the first to step up and take charge if one is lacking. You value military rank and social station over abstract ideals.
* **Martial Nimbleness:** You view combat as a sacred craft. You might describe your movements in battle as a form of dance, incorporating acrobatics, tumbling, and unarmed strikes to improve your battlefield nimbleness.
* **Pragmatic Magic:** If you are a spellcaster, you likely view magic as a tool for the battlefield. You favor protective abjurations or combat-focused evocations over "frivolous" or purely intellectual arcane pursuits.

##### **Fighting Styles:** 
Oeridian armies are the most feared in the Flanaess. 
* The heavy cavalry and mounted archers of Furyondy and Nyrond are legendary, favoring the lance and longsword. 
* The ancient Aerdi excelled with shield, spear, and a short slashing sword. 
* In the Sheldomar Valley (Keoland), bold Oeridian swordsmen often forgo heavy armor entirely to maximize their speed and flexibility. 
* In Veluna, Oeridians prefer bludgeoning weapons and uniquely utilize military units armed with non-lethal _man-catchers_ to quell uprisings peacefully.
# Oeridian Racial Traits

> [!abstract] Overview
> The **Oeridians** are the preeminent conquerors and law-bringers of the Flanaess. Driven by prophecies of greatness and a fierce, pragmatic nature, they were the first to tame the eastern lands following the Twin Cataclysms. Their military traditions, flashy attire, and respect for hierarchy form the foundation of the civilized Flanaess.

## Physical Description
Oeridians are considered the prototypical human in build—flexible, sturdy, and well-proportioned:
* **Skin:** Ranges from tan to a distinct olive hue.
* **Hair:** Honey-blonde to black; brown and auburn are most common. It can be straight, wavy, or curly.
* **Eyes:** Spans the full spectrum, though brown and gray are typical; auburn eyes are uniquely common.
* **Build:** Robust and well-proportioned. Males average 5’8” to 5’10” (160-190 lbs); females average 5’5” to 5’7” (110-130 lbs).
* **Dress:** The flashiest dressers in the Flanaess, favoring bold plaids, checks, and diamond or lozenge patterns. They typically wear close-fitting trousers and short tunics with a cape or cloak.

---

## Oeridian Human Traits
As an Oeridian human, you have the following racial traits.

* **Ability Score Increase.** Your **Constitution** score increases by 2, and your **Strength** score increases by 1. However, your **Charisma** decreases by 1, reflecting the blunt, short-tempered nature of your people.
* **Age.** Humans reach adulthood in their late teens and live less than a century.
* **Size.** Your size is **Medium**.
* **Speed.** Your base walking speed is **30 feet**.
* **Inner Warrior.** You gain proficiency in one of the following: **Acrobatics**, **Athletics**, or **Performance (Dance)**.
* **The Oeridian Key.** Your aggressive and direct nature allows you to act with sudden, violent precision. You gain a **+2 bonus to all Initiative rolls**.
* **Oeridian Heritage.** Choose one of the following lineages based on your character's background:
	* **Aerdi Lineage (East):** You gain proficiency in the **Deception** skill and one extra language of your choice.
	* **Nyrondal/Furyondian Lineage (Central):** You have proficiency in **Animal Handling (Horses)** and gain proficiency with the **Longsword** and **Longbow**.
	* **Vollar Lineage (Veluna):** You have proficiency in the **Persuasion** skill and can choose to deal non-lethal damage with any melee weapon attack without penalty.
* **Languages.** You can speak, read, and write **Common** and **Old Oeridian**.
