# The History and Origins of the Suloise
The Suloise (or Suel) are an ancient, magically gifted, and haughty human race whose history is defined by incredible arcane achievements and catastrophic devastation. Over a thousand years ago, the Suel Imperium was the mightiest empire of its time, located in the western lands south of the Crystalmist Mountains and west of the Hellfurnaces. The Suloise people learned the arts of sorcery and civilization from the elves, but their ambition drove them to surpass their teachers, delving into fell magics and breeding altered slave races like the derro.

The Suel Imperium eventually engaged in a sixty-three-year war with the Baklunish Empire for supremacy over western Oerik. This conflict culminated in the Twin Cataclysms. The Suel unleashed the Invoked Devastation, wiping out the Baklunish civilization, but Baklunish mage-priests retaliated with the Rain of Colorless Fire, a magical inferno that burned the Suel Imperium into the ash-filled wasteland now known as the Sea of Dust.

![[Suloise Rain of Colorless Fire.png|900]]

Fleeing this destruction, the surviving Suel initiated the Great Migrations into the Flanaess. The great seer Slerotin, the Last Mage of Power, led twelve noble houses through a magical tunnel he carved through the Hellfurnaces. Upon entering the Flanaess, the Suel battled the native Flan and the migrating Oeridians. History notes that the majority of these Suel refugees were exceptionally cruel—they lied, cheated, stole, enslaved, and frequently employed evil humanoids like orcs as mercenaries. However, there were notable exceptions, such as the honorable Houses of Rhola and Neheli, who allied with Oeridian tribes and native Flan to found the peaceful and prosperous Kingdom of Keoland in the Sheldomar Valley.

>> The Suel are the lightest in complexion of all human ethnic groups in the Flanaess, possessing fair skin and a remarkably high number of albinos. **Males average between 6’0” to 6’3” tall and weigh between 160-225 lbs, while females average 5’10” to 6’0” and weigh 110-165 lbs**. They tend to have lean, narrow facial features. Their eyes are typically pale blue or violet, with deep blue and gray also occurring. Suel hair is naturally wiry, curly, or kinky, and is almost always blond, light red, or platinum.
>> 
>> Ancient Suel fashion consisted of wide-legged pantaloons and loose-fitting blouses, a style still popular today but adapted for the climate. In the frozen north, garments are made of fur or felted wool, worn with capes and mittens, while in the south, lightweight fabrics and loose vests are preferred. **The Suel heavily favor solid colors, often maintaining only one or two colors in their entire wardrobe**. They also wear special family emblems and souvenir trinkets passed down through generations as proud markers of their heritage.

>> [!note|wide-3]- Culture and Personality
>> The Suloise are widely known as a **proud, commanding, determined, opinionated, and overbearing people**. They hold a deep-seated belief in the superiority of their culture, art, and magic over all other human races. Suloise place massive importance on their ancestral lineages and family ties, though their definition of "family" may be narrow and restricted to immediate relatives. They are exceptionally short-tempered and impatient; when resolving an issue, they are just as likely to use direct force—magical or physical—as they are to use negotiation.
>> 
>> The race is highly gifted in manual dexterity, excelling in detailed arts and crafts such as embroidery, sewing, leather tooling, sculpting, and painting. Suloise weavers demand the highest prices in the Flanaess for their intricately patterned fabrics. Above all, **the Suel have a profound respect for and tradition of arcane magic**, producing many of the most powerful wizards in the Flanaess.

>> [!warning|wide-2]- Suel Subgroups and Factions
>> Because the Suel scattered across the Flanaess, they developed into several distinct subcultures that a player can choose from:
>> - **The Thillonrian Barbarians (Snow, Ice, and Frost Barbarians):** Living in the far northeast, these people are considered the purest-blooded Suel remaining. They have adopted a barbaric lifestyle of raiding, exploring, and seafaring, and their warriors frequently become berserkers. They speak the Cold Tongue (Fruz), a dialect of Suloise mixed with Flan.
>> - **The Scarlet Brotherhood (Tilvanot Peninsula):** A deeply evil, secretive, and totalitarian society of Suel supremacists. They keep strict records of pureblood ancestry and seek to dominate the Flanaess using espionage, assassination, and martial arts. Their elite enforcers are deadly monks, and they forbid wizardry among their lower ranks to prevent a repeat of the Invoked Devastation. They are the only people who still actively speak the ancient Suloise language.
>> - **The Central Flanaess Suel (Keoland, Duchy of Urnst, etc.):** Descendants of the honorable Houses Rhola and Neheli. They have civilized their famous temper, often mingling with Oeridians and Flan, and are more cooperative. Wizards from this region are usually formally schooled.
>> - **Island Realms Suel (Lordship of the Isles):** Master traders, seafarers, and negotiators. Recently, many of these Suel have been expelled from the Spindrift Isles by elves and have either joined the Scarlet Brotherhood or turned to adventuring.
>> - **Amedio Jungle and Hepmonaland Suel:** After a thousand years in the deep jungles, these Suel have become tanned, freckled, and adapted to the wild. Some of these isolated tribes have devolved into barbaric, cannibalistic practices, utilizing martial arts mimicking simians.

### **Playing a Suloise Character** 
##### **The Suloise Mindset and Roleplaying Hooks**
Playing a Suel character means balancing a legacy of incredible ancient power with the realities of a scattered, ruined people.

- **Pride and Temper:** The Suel are infamous for their short tempers, impatience, and overbearing pride. When resolving a dispute, a Suel character is just as likely to resort to direct force (whether political, magical, or physical) as they are to use negotiation. They are proud to a fault and will rarely allow others to know if they are suffering from personal or financial hardships.
- **The Weight of Ancestry:** Family obligations are paramount, and a Suel adventurer will fiercely protect what they consider their "family," even if that definition is exceptionally narrow. Many Suel can trace their ancestry all the way back to the Suel Imperium over a thousand years ago, and they frequently wear inherited emblems or souvenir trinkets on their clothing to display this heritage.
- **Good vs. Evil Suel (Noblesse Oblige vs. Supremacy):** Your player's alignment will heavily dictate how their Suel pride manifests. Evil or neutral Suel (like those of the Scarlet Brotherhood) view themselves as the destined masters of the world, believing their superiority gives them the moral license to lie, cheat, steal, and enslave "lesser" races. Good-aligned Suel (such as descendants of the Houses of Rhola and Neheli) also believe in the absolute superiority of Suel culture, but they interpret this as a solemn responsibility. These honorable characters operate under a strict code of _noblesse oblige_, holding themselves to impossibly high moral standards and believing it is their self-sacrificing duty to protect and civilize the other races of the Flanaess.
- **Magic as a Birthright:** The ancient Suel Imperium was arguably the greatest magical society in human history. Nearly all Suel hold a deep respect for magic, and players are highly encouraged to take the Spellcraft proficiency or play a wizard, as their manual dexterity gives them an innate advantage with somatic spell components.

##### Fighting Styles
Suel martial traditions are defined by a singular philosophy: **Speed and Precision over Raw Force.** The Suel historically relied on their superior manual dexterity to strike first and end a conflict before a counter-attack could be mounted.

* **Central Suel (The Great Migration Legacy):** These traditional armies historically favored weapon-and-shield techniques that prioritized speed. They often forgo heavy plate in favor of chain or reinforced leather to maintain maximum mobility.
* **The Silent Sash (Scarlet Brotherhood):** This terrifying unarmed martial art focuses on pressure points and nerve strikes. It is designed to disable an opponent silently, often utilizing sashes or hidden garrotes to finish a foe without a sound.
* **Thillonrian Barbarians (The Sea-Wolves):** The Suel of the frozen north (Fruzti, Schnai, and Cruski) prefer massive two-handed axes and swords. They are the premier naval combatants of the Flanaess, specializing in high-speed ramming and aggressive boarding actions.
* **Amedian Simian-Style:** The Suel of the Amedian Jungles have adapted to their environment with a primal fighting style. They utilize low-center-of-gravity throws, grasping locks, and high-impact kicking techniques modeled after the powerful great apes of the southern wilds.

### Suloise Combat Mechanics (Optional)
Depending on your character's heritage, you may swap your **Suel Superiority** trait for one of these specialized fighting styles:

* **Sash Adept:** Your unarmed strikes deal **1d4** bludgeoning damage. When you hit a creature with an unarmed strike, you can use a Bonus Action to attempt to **Grapple** them using your Dexterity (Acrobatics) instead of Strength (Athletics).
* **Thillonrian Boarder:** You have proficiency with the **Greataxe** and **Greatsword**. Additionally, you have **Advantage** on Strength (Athletics) checks made to climb or swim.
* **Amedian Grasp:** You have **Advantage** on saving throws against being knocked **Prone**. When you successfully grapple a creature, they take bludgeoning damage equal to your Strength modifier (minimum of 1).
* **Imperial Skirmisher:** You gain a **+1 bonus to AC** while you are wearing Light armor and wielding a finesse weapon.

# Suloise Racial Traits

> [!abstract] Overview
> The **Suloise** (or Suel) are the scions of a shattered empire, defined by their strikingly pale features and a deep-seated affinity for arcane power. While scattered across the Flanaess—from the frozen north to the tropical south—they carry the weight of a thousand-year-old legacy in their very blood.

---

## Physical Description
The Suel are perhaps the most visually distinct human ethnicity in the Flanaess:
* **Skin:** Extremely pale, almost bone-white, and prone to severe sunburn.
* **Hair:** Fair to a fault—usually platinum blonde, strawberry blonde, or white.
* **Eyes:** Predominantly pale blue, violet, or light grey.
* **Build:** Generally lean and athletic.

---

## Suloise Human Traits
As a Suloise human, you have the following racial traits.

* **Ability Score Increase.** Your **Intelligence** score increases by 2, and your **Dexterity** score increases by 1. However, your **Constitution** decreases by 1.
* **Age.** Suel reach adulthood in their late teens and live less than a century. 
* **Size.** Suel vary in height but are typically lean, ranging from 5 to over 6 feet tall. Your size is **Medium**.
* **Speed.** Your base walking speed is **30 feet**.
* **Ancestral Arcanist.** You know one **cantrip** of your choice from the **Wizard** spell list. Intelligence is your spellcasting ability for it.
* **Slerotin’s Eye.** Touched by the prophetic blood of the Last Mage of Power, you gain a **+2 bonus to all Initiative rolls**.
* **Suloise Heritage.** Choose one of the following lineages based on your character's background:
	* **Northern Lineage (Thillonrian):** You have resistance to **Cold** damage.
	* **Southern Lineage (Amedio/Hepmonaland):** You have resistance to **Fire** damage.
	* **High-Noble Lineage (Keoland/Central):** You gain proficiency in the **Arcana** skill and one extra language of your choice.
* **Languages.** You can speak, read, and write **Common** and **Ancient Suloise**.
