# **The History and Origins of the Baklunish** 
The Baklunish people trace their lineage back to a legendary, advanced civilization whose history is marked by incredible elemental magic, grand nomadic traditions, and an apocalyptic war. Originally a nomadic herder people from the plains of western Oerik, the ancient Bakluni were subjugated by the mighty Suel Imperium for centuries. Eventually, under leaders like Ali-ben-Onar, the Bakluni united to throw off the Suloise yoke, utilizing captured magical artifacts known as the Binders to force the Suel to cede vast northern territories.

![[Baklunish Cavalry.png|500]]

This uneasy peace ultimately collapsed into a titanic sixty-three-year conflict known as the Baklunish-Suloise Wars. This war culminated in the Twin Cataclysms. The Suel unleashed the *Invoked Devastation*, an apocalypse that annihilated the Baklunish civilization and turned their lands into the Dry Steppes. In immediate retaliation, a cadre of Baklunish mage-priests gathered at their holiest site—a stone circle known as Tovag Baragu (the "Naval of the Earth")—and brought down the Rain of Colorless Fire, completely reducing the Suel Imperium to the ash-filled Sea of Dust.

![[Baklunish Invoked Devastation.png|500]]

Fleeing the destruction of their homeland, the surviving Baklunish migrated north and west to the shores of the Dramidj Ocean. Here, they founded the prosperous successor states of the Baklunish West, such as Zeif, Ekbir, Tusmit, and Ket, while others maintained their ancient nomadic lifestyle on the plains.

>> The Bakluni are a deeply contemplative, practical, and highly superstitious people. They carefully think through matters before acting, as courting failure is seen as a stain on one's honor. >> - **Honor** demands keeping one's word and acting according to one's station.
>> - **Family** extends beyond blood relatives to include employees, mercenary companies, or a merchant's caravan, with elders and parents treated with utmost reverence.
>> - **Generosity** dictates that hoarding wealth is a path to ruin; a good Baklunish shares their time, wisdom, and alms with the less fortunate.
>> - **Piety** requires paying homage to a wide variety of gods to ensure no divine force is slighted, keeping the fickle hand of fate at bay.

>> [!warning|wide-2]- Baklunish Subgroups and Factions
>> As the Baklunish rebuilt their civilization, they split into distinct cultural paths:
>> - **The Settled Baklunish (Zeif, Ekbir, Tusmit, Ket):** Having achieved considerable wealth and power, these Baklunish have built great cities and roads, creating an urban-centered society based heavily on trading, agriculture, and exploring beyond their borders.
>> - **The Nomadic Baklunish (Tiger Nomads, Wolf Nomads, Plains of the Paynims, Ull):** These tribes maintain the ancient, horse-based nomadic culture of raiding, herding, and trading. They are highly mobile, fiercely independent, and are frequently hired as mercenary cavalry throughout the western Flanaess.
>> - **The Monastic Orders (D'ai Shaton, Order of the Iron Fist, etc.):** The Baklunish boast the longest history of martial monastic orders in the Flanaess. Devoted to the deities Xan Yae and Zuoken, these monks practice a complicated form of unarmed combat known as _Da’Shon_ (Falling Hail) and seek physical and mental perfection.
>
>> [!summary|min-0]- The Baklunish Pantheon
>>The Baklunish revere a tight-knit pantheon of deities, and an honorable Baklunish prays to all of them to ensure good fortune. The pantheon includes: 
>>* **Istus** (the most powerful goddess of fate and destiny) 
>>* **Al’Akbar** (guardianship and faithfulness) 
>>* **Xan Yae** (twilight, shadows, and mental mastery) 
>>* **Zuoken** (monks and physical perfection) 
>>* **Geshtai** (lakes, rivers, and wells) 
>>* **Mouqol** (trade, negotiation, and appraisal) 
>>
The Baklunish will also offer prayers to dark entities like **Incabulos** (known to them as Sevelkhar the Waster) simply to appease him and keep his plagues at bay.

### **Playing a Baklunish Character**
> [!abstract] Overview
> The **Baklunish** are a people of deep tradition, golden-hued skin, and ancient honor. Survivors of the Twin Cataclysms that claimed their original empire, they now thrive as both nomadic masters of the desert and refined city-states. Their culture is built upon the "Four Feet of the Dragon": Honor, Family, Generosity, and Piety.
##### **The Baklunish Mindset and Roleplaying Hooks:**

- **The Weight of Fate:** Baklunish characters often believe deeply in destiny, trusting in Istus while remaining pragmatic ("Trust in Istus, but tie up your camel").
- **Superstition and Tokens:** The threat of diabolic influences makes the Bakluni highly superstitious. Most will not leave their homes without a personal good luck token—such as a bent iron nail, a lucky copper piece, or a woven hair necklace. Being deprived of this token makes a Baklunish character highly nervous and jumpy.
- **The Hakimas:** Baklunish society highly respects Hakimas—wise women or midwives gifted with "The Sight." These women can magically discern truth from lies and are the only citizens permitted to speak out with impunity against the edicts of Caliphs or Mullahs.

##### Physical Description
The Baklunish possess a distinct, warm appearance reflecting their heritage in the golden lands of the west: 
* **Skin:** Golden-hued to deep amber; they age with a dignified, weathered grace. 
* **Hair:** Invariably straight and fine, ranging from dark brown to a dusky bluish-black. 
* **Eyes:** Predominantly green or gray-green. Jade-green eyes are prized as a divine blessing, while rare golden eyes hint at djinni bloodlines. 
* **Build:** Typically long of limb and face with high cheekbones. Males average 5’0” to 6’6” (140-225 lbs).

##### Baklunish Human Traits
As a Baklunish human, you have the following racial traits. 

* **Ability Score Increase.** Your **Wisdom** score increases by 2, and your **Dexterity** score increases by 1. However, your **Strength** decreases by 1.
* **Age.** Baklunish reach adulthood in their late teens and live less than a century. 
* **Size.** Baklunish are typically tall and slender. Your size is **Medium**. 
* **Speed.** Your base walking speed is **30 feet**. 
* **Pious Culture.** You have proficiency in the **Religion** skill. 
* **The Hakima’s Sight.** You possess a fragment of the prophetic "Sight" granted by Istus to the wise women of your people. You have a supernatural knack for anticipating danger. You gain a **+2 bonus to all Initiative rolls**. 
* **Baklunish Heritage.** Choose one of the following lineages based on your character's background:
	* **Nomadic Lineage (Paynims/Wolf):** Born to the saddle and the open steppe, you possess the endurance of the Great Waste. You have proficiency in the **Animal Handling** and **Survival** skills. You have Advantage on saving throws made to avoid falling off your mount. Additionally, you can travel for up to 10 hours a day before needing to check for Forced March exhaustion.
	* **Settled Lineage (Zeif/Ekbir/Ket):** Raised in the glittering caliphates and trade hubs, you understand the flow of coin and conversation. You gain proficiency in **Insight** and **Persuasion**. You have Advantage on Intelligence (**History**) or Intelligence (**Investigation**) checks made to navigate or find information within a city or large town. You also learn one extra language of your choice.
	* **Jinn-Touched Lineage:** You know one **cantrip** of your choice from the **Druid** spell list (Elemental only: *Control Flames, Gust, Mold Earth,* or *Shape Water*). Wisdom is your spellcasting ability for it. 
* **Languages.** You can speak, read, and write **Common** and **Baklunish**.
