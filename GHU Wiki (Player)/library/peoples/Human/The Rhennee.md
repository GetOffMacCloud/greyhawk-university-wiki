# **The History and Origins of the Rhennee** 
The Rhennee are a true enigma among the human races of the Flanaess, for they firmly believe they are not native to Oerth at all. According to their own secretive folklore, the Rhennee arrived on Oerth by accident from their home world—or island continent—known as "Rhop". Some sages speculate that Rhop was actually a massive "Elder Being," perhaps a supernatural turtle-like creature measuring miles across, which either banished them or deposited them in this realm.

Whatever the truth of their extraterrestrial origins, history records that the Rhennee first appeared in the Flanaess near the Adri Forest around 150 CY. To escape harassment and persecution by the expanding Oeridian armies of the Great Kingdom, the Rhennee fled westward. Driven from the land, they took to the waterways, adopting a nomadic life on the rivers and lakes of the central Flanaess, particularly the sprawling Nyr Dyv (Lake of Unknown Depths). Today, they remain an insular, waterborne people, completely distinct from the Suel, Oeridian, Flan, and Baklunish.

>> [!example|wide-3]- Physical Appearance and Dress
>> Rhennee are typically tan or olive-skinned, bearing wide facial features and a short, wiry, but exceptionally strong build. **Males average between 5’0” to 5’6” tall and weigh 140-175 lbs, while females average 4’8” to 5’0” tall and weigh 100-140 lbs**. Their hair is almost universally curly, ranging from dark brown to black, and their eyes are most often gray, blue, or hazel, with green appearing occasionally.
>>
>> The Rhennee dress to entice and impress. Males favor tight-legged pantaloons, loose-fitting shirts, and sturdy leather jerkins. Females traditionally wear long, flowing, sleeveless dresses with low-cut tops. The barge-folk love bright, colorful garments—especially in shades of blue and green—and frequently adorn themselves with jewelry made from mollusk shells and precious freshwater stones.
>
>> [!note|wide-3]- Culture and Personality
>> The Rhennee are widely viewed by outsiders as proud, aggressive, secretive, and haughty. They refer to all non-Rhennee as **"Gajos"** (or _gadjo_) and treat them with deep suspicion. Because they have been ostracized and persecuted by the nations of the Flanaess, the Rhennee have developed a strict internal moral code: they will gladly lie to, cheat, and steal from a Gajo, but they consider it taboo to steal from or betray their own family. To the Rhennee, any Gajo property that is not physically tied down or guarded is considered "public property".
>> 
>> Rhennee society is based on the family unit, lived out on large barges (roughly 15 by 60 feet) that house up to 45 people. The society is highly chauvinistic, ruled by a male "capo" or lord who may take as many wives as he can support. However, the true backbone of Rhennee culture is the **Verda** (the "wise woman"). The Verda serves as the tribe's healer, advisor, and fortune-teller, and holds absolute veto power; no male noble will knowingly contradict her. Rhennee women are expected to remain chaste until marriage; those who do not are forbidden to marry and become an **"Aunta"** (prostitute), living on a separate small vessel and servicing the males of the fleet.
>> 
>> The Rhennee are highly superstitious regarding sickness, childbirth, and death. Women giving birth must enter 40 days of absolute seclusion with the Verda. The sick are strictly quarantined or cast out entirely to protect the tribe. The dead are weighted with stones or sand and buried at sea, as the Rhennee deeply fear necromancy and priests of death gods.
>
>> [!warning|wide-2]- Rhennee Subgroups and Factions
>> - **The Barge-Folk:** The vast majority of Rhennee, navigating the Nyr Dyv and the Selintan, Veng, and Velverdyva rivers. They earn their keep through transporting goods, fishing, and smuggling.
>> 
>>  - **The Attloi:** A distinct sect of Rhennee who never took to the water. They travel in horse-drawn caravans around the Adri Forest region. They share the customs of the barge-folk but bury their dead on land, marking the graves as sites of evil.
>>   
>>   - **The Rhel-far:** An evil, greedy offshoot of land-dwelling Rhennee residing in the Adri Forest. They often serve as spies and agents for evil warlords or the Death Knight Prince Myrhal, posing as normal Rhennee to escape notice.
>
>> [!summary|min-0]- The Rhnnee Pantheon
>> The Rhennee practice no formal religion and have no clerics, druids, or paladins. They revere the elemental forces of water and refuse to pollute any water source. The closest thing they have to divine figures are the **"Vetha"**—older women or Verdas who are endowed with the sorcerous and divinatory powers of their mythical homeland, Rhop. Recently, some land-based Attloi have adopted Flanaess deities of travel, luck, and fate, such as Fharlanghn, Olidammara, and Istus.

### **Playing a Rhennee Character**
##### The Rhennee Mindset and Roleplaying Hooks
Playing a Rhennee character means balancing a profound loyalty to your _familja_ with a deep-seated suspicion of the "Gajo" world that has historically persecuted your kind.
- **The Gajo Barrier:** You refer to all non-Rhennee as _Gajos_ (or _gadjo_). Years of being ostracized have hardened your heart; you may view Gajo property as "public property" if left unguarded, and you treat outsiders with a haughty, aggressive secrecy until they prove themselves.
- **Family Above All:** Your internal moral code is absolute: you never steal from, lie to, or betray a fellow Rhennee. Your party members are Gajos, but if they earn your trust, you may eventually view them as "honorary kin," protecting them with a fierce, wiry strength.
- **Waterborne Superstition:** You are highly superstitious regarding sickness and death. You deeply fear necromancy and the "pollution" of land-based graves. You likely carry a small token of freshwater stone or shell to keep "land-sickness" at bay while adventuring.
- **The Enigma of Rhop:** You believe your people are not from Oerth, but from a lost world or "Great Turtle" called Rhop. This gives you a sense of being a perpetual alien; you are on this world, but not _of_ it.
    
##### **Fighting Styles**
Rhennee martial traditions focus on close-quarters brutality and utilizing the environment of a cramped barge deck. They favor dual-wielding, hidden blades, and grappling.
- **Rope Fighting:** A unique defensive martial art developed on the barges. It utilizes coils of rope or sashes to trip, bind, and disarm opponents, turning a tool of the trade into a lethal weapon.
- **The Darkhagard (Elite Enforcers):** These "Warrior-Protectors" are the elite of the Rhennee fleets. They are masters of fisticuffs and the **Darkha** (a specialized curved blade), specializing in rapid strikes that end a fight before a Gajo guard can blow their whistle.
- **Attloi Caravan Guard:** Land-dwelling Rhennee have adapted their styles for the road, utilizing whips and shortbows to defend their wagons from horse-back or behind cover.

### **Rhennee Combat Mechanics (Optional)**

Depending on your character's heritage, you may swap your **Water-Wise** trait for one of these specialized fighting styles:
- **Darkha Initiate:** You gain proficiency with the **Darkha** (use scimitar stats) and **Daggers**. When you hit a creature with a melee attack using one of these weapons, you can use a Bonus Action to attempt to hide a "dirty trick," giving the target Disadvantage on its next attack roll against you.
- **Rope-Binder:** You gain proficiency with **Whips** and **Nets**. You can use a Whip to attempt a Grapple or Shove (Trip) against a creature within 10 feet, using your Dexterity (Acrobatics) instead of Strength.
- **Barge-Brawler:** Your unarmed strikes deal **1d4** bludgeoning damage. When you hit a creature with an unarmed strike on your turn, you can use a Bonus Action to attempt to **Shove** them.
- **Attloi Whistler:** You gain proficiency with **Shortbows** and **Slings**. Being within 5 feet of a hostile creature doesn’t impose disadvantage on your ranged attack rolls with these weapons.

# **Rhennee Racial Traits**

> [!abstract] Overview
> The **Rhennee** are a true enigma, a waterborne, nomadic people who arrived in the Flanaess as outcasts from another realm. Insular, proud, and fiercely protective of their secretive culture, they navigate the great rivers and the Nyr Dyv, living entirely apart from the established nations of men.

---

## Physical Description

Rhennee are short, wiry, and exceptionally strong for their size:
- **Skin:** Tan or olive-hued, often weathered by sun and spray.
- **Hair:** Universally dark and curly, ranging from dark brown to black.
- **Eyes:** Most often gray, blue, or hazel.
- **Build:** Short and sturdy; males average 5’3”, females 4’10”.

## Rhennee Human Traits
As a Rhennee human, you have the following racial traits.

- **Ability Score Increase.** Your **Dexterity** score increases by 2, and your **Strength** score increases by 1. However, your **Charisma** decreases by 1 (representing your abrasive nature toward Gajos).
- **Age.** Rhennee reach adulthood in their late teens and live slightly shorter lives than other humans, rarely passing 70.
- **Size.** Your size is **Medium**.
- **Speed.** Your base walking speed is **30 feet**.
- **Rhennee Cant.** You know the secret Rhennee Cant, a language of slang, gestures, and body language. You gain proficiency in the **Sleight of Hand** skill and with **Thieves' Tools**.
- **Water-Wise.** You have **Advantage** on Athletics (Swimming) checks and all checks made to pilot or navigate waterborne vessels.
- **Rhennee Heritage.** Choose one of the following lineages based on your character's background:
	- **Barge-Folk (Nyr Dyv):** You gain proficiency with **Navigator’s Tools**. You have **Advantage** on saving throws against being **Exhausted** by extreme weather or heavy labor.
	- **Attloi (Land-Caravan):** You swap your swimming bonuses for proficiency in the **Animal Handling** skill and one **Musical Instrument** of your choice.
	- **Vetha-Touched:** You carry the "Second Sight" of the wise women. You know the _Guidance_ cantrip. Wisdom is your spellcasting ability for it.
- **Languages.** You can speak, read, and write **Common** and **Rhennee Cant**.
