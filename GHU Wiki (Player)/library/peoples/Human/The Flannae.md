# **The History and Origins of the Flan** 
The Flannae (or Flan) are the first known human inhabitants of eastern Oerik, which is why the continent is named the Flanaess in their honor. Originally a peaceful, nomadic people of hunter-gatherers, they lived in the lands west of the Hellfurnaces before fleeing the persecution of their warlike Suel neighbors. Under the protection of their patron powers Beory, Pelor, and Rao, they made a perilous crossing of the Hellfurnaces in 3250 SD (-2266 CY).

Upon entering the Flanaess, they were initially welcomed by the native demi-humans (elves, dwarves, and gnomes), though relations eventually strained as the Flan built their own civilizations. In 3365 SD (-2151 CY), they founded their first great city, Haradaragh, in the Lortmil Mountains, signaling their shift from simple nomads to a settled people. The Flan went on to build legendary, and sometimes terrible, ancient empires. Some, like the realm of Queen Ehlissa, were utopian. Others, driven by the dark magic of the Ur-Flan, were steeped in necromancy, greed, and demonic worship, such as the cursed mountain-city of Tostenhca, the kingdom of Sulm, the Isles of Woe, and Vecna's terrifying Occluded Empire.

Ultimately, the Flan empires could not withstand the Great Migrations of the invading Suel and Oeridians. Decimated by these fierce conquerors, the Flan were pushed to the fringes of the continent or absorbed into the new Oeridian and Suel nations. Today, pure-blooded Flan are rare, though their cultural and religious influence remains a foundational pillar of the modern Flanaess.

>>[!example|wide-3]- Physical Appearance and Dress
>>The Flannae are easily distinguishable from other human races by their bronze complexions and strong, angular features with heavy brows. Their skin tones range from a lighter copper color to a deep, dark brown. **Males average between 5’3” to 5’6” tall and weigh 150-180 lbs, while females average 4’8” to 5’2” tall and weigh 110-130 lbs**, giving them a somewhat stocky, sturdy, and hirsute build. Their eyes are typically dark brown or black, with amber or brown appearing less frequently. Flan hair is usually black or brown-black, and is naturally wavy or curly (though some modern Flan have taken to straightening it).
>>
>>Ancient Flan nomads wore simple, utilitarian clothing, such as loincloths, capes, and leather foot-wrappings. They heavily favored brightly colored body paints—primarily vermilion and yellow ochre—applied in primitive patterns, a practice still proudly maintained by the surviving Rovers of the Barrens. In civilized regions, Flan clothing follows current fashions, though they strongly prefer solid, bright primary colors.
>
>> [!note|wide-3]- Culture and Personality
>> The Flan are reclusive, independent, practical, hard-working, and reserved. To outsiders, they can appear unemotional and slow to speak, but those who earn their trust will find them to be loyal, passionate, and jovial friends. They possess a profound "survivalist instinct" and are natural hunters and gatherers who easily learn to live off the land. Even when food is overly abundant, they tend to eat lean.
>> 
>> They have a deep, culturally ingrained love of nature, wind instruments, dancing, storytelling, and family ancestry. A uniquely Flan custom is the planting of a tree at the doorway of a new home. This tree connects the family to the Oerth Mother, Beory. If the tree sickens or dies, it is seen as a dire omen of bad luck or evil influence, prompting the family to move; conversely, travelers often carry a pressed leaf from their home tree for good luck. The Flan also highly respect the manufacturing of leather and wooden crafts, and tanning is a common practice.
>
>>[!warning|wide-2]- Subgroups and Factions
>>Because they were scattered by invaders, the Flan survive in distinct pockets:
>>- **The Rovers of the Barrens:** One of the few remaining pure-blooded Flan groups, these fierce, painted nomads maintain the ancient horse-culture of the plains.
>>- **The Tenha (Duchy of Tenh):** The other major pure-blooded Flan society, they are a civilized people who maintained their cohesion and built cities in the northeast.
>>- **Mixed Nations (Geoff, Sterich, Perrenland):** In these regions, the Flan intermingled heavily with Oeridians and Suel, but still maintain many of their ancient traditions, fighting styles, and druidic practices.
>>- **The Ur-Flan:** An ancient, sinister offshoot of the Flan who absconded from the Old Faith to delve into dark arts, necromancy, and the worship of demons and Tiamat. While their empires (Caerdiralor, Sulm, Isles of Woe) are dead, their dark legacy still taints the bloodlines of some modern Flan.
>
>> [!summary|min-0]- The Flannae Pantheon
>> The Flan are the originators of the **Old Faith**, the ancient druidic religion that believes nature is an independent, self-regulating entity bound by "The Balance". The pantheon centers on **Beory** (the Oerth Mother) and **Obad-Hai** (the Shalm, Lord of Nature). Other native Flan deities include:

>> * **Nerull** (the Reaper/Winter)

>> * **Allitur** (Ethics)

### Playing a Flannae Character
> [!abstract] Overview
> The **Flannae** (or Flan) are the first known human inhabitants of eastern Oerik, the "Ancient People" for whom the Flanaess is named. Once masters of legendary and sometimes terrible empires, they are now a scattered but resilient people defined by a primordial connection to the Oerth Mother and the Old Faith.
##### The Flan Mindset and Roleplaying Hooks
Playing a Flan character means honoring a primordial connection to the land and the spirits of the earth, embodying a resilient and mystical legacy that predates the arrival of all other human empires.

* **Nature's Bond:** You are heavily tied to the natural world and often feel claustrophobic or "trapped" in the stone-walled, crowded cities of the Oeridians. You possess an innate affinity for open spaces, the shifting of the wind, and the quiet growth of the "Old Faith."
* **The Weight of Antiquity:** As a member of the oldest human race on the continent, you carry a quiet, internal pride. You may look at the "new" kingdoms of the Suel and Oeridians as transient, temporary things compared to the ancient standing stones and hidden groves of your ancestors.
* **The Doorway Tree:** You likely follow the sacred custom of planting a tree at the entrance of your home to connect your family to Beory (the Oerth Mother). You might carry a pressed leaf from this tree as a lucky charm; should the tree die, you view it as a dire omen requiring immediate spiritual cleansing or relocation.
* **Reserved Loyalty:** To outsiders, you may appear slow to speak or even unemotional. However, this is a cultural pragmatism; once someone earns your trust, you are a jovially fierce and lifelong ally.
* **Unusual Allies:** You are the most inclined of all humans to live and work alongside Elves, Dwarves, and Gnomes. You remember the ancient alliances formed when your people first crossed the Hellfurnaces, and you find the "elder races" often have more in common with you than your fellow humans.
* **Survivalist Pragmatism:** Even in times of great abundance, you tend to eat lean and waste nothing. You respect the craft of leather and wood, viewing the skinning of a beast or the carving of a bow as a sacred cycle of the Oerth.

## Physical Description
The Flan are easily distinguishable by their bronze complexions and sturdy, angular features:
* **Skin:** Ranges from a lighter copper to a deep, dark brown.
* **Eyes:** Typically dark brown or black; amber occurs less frequently.
* **Hair:** Black or brown-black; naturally wavy or curly.
* **Build:** Stocky and sturdy with heavy brows. Males average 5’3” to 5’6”; females average 4’8” to 5’2”.
* **Dress:** Historically utilitarian (leather and loincloths) with bright vermilion and yellow ochre body paints. Civilized Flan prefer solid, bright primary colors.

## Flan Human Traits
As a Flan human, you have the following racial traits.

* **Ability Score Increase.** Your **Wisdom** score increases by 2, and your **Constitution** score increases by 1. However, your **Intelligence** score decreases by 1 (reflecting a cultural preference for intuition over academic study).
* **Age.** Humans reach adulthood in their late teens and live less than a century.
* **Size.** Your size is **Medium**.
* **Speed.** Your base walking speed is **30 feet**.
* **Survivalist Instinct.** You have proficiency in the **Survival** skill. You can forage for food and water for yourself and up to five other people as long as the land provides.
* **Earth-Bonded.** You have a mystical connection to the natural world. You have **Advantage** on saving throws against being **Poisoned** or contracting **Diseases**.
* Flannae Heritage. Choose one of the following lineages based on your character's background:
	* **Steppe-Rover Lineage (Barrens):** Descended from the hardy nomads of the north and west, your people are the masters of the long horizon. Your base walking speed increases to **35 feet**. You also have proficiency with the **Shortbow** and the **Shortsword**. You ignore difficult terrain caused by non-magical flora (brush, briars, and tall grass). Additionally, you have Advantage on checks made to resist being Tracked.
	* **Tenha Lineage (Northeast):** _Your people are the keepers of the ancient Flannae chronicles, possessing a resilience born from centuries of defending their borders._

- **Skills:** You gain proficiency in **History** and **Insight**.
    
- **Ancient Memory:** You can read and write **Old Ferral** (the secret tongue of the Flannae). Additionally, you can add your **Wisdom modifier** to any Intelligence (History) check you make regarding the ruins or lore of the Flanaess.
    
- **Resolute Mind:** You have Advantage on saving throws against being **Charmed**.
	* **Old Faith Lineage (Geoff/Sterich):** You have proficiency in the **Medicine** skill and gain the **Druidcraft** cantrip. Wisdom is your spellcasting ability for it.
* **Languages.** You can speak, read, and write **Common** and **Flan**. You may choose **Sylvan** as an additional language.
