# **The History of the Olvenfolk**

The history of the High Elves (Olve) spans over five millennia, beginning with "The Awakening" following the legendary battle between Corellon Larethian and the orc-god Gruumsh. Long before the human migrations, elven kingdoms ruled the Flanaess.

The most storied of these was **Forodorn**, a rare High Elf-dominated empire that stretched from the Yatil Mountains to the Lortmils. Forodorn was eventually sundered by the terrifying empire of **The Whispered One, Vecna**. Following this defeat, the survivors were pushed into the Vesve Forest (becoming the Highfolk) and southwest into Celene. Today, they are the most cooperative of the elven subraces, maintaining vital alliances with the humans of the West.

### **Playing a High Elf (Olve)**

##### **The Olve Mindset**

- **The Weight of Memory:** You remember the spires of Forodorn before Vecna cast them down. You treat ruins with the reverence of palaces.
    
- **The Long Trance:** You do not sleep. You enter **Reverie** for 4 hours to sort memories. You are aware of your surroundings via passive senses but require an additional 4 hours of light activity to finish a Long Rest.
    
- **Ethereal Superiority:** To you, human iron is brittle and human speech is clumsy. You don't intend to be rude; it is simply a fact of your long life.
    

---

##### **High Elf (Olve) Traits**

_These traits are added to your Base Elf statistics._

- **Ability Score Increase.** Your **Intelligence** score increases by 1.
    
- **Olven Martial Training.** You are proficient with the **Longsword, Shortsword, Shortbow, and Longbow**, the traditional weapons of the Olvenfolk refined over five millennia of warfare.
    
- **High Elf Heritage.** Choose one specialized lineage:
    
    - **Knight of the High Forest (Vesve):** You are a master of guerrilla warfare. You gain proficiency in **Stealth** and **Survival**. You have Advantage on Stealth checks in forest terrain.
        
    - **Knight of Luna (Chivalric):** You are dedicated to elven chivalry. You gain proficiency in **Persuasion** and **Insight**. You have Advantage on saving throws against being Frightened.
        
    - **Moonbow's Grace (Mystic):** You possess the "Dreamsight," a gift of the Seldarine that opens the mind to the threads of magic. You gain proficiency in **Arcana** and know one **cantrip** of your choice from the **Wizard** spell list (Intelligence is your casting stat).
