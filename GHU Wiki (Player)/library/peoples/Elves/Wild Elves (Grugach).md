# **The History of the Grugach**

The Grugach are an ancient offshoot of the Sylvan Olve who retreated into the deepest, most inaccessible "temperate bands" of the Flanaess following the Great Migrations. While their cousins sought alliances with humans to survive, the Grugach chose **absolute xenophobia**.

They believe that every contact with "outsiders" (including other elves) is a potential taint. Their history is unwritten, passed down through ritual tattoos and oral chants. In 576 CY, they act as the "Hidden Teeth" of the old forests, ensuring that any who wander into their woods without permission never return.

> > [!example|wide-3]- Physical Appearance and Feral Stance
> > 
> > Grugach are smaller, lighter and thinner than any other elves, **standing between 4'2" and 5'0" and weighing only 80–110 lbs**. They are slight and wiry, all sinew and no softness, and far stronger than their size suggests. Their skin is **very fair**, often covered in **smears of mud, crushed leaf-pigment, and ritual scarification**. Their hair is **dark brown or black**, wild and unkempt, matted with twigs or bone charms, and it **turns silvery-white with great age**.
> > 
> > Their "fashion" is purely functional: **hide and leather worked with forest motifs, bone jewelry, and leather straps** for carrying obsidian-tipped spears. They eschew all metalwork, believing it "deadens" the spirit's connection to the wood.
> 
> > [!note|wide-3]- Culture: The Law of the Snare
> > 
> > Grugach society is tribal and brutally meritocratic. They do not value books, gold, or diplomacy. They value the **Hunt, the Trap, and the Tribe**.
> > 
> > - **Xenophobia:** To a Grugach, anyone who does not speak the tribal dialect of Elven is a predator or prey. They do not speak Common.
> >     
> > - **The Primitive Arcane:** While they shun "Book Magic," they possess a raw, instinctual connection to the Feywild, manifesting as supernatural stealth and the ability to command the very vines of the forest.
> >     
> 
> > [!warning|wide-2]- Hidden Homelands
> > 
> > Each of the six known Grugach families holds one of these forests, and none of them will tell you where the others are:
> > 
> > - **The Celadon Forest** — between Nyrond and the County of Urnst.
> > - **The Grandwood** — deep in the lands of the Great Kingdom.
> > - **The Vesve Forest** — the Vesve deep, where even the Highfolk will not track.
> > - **The Hornwood** — in Geoff, beneath the shadow of the Crystalmists.
> > - **The Phostwood** — the wood of pale, glowing trees.
> > - **The Valley of the Mage** — hidden even from the valley's own elves.

### **Playing a Grugach (Wild Elf)**

##### **The Grugach Mindset**

- **The Silent Hunter:** You do not speak unless absolutely necessary. You communicate through clicks, whistles, and hand signals.
    
- **Instinct over Reason:** You trust your nose and your gut. If a "civilized" person explains a complex plan, you judge its merit by how it smells.
    
- **Territorial Protector:** You view the party as your "pack." Anyone outside the pack is a threat until proven otherwise—and proving otherwise usually requires a life-debt.

##### **Grugach (Wild Elf) Traits**

_These traits are added to your Base Elf statistics._

- **Ability Score Increase.** Your **Strength** score increases by 2, and your **Dexterity** score increases by 1. However, your **Intelligence** decreases by 2 (reflecting a total rejection of formal education and literacy).
    
- **Feral Instinct.** You have **Advantage** on Wisdom (Survival) checks to track creatures and Intelligence (Nature) checks to identify poisonous plants or animals.
    
- **Trapmaster.** You gain proficiency with **Thieves' Tools** (used here as Trapping Tools). When you spend 10 minutes setting a trap in a natural environment, the DC to detect or disarm it increases by your Proficiency Bonus.
    
- **Primitive Weapon Mastery.** You are proficient with **Spears, Short swords, Longbows, and Nets**. When using a spear, you deal an extra **1d4 damage** on a successful hit.
    
- **Languages.** This replaces the base elf Languages trait. You can speak **Elven** and **Sylvan**. You do not speak Common.
    
- **Illiterate.** You cannot read or write any language, including Common or Elven. You can learn to read and write only through a Downtime Activity spent studying under a tutor of adequate skill.
    
- **Grugach Heritage.** Choose one specialized tribal path:
    
    - **Deepwood Stalker:** You are a master of the silent kill. You can take the **Hide** action as a bonus action while in a forest or jungle.
        
    - **Pit-Viper:** You know the secret of elven neurotoxins. You can apply poison to your weapons as a bonus action. You gain proficiency with the **Poisoner's Kit**.
        
    - **Spoke-Singer:** You possess a raw connection to the forest's voice. You can cast _Speak with Animals_ at will, but only with creatures native to your home forest.
