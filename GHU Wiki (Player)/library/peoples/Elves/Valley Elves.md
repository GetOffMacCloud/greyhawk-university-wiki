# **The History of the Valley Elves**

The origin of the Valley Elves is a subject of intense academic debate. Some believe they are a localized offshoot of the High Elves who were altered by the strange, high-altitude magics of the **Barrier Peaks**. Others whisper that they are the result of ancient crossbreeding between Olvenfolk and early Suloise explorers, though the elves themselves vehemently deny this.

Their history is inextricably linked to the **Mage of the Valley**. For centuries, they have served as his private army and labor force. They helped construct the Mage’s hidden fortresses and now patrol the narrow passes with a cold, mechanical efficiency. Because of their loyalty to a human wizard and their willingness to mingle with "lesser" races for his benefit, they are viewed as outcasts by the Court of Celene.

> > [!example|wide-3]- Appearance and Human Semblance
> > 
> > Valley Elves are notably taller than their kin, **standing between 5’6” and 6’2” (avg. 150 lbs)**, putting them at nearly human height. Their ears are slightly less pronounced, and their features, while sharp, lack the ethereal "otherworldliness" of the Olve. Their skin is typically a **deep, healthy tan**, and their hair is most often **dirty blonde or light brown**.
> > 
> > They dress for utility and infiltration. Unlike the flowing robes of the Grey Elf or the leaf-leathers of the Sylvan Elf, Valley Elves wear **sturdy human-style travel gear**: wool tunics, heavy leather boots, and dark cloaks. They are the only elves who regularly cut their hair short to blend into human crowds.
> 
> > [!note|wide-3]- Personality: The Cold Pragmatists
> > 
> > Valley Elves lack the boisterousness of Wood Elves and the arrogance of Grey Elves. They are **quiet, observant, and intensely pragmatic**. They view the world through the lens of "The Mission."
> > 
> > - **Social Friction:** Their bluntness and lack of elven "poetry" makes them seem robotic or "hollow" to other elves. Among humans, they are often seen as cold and untrusting.
> >     
> > - **The Mage's Brand:** Every Valley Elf carries a deep, subconscious loyalty to their valley home. They are difficult to bribe and impossible to intimidate.
> >     
> 
> > [!warning|wide-2]- The Hidden Valley
> > 
> > - **The Barrier Peaks:** The only place where these elves live in large numbers. Their villages are built into the cliff-faces, accessible only by narrow, trapped trails.
> >     
> > - **The Mage's Spies:** Small groups of Valley Elves can be found in the Great Kingdom or the City of Greyhawk, working as mercenaries while secretly funneling information back to their master.

### **Playing a Valley Elf**

##### **The Valley Elf Mindset**

- **The Outsider:** You are an elf who doesn't fit in with elves, and a "near-human" who isn't human. You find comfort in logic and orders rather than tradition or nature.
    
- **Infiltration Expert:** You know how to walk through a human market without drawing a second glance. You've mastered the "Human Shrug" and the "Bored Human Stare."
    
- **Arcane Cynic:** You have grown up around the highest levels of wizardry. You aren't impressed by fireballs; you treat magic like a dangerous tool that must be respected but never worshipped.
    

---

##### **Valley Elf Traits**

_These traits are added to your Base Elf statistics._

- **Ability Score Increase.** Your **Intelligence** score increases by 1 and your **Constitution** score increases by 1. However, your **Charisma** decreases by 1 (due to your cold, unsettling demeanor).
    
- **Arcane Resistance.** Living in a valley of concentrated magic has hardened your spirit. You have **Advantage on saving throws against Enchantment and Illusion spells**. In addition, you can use your reaction to gain Advantage on a saving throw against any other spell a number of times equal to your Proficiency Bonus, regaining all uses after a long rest.
    
- **Human Semblance.** You gain proficiency in the **Deception** skill. Additionally, you have Advantage on Charisma (Deception) checks made to pass yourself off as a human.
    
- **Martial Versatility.** You have proficiency with **Shortswords, Longswords, and Crossbows** (Light and Heavy). In the Valley, the crossbow is favored over the traditional elven bow for its mechanical precision.
    
- **Valley Heritage.** Choose one specialized role within the Mage’s service:
    
    - **Vanguard Scout:** You are trained to lead patrols through the Barrier Peaks. Your base walking speed increases to **35 feet**, and you gain proficiency in **Acrobatics**.
        
    - **Arcane Warden:** You have been trained to capture rogue mages alive. You know the _Shocking Grasp_ cantrip. Once per long rest, you can cast _Hold Person_ without expending a spell slot (Intelligence is your spellcasting ability for this spell).
        
    - **Deep Infiltrator:** You are a master of human languages and customs. You gain proficiency with the **Disguise Kit** and learn two additional human dialects (e.g., **Suloise** and **Keolandish**).
