# **The History of the Sylvan Olve**

The Sylvan Elves are an ancient, naturalistic people who spread across the Flanaess long before the first human kingdoms. Unlike their High Elf cousins, the Sylvan clans chose a life of harmony with the deep woods. When the **Flan** tribes first arrived, the Wood Elves welcomed them, leading to a massive cultural exchange of nature lore and the shared worship of the forest gods.

Over centuries of human expansion, the Sylvan Olve were pushed into the deepest forests. They became the "Hidden Guardians," forging pragmatic alliances with dwarves, gnomes, and rangers to protect their borders. Today, they are instrumental in the defense of the **Highfolk** and the northern forests against the encroaching darkness of Iuz.

> > 
> > Wood elves average **five feet in height with a slim, athletic build (approx. 120 lbs)**. Their skin is a **tanned, ruddy, or coppery hue**, darker than the Olve. Their eyes are hazel or green, and their hair ranges from **coppery red to bright blonde**.

> > They dress in the "Colors of the Season": pale greens, fawns, and dove grays for daily life, shifting to **solid browns and russets** for war. They are masters of blending into the undergrowth, often appearing as nothing more than shifting shadows to the untrained human eye.
> 
> > [!note|wide-3]- Culture and Personality
> > 
> > Sylvan elves are reclusive but **boisterous and fun-loving** once their trust is earned. They value physical prowess and survival skills over high magic. Their society is strictly **clan-based**, with precise social rules governing family roles (Singers, Herbalists, Hunters).
> > 
> > - **Tree-Cities:** They do not build stone monuments. They live in "Tree-Cities" connected by vine bridges or simple hidden hamlets that leave no mark on the earth.
> >

> > [!warning|wide-2]- Regional Alliances

> > - **The Fellreev Alliance:** A gritty, pragmatic alliance with human bandits to wage guerrilla war against Iuz.
> >     
> > - **The Gamboge Clans:** The most militant Wood Elves, standing in constant readiness against the Bone March.

### **Playing a Wood Elf (Sylvan Olve)**

##### **The Wood Elf Mindset**

- **Wary but Loyal:** You are suspicious of "stone-dwellers," but you will die for an ally who proves they respect the balance of the wild.
    
- **Master of the Ambush:** You find pitched battles foolish. You prefer to strike from the shadows and vanish before the enemy can draw their steel.
    
- **The Call of Lanna Raer:** Your faith is physical. Every hunt is a prayer to Ehlonna, and every strike against evil is a tribute to Trithereon.
    

---

##### **Wood Elf (Sylvan) Traits**

_These traits are added to your Base Elf statistics._

- **Ability Score Increase.** Your **Wisdom** or **Strength** score increases by 1.
    
- **Sylvan Martial Training.** You are proficient with the **Longsword, Shortsword, Shortbow, and Longbow**, the ancestral weapons of the forest clans, honed across ages of border warfare.
    
- **Mask of the Wild.** You can attempt to hide even when you are only lightly obscured by foliage, heavy rain, falling snow, mist, or other natural phenomena.
    
- **Fleet of Foot.** Your base walking speed increases to **35 feet**.
    
- **Sylvan Heritage.** Choose one specialized lineage:
    
    - **Fellreev Skirmisher:** You are a ghost of the north. You gain proficiency in **Stealth**. You have Advantage on Stealth checks made in forest terrain.
        
    - **Silverwood Caretaker:** You know the secrets of the roanwoods. You gain proficiency in **Medicine** and **Survival**. You can spend 10 minutes to harvest medicinal sap that functions as a _Potion of Healing_ (lasts 24 hours).
        
    - **Grugach Trapper:** (Wild Elf Only). You have lived by snare and ambush. You gain proficiency in **Survival** and with the **Handaxe** and **Net**. You have Advantage on checks made to craft or detect mechanical traps (pits, snares, spikes).
