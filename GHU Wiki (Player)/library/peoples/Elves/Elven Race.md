# **The History and Origins of the Elven Race**

The Elves (known as _Olve_ in their own tongue) are not native to the Prime Material Plane in the same way humans or dwarves are. Their legends speak of a time when the boundary between Oerth and the Feywild was a shimmering veil, allowing their ancestors to step through. They are the "Firstborn" of the Flanaess, having established vast, singing empires long before the Great Migrations of the Suloise and Oeridians.

The history of the elves is a tragic chronicle of "The Long Retreat." As the human populations expanded and the Twin Cataclysms reshaped the world, the elves found their vast forests shrinking. While they are masters of magic and blade, their low birth rates and immense lifespans make every elven life an irreplaceable treasure. By 576 CY, the elven race is a beautiful but fading sunset, clinging to their remaining forest kingdoms and hidden valleys.

> [!multi-column] Contents
> 
> > [!example|wide-3]- Physical Appearance and Physiology
> > 
> > Elves are defined by an **ethereal, haunting grace**. They are shorter and slimmer than humans, standing between **5’0” to 6’0” tall and weighing only 100-145 lbs**. Their bones are light, and their movements are fluid, almost liquid. They lack the body hair of humans and possess **large, almond-shaped eyes** and the iconic **tapered, pointed ears** that can hear the heartbeat of the forest.
> > 
> > Their physiology is fundamentally different from humans. They **do not sleep**; instead, they enter a state of "Reverie," a semiconscious meditation that lasts 4 hours. During this time, they mentally sort their centuries of memories. An elf who is denied this trance becomes physically and mentally unraveled.
> 
> > [!note|wide-3]- Culture and Personality
> > 
> > To an elf, time is a river, not a ticking clock. Because they can live for **750 years**, they are rarely in a hurry. They are patient, contemplative, and can seem aloof or cold to shorter-lived races. They value **Art, Nature, and Arcane Mastery** above gold or land.
> > 
> > Elven society is highly specialized. An elf might spend 100 years mastering the curve of a single bow or 50 years perfecting one spell. They are **fiercely protective of their borders** and highly secretive about their internal politics. To an elf, a promise is a sacred bond that can span human generations.
>
> > [!warning|wide-2]- The Elven Temperament
> > 
> > - **Detachment:** Elves often seem "spaced out" to humans because they are perceiving the flow of magic and time that humans ignore.
> >     
> > - **Haughtiness:** They don't necessarily think they are "better" than humans—they simply _know_ they have more experience. An elven commoner has seen more history than a human king.
> >     
> > - **Vengeance:** Elves do not forget slights. A blood-feud between an elven house and a human village can last for centuries.
>
> > [!summary|min-0]- The Elven Pantheon (The Seldarine)
> > The Elves revere the Seldarine, the "Fellowship of Brothers and Sisters of the Wood." They do not build massive cathedrals; instead, they worship in natural groves and stone circles.
> > 
> > - **Corellon Larethian:** The Creator. God of Magic, Arts, and War.
> > - **Sehanine Moonbow:** Goddess of Dreams, Death, and the Moon.
> > - **Solonor Thelandira:** God of Archery and the Hunt.
> > - **Labelas Enoreth:** God of Time, Philosophy, and Longevity.

### **Playing an Elven Character**

> [!abstract] Overview
> 
> The **Elf** is the master of the long game. Whether they are a wizard, a ranger, or a fighter, they rely on **Agility and Perception** over brute force. They are the survivors of an older, more magical world.

##### **Elven Roleplaying Hooks:**

- **Memory as Identity:** Your memories are your most prized possessions. Losing your memory is worse than death.
    
- **Formalism:** You find human slang and shortcuts annoying. You speak with a formal, rhythmic cadence.
    
- **Nature Connection:** You feel physically ill in stagnant, polluted, or overly industrial human cities (like parts of the Great Kingdom).

##### Elven Racial Traits (Base)

- **Ability Score Increase.** Your **Dexterity** score increases by 2. However, your **Constitution** decreases by 1 (due to your slender, light-boned frame).
    
- **Age.** Elves reach physical maturity at 20 but are not socially considered adults until 100. They live up to 750 years.
    
- **Size.** You are Medium.
    
- **Speed.** Your base walking speed is **30 feet**.
    
- **Darkvision.** Accustomed to twilight forests and the night sky, you can see in dim light within 60 feet as if it were bright light, and in darkness as if it were dim light.
    
- **Fey Ancestry.** You have **Advantage** on saving throws against being **Charmed**, and magic can’t put you to sleep.
    
- **Trance (Reverie).** You don’t need to sleep. Instead, you meditate deeply, remaining semiconscious, for 4 hours a day.
    
- **Keen Senses.** You have proficiency in the **Perception** skill.
    
- **Languages.** You can speak, read, and write **Common** and **Elven**.
