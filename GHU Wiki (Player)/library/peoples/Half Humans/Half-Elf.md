# **The History and Origins of the Half-Elves**

Unlike the ancient human and demi-human races of the Flanaess, half-elves do not have a unified history, a sprawling ancestral empire, or a true homeland to call their own. They are the result of the fact that while demi-human races have historically remained relatively unmixed, humanity has industriously intermixed with other populations wherever they have expanded.

The origins of half-elves are often fraught with social complexities. In many lands, there are "unhappy circumstances" surrounding their birth, leading to a life where they must find their own way in the world as outcasts or orphans. Even among their elven kin, their acceptance is not guaranteed. Many elves, particularly the haughty gray elves, view half-elves with deep suspicion, believing them to possess a divided nature and uncertain allegiances. This distrust often masks the elves' own discomfort regarding their race's history and origins.

However, in cosmopolitan or highly cooperative regions like the Duchy of Ulek, half-elves are celebrated, standing as a living bridge between the strengths of both humans and elves.

>
>> [!example|wide-3]- Physical Appearance and Dress
>> Half-elves blend the features of their human and elven parents, resulting in a wide, striking variety of appearances. They typically possess a **lithe, attractive build**. Depending on their specific human and elven bloodlines, their features can vary wildly: some possess dark curly hair, violet eyes, and golden-tan skin; others have jet-black hair and pale skin; while others still may have coppery rust-brown hair and pale green eyes.
>>
>> Their dress generally reflects the culture in which they were raised. A half-elf from the deep forests will favor traditional woodland garb, while those living in human cities will adopt the local fashion, occasionally adding a touch of elven flair.
>
>> [!note|wide-3]- Culture and Personality
>> Caught between two worlds, half-elves are **notoriously adaptable, versatile, and driven**. Because they often lack a true societal safety net, they frequently turn to the adventuring life or seek out specialized professions where their unique talents are valued. They are naturally drawn to multi-classing, utilizing both martial prowess and magical or thieving skills to survive.
>>
>> Interestingly, half-elves are heavily over-represented in **martial monastic orders**; their physical grace combined with the isolation of a monastery appeals to those seeking an escape from a prejudiced society.
>>
>> Half-elves who live near human communities frequently adopt a specialized naming convention. To make dealing with humans easier, they will often take a traditional, melodious elven family name (like Silverfrond or Starglow) and append a specific career or location tag to it. In the Spindrift Isles, the term "half-elf" is rarely used at all; the locals instead refer to them by the slang term **"Hulf"** (a combination of Human and Elf).
>
>> [!warning|wide-2]- Subgroups and Factions
>> Because they rarely have their own nations, half-elves tend to congregate in specific regions or join dedicated organizations:
>>
>> - **The Duchy of Ulek:** This elven realm holds thousands of half-elves, making them perhaps more common here than anywhere else in the Flanaess. Human and half-elven bards are considered among the finest in the world due to their close association with elven minstrels. Half-elves here frequently serve as paladins and champions.
>> - **The Hospii (City of Glass):** A unique, nomadic group of mixed descendants (primarily of high elf and Suloise heritage). They escaped to the wilderness surrounding the City of Glass when the elves fully took over the Spindrift Isles.
>> - **The Knights of the Pursuit Rune:** A martial order heavily populated by half-elves and half-orcs. Wearing blue chainmail emblazoned with a silver rune of pursuit, these knights dedicate themselves to overcoming great oppression, driven by the unhappy circumstances of their own youths.
>
>> [!summary|min-0]- The Half-Elven Pantheon
>> Half-elves may worship the deities of either of their parent races. Many find solace in the **Old Faith**, revering the Oerth Mother Beory or Obad-Hai.
>>
>> Those with a martial or chivalric bent may become "Valiants," specialty priests of the Oeridian war god **Heironeous**. Others who dedicate themselves to freedom and the protection of the oppressed gravitate toward **Trithereon**.

### **Playing a Half-Elf Character**

##### **The Half-Elf Mindset and Roleplaying Hooks**

- **The Bridge or the Outcast:** You must decide how your character views their heritage. Do you embrace the harmonious acceptance found in places like the Duchy of Ulek, serving as a diplomat and bard between the races? Or do you harbor resentment for the gray elves who view your allegiances as uncertain and divided?
- **Driven to Prove Yourself:** Knowing the hardships and "unhappy circumstances" that often plague half-elven births, you may possess a fierce, protective streak. You are highly likely to intercede when you see others being oppressed, perhaps joining a knightly order or monastic tradition to hone your skills.

##### **Fighting Styles**

Half-elves rarely specialize in one discipline — they survive by being versatile. Many become bards, rangers, and multiclass warriors-turned-wizards, pairing rapier and shortbow with a carefully-chosen repertoire of spells. The monastic orders of the Flanaess are disproportionately half-elven; there, they train to fight unarmed and unarmored, trusting grace over gear.

# **Half-Elf Racial Traits**

> [!abstract] Overview
> The **Half-Elves** are the Flanaess's living hinge between human ambition and elven endurance. They inherit fey resilience to charm and sleep, a versatile spread of ability improvements, and an adaptable social grace — but no ancestral homeland. Their power is their range: there is almost nothing a half-elf cannot learn to do well.

---

## Physical Description

- **Skin:** Highly variable — pale, golden-tan, olive, or copper-brown, depending on parent bloodlines.
- **Hair:** Black, brown, auburn, blond, or rusty copper; often worn long in elven fashion.
- **Eyes:** Wide range; violet, green, amber, hazel, blue, or brown — more striking than purely human coloring.
- **Build:** Lithe and attractive; slightly taller than elves, slightly slimmer than humans.
- **Dress:** Reflects culture of upbringing; woodland garb for those raised among elves, local fashion with elven flourishes for city-raised half-elves.

---

## Half-Elf Traits

As a Half-Elf, you have the following racial traits.

- **Ability Score Increase.** Your **Charisma** score increases by 1. Two other ability scores of your choice each increase by 1 as well, reflecting your blend of human flexibility and elven grace.
- **Age.** Half-elves mature at the same rate as humans and reach adulthood around age 20. They live much longer than humans, however, often exceeding 180 years.
- **Size.** Your size is **Medium**.
- **Speed.** Your base walking speed is **30 feet**.
- **Darkvision.** Thanks to your elven heritage, you have superior vision in dark and dim conditions. You can see in dim light within 60 feet as if it were bright light, and in darkness as if it were dim light. You can't discern color in darkness, only shades of gray.
- **Fey Ancestry.** You have Advantage on saving throws against being **Charmed**, and magic can't put you to sleep.
- **Diplomatic Grace.** You can navigate the social boundaries of both human and elven societies with ease. You gain proficiency in the **Persuasion** or **Deception** skill (your choice). Additionally, once per short rest, when you make a Charisma check while interacting with an elf or human NPC, you may gain Advantage on that roll.
- **Skill Versatility.** You gain proficiency in two skills of your choice, reflecting the breadth of experience half-elves accumulate from navigating two worlds.
- **Half-Elven Heritage.** Choose one lineage based on your upbringing:
    - **Ulek Minstrel:** Trained alongside the legendary elven bards of the Duchy of Ulek, you gain proficiency with one musical instrument of your choice and the **Performance** skill. When you use the Help action to assist an ally's Charisma check, your ally gains an additional +2 to the roll.
    - **Hospii Nomad:** Descended from the exiled wanderers of the Spindrift Isles, you are adapted to a rugged, transient life. You gain proficiency in **Survival** and **Stealth**, and you have Advantage on checks made to navigate in wilderness areas.
    - **Pursuit Knight:** Sworn to protect those who suffer as you once did, you gain proficiency in **Athletics**. When you hit a creature that is restraining, grappling, or otherwise physically controlling an unwilling creature, you deal an extra **1d4 damage** on your attack roll.
- **Languages.** You can speak, read, and write **Common**, **Elvish**, and one language of your choice.
