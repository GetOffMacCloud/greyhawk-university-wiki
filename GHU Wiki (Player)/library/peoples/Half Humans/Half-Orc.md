# **The History and Origins of the Half-Orcs**

Unlike the ancient human and demi-human races of the Flanaess, half-orcs do not have a unified history, a sprawling ancestral empire, or a true homeland to call their own. They are the crossbred descendants of humans and orcs (the Euroz), often born of "unhappy circumstances" following raids, warfare, or captivity.

Because of their heritage, half-orcs are almost universally treated as social outcasts, relegated to living among orcish tribes or in the squalid slums of human cities. Some regions are more tolerant than others. The Bandit Kingdoms, particularly the cities of Stoink and Rookroost, hold significant half-orc enclaves, and the humanoid-ruled Pomarj has no shortage of them.

The prejudice has deep roots. The **Hateful Wars** of 498 to 510 CY, in which the dwarves, gnomes and elves of the Lortmils drove the humanoid tribes out of the mountains, ended with those tribes pouring into the Pomarj and overrunning it. In 576 CY the Pomarj is still a patchwork of orc and ogre chieftains raiding the Wild Coast, the Bone March's marcher lords are losing ground to the humanoids of the Blemu Hills, and the Old One's return to the north has put an orc-friendly demigod back on a throne. To the farmers of Keoland and the burghers of Nyrond, a half-orc is the war walking into town. In the Yeomanry, Sterich, and northern Aerdy a half-orc who is recognized can expect to be run out, or worse. A half-orc must tread carefully, constantly proving their worth to a world that inherently shuns and distrusts them.

>> [!example|wide-3]- Physical Appearance and Dress
>> Half-orcs blend the features of their human and orcish parents, though many player characters are those who can "pass" as exceptionally ugly, brutish, or scarred humans. They average **around 5'0" to 6'0" in height and weigh heavily, usually between 135 to nearly 200 pounds**, possessing thick skin, heavy brows, and a naturally muscular, imposing build.
>>
>> Their dress is entirely utilitarian and heavily reliant on the culture that raised them. Those raised among orcs or bandit gangs favor **crude armors, spiked leathers, and clan insignia**—such as the distinctive symbols of the "Clear Sky" camp. Half-orcs living in human cities dress down to avoid drawing unwanted attention, favoring dark, practical traveling clothes, heavy cloaks, and easily accessible weapons.
>
>> [!note|wide-3]- Culture and Personality
>> A half-orc raised in human or orcish society quickly learns that they **cannot appear weak, injured, or cowardly** if they expect to live long. This harsh reality promotes an unusually serious, forceful, and pragmatic personality well-suited for adventuring or harsh military duty. Half-orcs are usually entirely free of the tribal ties and loyalties that bind true orcs, which makes them excellent go-betweens or extremely loyal mercenaries for whoever is willing to pay and respect them.
>>
>> Due to their harsh upbringing, many half-orcs have a predisposition toward brute-force aggression, egotism, and a carnivorous appetite. Many find a comfortable living as "thugs," unconcerned with larger issues of morality so long as they are paid to intimidate others. However, those of non-evil alignments who overcome the cold glares, refusal of service, and bigotry can become steadfast allies, fierce protectors, and celebrated heroes.
>
>> [!warning|wide-2]- Subgroups and Factions
>> Because they are outcasts, half-orcs tend to congregate in specific fringe groups, mercenary bands, or accepting guilds:
>>
>> - **The Bandit Kingdoms and the Lands of Iuz:** Since his return in 570 CY the Old One's priesthood has made a special effort to recruit half-orcs, placing them in positions of seeming authority where their local knowledge is put to deadly use. They serve as spies, go-betweens and garrison captains, though they frequently clash with the true-blooded humanoids of the Horned Society next door.
>> - **The Pomarj:** The orc and ogre chieftains of the Drachensgrab Hills breed half-orcs by the hundred and value them as scouts, translators and raid-captains against the Wild Coast. A half-orc warlord called Turrosh Mak has begun gathering a following there.
>> - **The City of Greyhawk:** Half-orcs are rare but not unknown in the Domain of Greyhawk. They maintain a small population, most famously finding work in the River Quarter under the aging but active Selczek Gobayuik, the half-orc Guildmaster of Embalmers and Gravediggers. A few find employ with Turin Deathstalker's Guild of Assassins.
>> - **The Knights of the Pursuit Rune:** A noble martial order easily distinguishable by their blue chainmail emblazoned with a silver rune. Sworn to overcome great oppression and rescue those in need, this order boasts a disproportionate number of half-orcs and half-elves who use the unhappy circumstances of their own births to fuel their crusade for justice.
>
>> [!summary|min-0]- The Half-Orc Pantheon
>> Half-orcs have no pantheon of their own and adopt the gods of the societies they inhabit. Evil half-orcs serving in the north often revere **Iuz**, **Nerull**, or the orcish powers like **Gruumsh** and **Bahgtru**.
>>
>> Neutral or good half-orcs seeking to protect others or find acceptance in human lands frequently become devout followers of **St. Cuthbert** or the Oeridian war god **Heironeous**.

### **Playing a Half-Orc Character**

##### **The Half-Orc Mindset and Roleplaying Hooks**

- **The Pragmatic Survivor (The Thug):** You are unimaginative about grand morality but highly practical. You know that gold spends the same whether it comes from a noble or a crime boss. You make your living as a bodyguard, enforcer, or mercenary, relying on your terrifying physical presence to solve problems before weapons are even drawn.
- **Proving Them Wrong:** You were raised in a society that expects you to be a marauding monster. You are driven by a burning need to prove your honor, courage, and worth. You might be fiercely protective of the weak, taking up the mantle of a paladin or a Knight of the Pursuit Rune to ensure no one suffers the bigotry and helplessness you endured.

##### **Fighting Styles**

Half-orcs favor brute-force aggression. They excel with large, heavy martial weapons — greataxes, greatswords, morningstars — often pairing them with chain mail or studded leather to maximize mobility while still absorbing punishment. They seldom use shields, trusting their toughness and menace to carry the fight.

# **Half-Orc Racial Traits**

> [!abstract] Overview
> The **Half-Orcs** are the hardest-pressed people of the Flanaess — forged by prejudice, tempered in war, and rarely given an inch of welcome. Their relentless endurance and savage critical damage make them devastating frontline warriors, while their menace and shrewdness let them survive cities that would sooner have them dead.

---

## Physical Description

- **Skin:** Thick; gray-green to sallow human tones, often mottled.
- **Hair:** Coarse black or dark brown; frequently worn in warrior knots or cropped short.
- **Eyes:** Dark brown, amber, or red-brown; tusks protrude slightly from the lower jaw.
- **Build:** Muscular and imposing; 5'0" to 6'0" tall, 135-200 lbs; heavy brows and broad shoulders.
- **Dress:** Utilitarian; crude armors and clan insignia among orcish bands, dark practical travel clothes in human cities.

## Half-Orc Traits

As a Half-Orc, you have the following racial traits.

- **Ability Score Increase.** Your **Strength** score increases by 2, and your **Constitution** score increases by 1. However, your **Charisma** decreases by 1, reflecting a brutish demeanor forged by a lifetime of prejudice and hard living.
- **Age.** Half-orcs mature a little faster than humans, reaching adulthood around age 14. They age noticeably faster and rarely live longer than 75 years.
- **Size.** Your size is **Medium**.
- **Speed.** Your base walking speed is **30 feet**.
- **Darkvision.** Thanks to your orcish heritage, you have superior vision in dark and dim conditions. You can see in dim light within 60 feet as if it were bright light, and in darkness as if it were dim light. You can't discern color in darkness, only shades of gray.
- **Menacing.** You gain proficiency in the **Intimidation** skill.
- **Relentless Endurance.** When you are reduced to 0 hit points but not killed outright, you can drop to 1 hit point instead. Once you use this trait, you can't use it again until you finish a long rest.
- **Savage Attacks.** When you score a critical hit with a melee weapon attack, you can roll one of the weapon's damage dice one additional time and add it to the extra damage of the critical hit.
- **Half-Orc Heritage.** Choose one lineage based on your upbringing and survival in the Flanaess:
    - **Bandit Lands Enforcer:** You spent your early life in the rough cities of the Bandit Kingdoms. You gain proficiency with **Thieves' Tools** and the **Stealth** skill. When you are in dim light or darkness and hit a creature that has not yet acted in combat, you deal an extra **1d6 damage** on your attack roll (once per turn).
    - **Pursuit Knight:** Driven by the injustice of your birth, you are sworn to end oppression wherever you find it. You gain proficiency with **medium armor** and the **Athletics** skill. When you move to intercept an attack targeting an ally within 5 feet of you, you can use your reaction to make yourself the target of that attack instead.
    - **Survivor's Grit:** You have learned that the world will show you no mercy, and you have built yourself into someone it cannot easily break. You gain proficiency in the **Survival** and **Athletics** skills. When you are at half your maximum hit points or fewer, you have Advantage on saving throws against being **Frightened** or **Charmed**.
- **Languages.** You can speak, read, and write **Common** and **Orcish**.
