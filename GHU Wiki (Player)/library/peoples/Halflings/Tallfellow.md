# **The History and Origins of the Tallfellow Halflings**

Known to the ancient Flan as the _Hobniz_, halflings are a cheerful, pastoral people who originated in the lush river valleys of the west-central Flanaess—specifically the Sheldomar, Fals, and Velverdyva basins. During the Great Migrations, they slowly spread alongside other races, migrating as far north as the Gamboge Forest and as far east as the Harp River.

The Tallfellows are the **least common of the three halfling ethnic groups**. While the Lightfoots settled heavily in human agricultural lands and the Stouts adapted to the rocky hills of the dwarves, the Tallfellows historically migrated to woodlands and forests, **usually living near elves**. They favor stable, peaceful realms ruled by stronger peoples, quietly maintaining their own shallow burrows and cottages in the safety of the forests and grasslands.

> [!multi-column] Contents
>
>> [!example|wide-3]- Physical Appearance and Dress
>> As their name suggests, Tallfellows are **about six inches taller than the typical Lightfoot halfling**, averaging close to 3½ feet. They possess a noticeably slimmer build and fairer complexions than their cousins. Like all halflings, their hair is naturally wavy or curly, though they generally lack the coarse facial hair common to the Stouts.
>>
>> In their daily dress, Tallfellows favor stolid, earthy colors—particularly brown, green, and tan—much like their gnomish neighbors. However, a quintessential Tallfellow outfit often features a **bright yellow shirt, topped off with a green cap adorned with a bright yellow feather**. They also heavily favor striped clothing. When preparing for a hunt or for war, they abandon these bright colors for a curiously mottled camouflage of intermixed greens and browns.
>
>> [!note|wide-3]- Culture and Personality
>> As a people, halflings are quiet, retiring, and generally do not seek out attention. They are fundamentally good-natured, have eagerly adapted to many of the creature comforts invented by humans, and strongly prefer a safe, comfortable life. Tallfellows share this peaceful demeanor, though their **close association with elvenkind** gives them a deep appreciation for the woodlands and a slightly more graceful bearing than the agrarian Lightfoots.
>>
>> Culturally, Tallfellows (like all halflings) employ naming conventions that outsiders find baffling. While they use a simple first name and a family/clan name, they obsessively use and identify themselves by an endless string of nicknames and pet names that other races find highly irksome.
>
>> [!warning|wide-2]- Subgroups and Factions
>> - **Highfolk (Valley of the Velverdyva):** This independent, 100-mile stretch of valley is the most famous haven for Tallfellows in the Flanaess. Over 2,000 Tallfellows dwell here in absolute harmony with humans, gnomes, and the massive populations of High Elves and Sylvan Elves. Together, they fiercely protect the homeland from the encroaching forces of Iuz.
>> - **The Ulek States & Urnst:** Scattered families of Tallfellows can be found living comfortably in the central and western regions stretching from the three Uleks to the Urnst states.
>
>> [!summary|min-0]- The Halfling Pantheon
>> The halfling pantheon reflects their love of home and their need for protection. The chief deity is **Yondalla the Provider**, goddess of protection and fertility. They also highly revere **Cyrrollalee**, the goddess of friendship, hospitality, and oathkeeping.
>>
>> For adventuring Tallfellows or those forced to defend their woodland homes, **Arvoreen** serves as the patron god of defense, war, and vigilance.

### **Playing a Tallfellow Halfling Character**

##### **The Tallfellow Mindset and Roleplaying Hooks**

- **The Woodland Scout:** Raised alongside the elves of Highfolk or the deep forests, you are more comfortable under the canopy of ancient trees than in a bustling human city. You blend halfling stealth with elven grace, acting as a quiet observer and protector of your natural borders.
- **The Reluctant Adventurer:** Like most of your kind, you vastly prefer a soft bed and a good meal to exploring dank dungeons. You likely took up the adventuring life only out of sheer necessity, and you rely on your natural agility and luck to survive a world that rarely takes you seriously.

##### **Fighting Styles**

Tallfellows blend traditional halfling weapons with the woodland arms of their elven neighbors. They are renowned slingers, but also train with the shortbow and the short sword under elven tutelage. They prefer to strike from concealment — the first arrow loosed from the canopy, then a fast withdrawal into the undergrowth.

# **Tallfellow Halfling Racial Traits**

> [!abstract] Overview
> The **Tallfellows** are the rarest and most elf-touched halflings — taller, fairer, and quieter than their cousins. Generations lived alongside the elves of Highfolk and the Gamboge have given them woodland grace, a deep connection to wild places, and a sling-arm touched by something very nearly supernatural.

---

## Physical Description

- **Skin:** Fair; often paler than Lightfoots or Stouts.
- **Hair:** Wavy or curly brown; rarely any facial hair.
- **Eyes:** Brown, hazel, or (rarely) pale green.
- **Build:** Slim and upright; about 3½ feet tall, around 40 lbs.
- **Dress:** Earthy browns, greens, and tans — often with a bright yellow shirt and a green feathered cap. Heavy striped fabrics; mottled green-brown camouflage for hunting.

---

## Tallfellow Halfling Traits

As a Tallfellow Halfling, you have the following racial traits.

- **Ability Score Increase.** Your **Dexterity** score increases by 2, and your **Charisma** score increases by 1, reflecting your nimble nature and the easy, charming manner your people cultivated living alongside elves. However, your **Intelligence** decreases by 1 — you follow instinct and grace, not scholarship.
- **Age.** A Tallfellow reaches adulthood at the age of 20 and generally lives into the middle of their second century.
- **Size.** Tallfellows average about 3½ feet tall and weigh around 40 pounds. Your size is **Small**.
- **Speed.** Your base walking speed is **25 feet**.
- **Lucky and Brave.** When you roll a 1 on the d20 for an attack roll, ability check, or saving throw, you can reroll the die and must use the new roll. Additionally, you have Advantage on saving throws against being **Frightened**.
- **Naturally Stealthy.** You can attempt to hide even when you are obscured only by a creature that is at least one size larger than you. In forested or natural terrain, you can attempt to hide even when only lightly obscured by undergrowth.
- **Expert Slinger.** Your people carry a deep, cultural mastery of the sling that borders on the supernatural. You gain proficiency with the **Sling**. You can draw, load, and fire a sling as a single object interaction (no bonus action needed to load it). When you hit with a sling attack, you can add your Charisma modifier to the damage roll (minimum +0).
- **Tallfellow Heritage.** Choose one specialized lineage based on your upbringing:
    - **Highfolk Skirmisher:** Raised alongside the elves of the Velverdyva valley, you have learned their woodland ways. You gain proficiency in the **Stealth** skill and the **Shortbow**. You have Advantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks made in forested or wilderness terrain.
    - **Sylvan Listener:** Years spent among the elves of Highfolk have left a mark on your spirit. You know one cantrip of your choice from the **Druid** spell list (Wisdom is your spellcasting ability for it). You also gain proficiency in the **Survival** skill and can communicate simple ideas (hunger, danger, direction) with Small or smaller woodland beasts.
    - **Reluctant Scout:** Your people's natural caution and grace make you an invaluable forward observer. You gain proficiency in the **Perception** and **Insight** skills. You can use your bonus action to take the **Disengage** action.
- **Languages.** You can speak, read, and write **Common** and **Halfling**.
