# **The History and Origins of the Lightfoot Halflings**

Known to the ancient Flan as the _Hobniz_, halflings are a cheerful, retiring people who have lived quietly in the Flanaess for centuries. The most common of the three halfling subraces are the Lightfoots, who are frequently referred to by scholars and other races as the **Hairfeet**.

The Hairfeet originally hailed from the lush, fertile river valleys of the west-central Flanaess—specifically the Sheldomar, Fals, and Velverdyva river basins. Unlike the Oeridians or the Suloise, the halflings never sought to build massive, continent-spanning empires. Instead, as the Great Migrations pushed populations eastward, the pragmatic halflings slowly spread alongside them, migrating as far north as the Gamboge Forest and as far east as the Harp River.

Throughout their history, lightfoot halflings have favored settling in stable realms ruled by "stronger" peoples, happily living alongside humans, dwarves, elves, and gnomes. They can be found in high numbers throughout the Sheldomar Valley, the three Uleks, the Urnst states, and heavily integrated into the Duchy of Ulek and the Free Assembly of the Kron Hills.

> [!multi-column] Contents
>
>> [!example|wide-3]- Physical Appearance and Dress
>> Lightfoot halflings are the smallest of their kind, averaging about **3 feet tall**. They possess lithe, proportional builds, with **ruddy complexions, brown eyes of various shades, and brown hair that is naturally wavy or curly**. Unlike the Stout halflings, Lightfoots rarely grow facial hair.
>>
>> They earn the moniker "Hairfeet" because a layer of light, downy hair covers most of their bodies, growing especially thick on the backs of their hands and the tops of their feet. Because they possess incredibly thick-soled feet, **lightfoots almost exclusively travel barefoot**, completely protected from the pain others would feel stepping on thorns or sharp stones.
>>
>> In their daily dress, lightfoots favor **earthy, stolid colors**—particularly brown, green, and tan—much like their gnomish neighbors. However, a quintessential lightfoot outfit often features a bright yellow shirt, topped off with a green cap adorned with a bright yellow feather. They also love heavily striped clothing. When hunting or at war, they swap their bright colors for a curiously mottled sort of green and brown camouflage.
>
>> [!note|wide-3]- Culture and Personality
>> As a people, the Hairfeet are **quiet, retiring, and generally do not seek out attention**. They are a pastoral, farm-loving folk who rarely get excited about grand politics or conquest. They are fundamentally good-natured and have eagerly adapted to many of the creature comforts invented by humans, strongly preferring a safe, comfortable life in their shallow, grassy burrow-homes or small cottages. Halfling adventurers are distinct exceptions to this rule.
>>
>> Culturally, lightfoots are famous for their baffling and highly informal naming conventions. While they use a simple first name and a family/clan name, they obsessively use and identify themselves by an endless string of nicknames and pet names that other races find highly irksome. For example, a halfling named Harriet Thorngage might be called "Goldie" for her hair color, "The Gager" from her last name, "Greensleeves" for her favorite shirt, and "Nettles" all within the same hour.
>>
>> Unfortunately, because of their small stature and natural stealth, lightfoot halflings live in a world that often does not take them seriously. They are frequently the target of torment, ridicule, and persecution by taller races who openly believe the prejudiced stereotype that "all adventuring halflings are thieves".
>
>> [!warning|wide-2]- Subgroups and Factions
>> - **Elmshire:** The most famous halfling homeland in the Flanaess, located in the Domain of Greyhawk along the southern coast of the Nyr Dyv. It is a massive, peaceful community of burrows.
>> - **The Guardians:** Because of the constant persecution and stereotyping halflings face, many dedicate themselves to the martial traditions of the "Guardian." These brave halflings serve as the fierce, disciplined protectors of their burrow communities, proving to the taller races that halflings are not to be trifled with.
>> - **The Ulek States:** Lightfoots live in massive numbers in the Principality and County of Ulek, where they are treated as equals and serve honorably in the local militias alongside dwarves and elves.
>
>> [!summary|min-0]- The Halfling Pantheon
>> The halfling pantheon reflects their love of home and their need for protection. The chief deity is **Yondalla the Provider**, goddess of protection and fertility. They also highly revere **Cyrrollalee**, the goddess of friendship, hospitality, and oathkeeping, whose faithful believe strongly in maintaining a well-kept home and defending the family legacy.
>>
>> The most important deity for adventuring or militant halflings is **Arvoreen**. Worshipped heavily by the "Guardians," Arvoreen is the god of defense, war, and vigilance, giving halflings the strength to stand up to the torment and ridicule of the larger world.

### **Playing a Lightfoot Halfling Character**

##### **The Lightfoot Mindset and Roleplaying Hooks**

- **The Reluctant Hero:** You love your burrow, your pipe, and your six daily meals. You are only adventuring because you absolutely have to (perhaps your village was threatened, or you are deeply in debt). You constantly complain about the lack of good food and soft beds in dungeons.
- **The Defiant Guardian:** You are sick and tired of humans looking down on you or checking their coin-purses when you walk into a tavern. You are a follower of Arvoreen, clad in heavy armor, determined to prove your bravery and shatter the "halfling thief" stereotype with the swing of a sword.
- **A String of Nicknames:** You have at least four nicknames you answer to, and you find other races' insistence on using only one formal name charmingly stiff. You introduce yourself differently to nearly every new acquaintance.

##### **Fighting Styles**

While some Guardians wield short swords or military picks, the traditional and most feared weapon of the halfling is the sling. Lightfoots possess a terrifying, innate mastery of the weapon. Halfling slingers will often barrage their enemies with a rain of bullets before darting behind larger allies or into the underbrush.

# **Lightfoot Halfling Racial Traits**

> [!abstract] Overview
> The **Lightfoots** are the most numerous and cosmopolitan halfling subrace, defined by their cheerful resilience, unshakeable luck, and astonishing capacity to live quietly alongside much larger peoples. Whether farming in Elmshire or standing shoulder-to-shoulder with dwarves in the Uleks, they embody the halfling gift for finding comfort anywhere.

---

## Physical Description

- **Skin:** Ruddy; warm peach to light tan.
- **Hair:** Brown, naturally wavy or curly; rarely any facial hair.
- **Eyes:** Brown in many shades.
- **Build:** Small and lithe; about 3 feet tall, 35-40 lbs. A layer of light, downy hair on hands and tops of feet.
- **Dress:** Earthy browns, greens, and tans — often with a bright yellow shirt and a feathered green cap. Almost always barefoot.

---

## Lightfoot Halfling Traits

As a Lightfoot Halfling, you have the following racial traits.

- **Ability Score Increase.** Your **Dexterity** score increases by 2, and your **Charisma** score increases by 1. However, your **Strength** decreases by 1, reflecting your small, light-boned frame.
- **Age.** A Lightfoot reaches adulthood at age 20 and generally lives into the middle of their second century.
- **Size.** Lightfoots average about 3 feet tall and weigh around 35-40 pounds. Your size is **Small**.
- **Speed.** Your base walking speed is **25 feet**.
- **Lucky and Brave.** When you roll a 1 on the d20 for an attack roll, ability check, or saving throw, you can reroll the die and must use the new roll. Additionally, you have Advantage on saving throws against being **Frightened**.
- **Naturally Stealthy.** You can attempt to hide even when you are obscured only by a creature that is at least one size larger than you.
- **Nimble.** You can move through the space of any creature that is of a size larger than yours.
- **Lightfoot Heritage.** Choose one specialized lineage based on your upbringing:
    - **Elmshire Slinger:** You have mastered the traditional weapon of your people. You gain proficiency with the **Sling**. You can draw, load, and fire a sling as part of a single action — no free hand required. When you score a critical hit with a sling, you can roll the damage die one additional time and add it to the damage (as a small-statured warrior's precision payoff).
    - **Arvoreen's Guardian:** You are a sworn defender of the burrows. When a creature you can see attacks a target other than you that is within 5 feet of you, you can use your reaction to impose Disadvantage on the attack roll. You must be wielding a shield.
    - **Irksome Nicknames:** You possess a dozen baffling aliases that you use to confuse outsiders and authorities. You gain proficiency in the **Deception** skill. You have Advantage on Charisma (Deception) checks made to pass yourself off as someone else or to deny your true identity.
- **Languages.** You can speak, read, and write **Common** and **Halfling**.
