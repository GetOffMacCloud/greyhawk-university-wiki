# **The History and Origins of the Goliaths**

The people who call themselves the *High-Kin* are known to everyone else in the Flanaess by a blunter name: **half-ogres**. Somewhere in their line, and usually not far back, there is an ogre. Nobody agrees on how it began. The Goliaths say giants walked among the first mountain tribes and left their blood behind. The lowlanders say what they always say about ogres and raided villages. Both are probably true in different valleys.

Whatever the origin, the result breeds true. Goliaths are a people now, not an accident: whole clans of them, with their own chiefs, their own laws and their own grudges. They have never held a kingdom. They hold the places nobody else wants: the dry steppes of Ull, the humanoid-ruled hills of the Pomarj, the broken floor of the Rift Canyon and the high passes of the Yatils. In 576 CY, they are the muscle behind other people's thrones, and more than one of them has begun to wonder why.

>> [!example|wide-3]- Physical Appearance and Dress
>> Goliaths stand **between 7 and 8 feet tall and weigh 280 to 340 pounds**, broad through the shoulder and long in the arm. Their skin runs from dusky tan to a grey the color of weathered stone, often with darker mottling across the shoulders and brow. Their hair is coarse and dark, their teeth heavy, and their brows pronounced. A Goliath can pass for an unusually large human only at a distance, and only in bad light.
>>
>> Dress follows the clan. Ull's Goliaths wear the felt and horsehide of the steppe. Pomarj warbands wear whatever they took from the last fight. Rift clans dress in dust-colored leather, and the Yatil mountaineers in layered wool and fur, with the iron-shod boots of the Perrenland companies.
>
>> [!note|wide-3]- Culture and Personality
>> Goliath culture is built around one question: *what can you carry?* Strength is the first virtue, but it is strength put to use. A Goliath who can lift a boulder and does nothing with it is a disappointment. One who carries a wounded clan-mate three days through the passes is a story told for a generation.
>>
>> They are blunt, slow to anger and slower to forgive. They keep their word in a way that lowlanders find inconvenient, because a Goliath's word includes the parts the other party assumed were negotiable. Among their own kind they are loud, physical and fond of contests. Among strangers they are quiet, because they have learned what strangers assume.
>
>> [!warning|wide-2]- The Clans of 576 CY
>> - **The Orakhan's Clans (Ull):** The largest Goliath population in the Flanaess. They serve the Orakhan of Ull as his heavy cavalry and his enforcers, riding the great steppe horses that can bear their weight. Baklunish by culture, Goliath by blood.
>> - **The Pomarj Warbands:** Since the Hateful Wars ended in 510 CY, the Pomarj has belonged to orcs, ogres and their chieftains. Goliaths born there grow up as warband muscle, feared by their orc neighbors and despised by the humans across the Wild Coast.
>> - **The Rift Clans (Bandit Kingdoms):** On the floor of the Rift Canyon, where the bandit lords' writ runs thin, Goliath clans hunt, raid and hire out. They are the closest thing the Goliaths have to a free people.
>> - **The Yatil Mountaineers:** The Goliaths of the Yatil passes keep an old pact with the dwarves of the deep holds, the "Iron and Stone," and hire out to the mercenary companies of Perrenland. They are the most trusted Goliaths in the Flanaess, which is not saying a great deal.
>
>> [!summary|min-0]- Faith
>> Goliaths keep no gods of their own. Those who worship at all follow the gods of strength and the mountain: **Kord** among the Rift clans and the Yatils, **Llerg** in the Pomarj, and **Ulaa** of the high places among the mountaineers. The Orakhan's clans follow the Baklunish gods, and a few of the Pomarj warbands have fallen to **Erythnul**, which the other clans do not speak of.

### **Playing a Goliath Character**

##### **The Goliath Mindset and Roleplaying Hooks**

- **Carry the Weight:** You measure yourself by what you can bear, and you measure others the same way. You do not understand people who have strength and hoard it.
- **The Ogre in the Room:** Every tavern goes quiet when you walk in. You have decided how to handle that: with a joke, with a glare, or by sitting down very slowly and ordering milk. Whichever it is, you have been doing it since you were twelve.
- **Somebody Else's Muscle:** Your people have fought in every war in the Flanaess and never once for themselves. Maybe that is fine. Maybe it is why you left.

##### **Fighting Styles**

Goliaths fight like the giants they claim: two-handed weapons, heavy thrown stones and spears, and a willingness to stand in the doorway and take it. The Orakhan's clans fight from horseback with lances. The Yatil mountaineers favor the great maul and the shield wall of the dwarven holds.

# **Goliath Racial Traits**

> [!abstract] Overview
> **Goliaths** are the giant-blooded folk of the Flanaess's margins, the people the lowlands call half-ogres. They are stronger than any other playable race, harder to put down, and shunned nearly everywhere they go.

---

## Physical Description

- **Skin:** Dusky tan to weathered grey, often mottled across the shoulders and brow.
- **Hair:** Coarse black or dark brown, worn long or shaved to the scalp.
- **Eyes:** Dark brown, amber, or a flat grey.
- **Build:** 7 to 8 feet tall, 280 to 340 lbs. Heavy brow, long arms, broad shoulders.
- **Dress:** By clan: steppe felt and horsehide, warband salvage, dust-colored leather, or mountain wool and fur.

---

## Goliath Traits

As a Goliath, you have the following racial traits.

- **Ability Score Increase.** Your **Strength** score increases by 2, and your **Constitution** score increases by 1. However, your **Intelligence** decreases by 1, reflecting a life in which nobody ever offered you a book.
- **Age.** Goliaths mature a little faster than humans, reaching adulthood around 16, and rarely live past 90.
- **Size.** Your size is **Medium**, but see Powerful Build.
- **Speed.** Your base walking speed is **30 feet**.
- **Darkvision.** Your ogre blood lets you see in dim light within 60 feet as if it were bright light, and in darkness as if it were dim light. You can't discern color in darkness, only shades of gray.
- **Powerful Build.** You count as one size larger when determining your carrying capacity and the weight you can push, drag, or lift.
- **Stone's Endurance.** When you take damage, you can use your reaction to roll a d12. Add your Constitution modifier to the number rolled, and reduce the damage by that total. Once you use this trait, you can't use it again until you finish a short or long rest.
- **Goliath Heritage.** Choose one clan:
    - **Orakhan's Clan (Ull):** You gain proficiency in the **Animal Handling** skill and with **Lances**. You can ride a mount one size category smaller than normal rules allow if it is a horse of the steppe breed, and you have Advantage on saving throws against exhaustion from forced marches. You speak **Baklunish** in place of Giant.
    - **Pomarj Warband:** You gain proficiency in the **Intimidation** skill and with the **Greataxe** and **Greatclub**. You speak **Orcish** in addition to your other languages.
    - **Rift Clan:** You gain proficiency in the **Stealth** and **Survival** skills. You have Advantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks made in rocky or broken terrain.
    - **Yatil Mountaineer:** You gain proficiency in the **Athletics** skill. You are naturally acclimated to high altitude, and you have Advantage on saving throws against the effects of extreme cold.
- **Languages.** You can speak **Common** and **Giant**. You can read and write them only if your clan or upbringing taught you.
