# **The History and Origins of the Rock Gnomes**

The gnomes of the Flanaess—known as the _Noniz_ in their native tongue of Neblin, with rock gnomes specifically called _Reloznoniz_—are a resilient, practical, and highly capable people. The surface gnomes originally hailed from the remote, northern wooded highlands, where they lived as simple herders and trappers. Several centuries before the Twin Cataclysms, they began to slowly expand southward, migrating into lands already inhabited by other species. Rather than conquer, the pragmatic gnomes recognized the authority of local elven and dwarven sovereigns while maintaining their own isolated, self-sufficient burrow communities.

It was not until the Great Migrations that the gnomes truly began mixing with other races, forming vital defensive alliances with elves, dwarves, and trustworthy humans to survive the invading hordes. That military tradition has never lapsed. In the Hateful Wars of 498 to 510 CY the gnome militias of the Kron Hills and Lortmils marched beside the dwarves and the elves of Celene to drive the humanoids out of the mountains, and in 576 CY their burrow communities in the Kron Hills, the County of Ulek, Veluna, Verbobonc, Highfolk, and Celene are prosperous, well-defended, and quietly expanding.

>> [!note|wide-3]- Culture and Personality
>> To outsiders, gnomes are widely regarded as fun-loving, artistic, and hard-working. They possess a renowned sense of humor and can find amusement in almost anything—even in the face of impending battles. Despite their jovial nature, they are exceptionally practical and tenacious, placing little value on empty honorifics or pretentious titles (gnomish warriors often affectionately refer to their female leaders simply as "mother" or "grandmother").
>>
>> Rock gnomes are undisputed **masters of the earth and the arcane**. They are widely celebrated as artificers, illusionists, and expert crafters of both fine textiles and complex stonework. Their skill with a hammer and chisel is legendary, rivaling even the dwarves; they have been instrumental in constructing magnificent architectural wonders across the Flanaess, from the pristine Mycla's Wall in Radigast City to the unique subterranean districts of Verbobonc.
>
>> [!warning|wide-2]- Subgroups and Factions
>> - **The Kron Hills:** The Kron Hills boast the largest concentration of rock gnomes in the Flanaess, with an estimated population of nearly 20,000. They have formed an independent realm known as the Free Assembly of the Kron Hills, ruled by the wise Urthgan, Eldest of Tulvar. They served with honor in the host that defeated the Horde of Elemental Evil at Emridy Meadows in 569 CY.
>> - **The Cairn Hills:** Several thousand gnomes inhabit the central Cairn Hills. Working closely with the Free City of Greyhawk, these gnomish miners extract the massive wealth of rubies, emeralds, and diamonds found in the northernmost promontory.
>> - **The Flinty Hills:** Defining the border between Nyrond and the embattled Bone March, where the marcher lords are losing ground to the humanoid tribes of the Blemu Hills, the gnomes of the Flinty Hills live under arms. An autonomous faction fiercely holds the eastern hills under the banner of Gnomeking Warren ap'Hiller, and every warren keeps a watch on the passes to the north.
>> - **Gnomeburg (Verbobonc):** Based on the brilliant designs of master gnomish architect Snirthiglin, a large population lives in Gnomeburg, a gorgeous district of Kron-style warrens nestled beneath preserved surface trees — like a living park.
>
>> [!summary|min-0]- The Gnomish Pantheon
>> While gnomes honor the broader deities of the Flanaess, they maintain their own distinct pantheon. The most favored gnomish deities are **Garl Glittergold** and **Baervan Wildwanderer**. They also pay homage to **Baravar Cloakshadow** (god of illusions and deception) and **Segojan Earthcaller**, the gnomish god of the deep earth said to have personally constructed legendary, indestructible stone walls hidden deep in the Yatil Mountains.

### **Playing a Rock Gnome Character**

##### **The Rock Gnome Mindset and Roleplaying Hooks**

- **Curious and Tenacious:** You are inquisitive by nature, eager to learn how things work, and pragmatic in your solutions. You are just as likely to use a clever illusion or a practical joke to defeat an enemy as you are a sword.
- **A Legacy of Illusions:** Your people have a deep, cultural affinity for trickery and arcane magic. Rock gnomes make unparalleled illusionist wizards and are adept at using magic to conceal their identities or protect their warrens from interlopers.

##### **Fighting Styles**

Rock gnomes rarely meet an enemy head-on. They rely on illusions, thrown alchemical flasks, hand crossbows, and short swords — favoring fights where terrain, surprise, and a well-timed cantrip tilt the odds. When forced into formation, they stand shoulder-to-shoulder with their dwarven neighbors in the Kron and Lortmil militias.

# **Rock Gnome Racial Traits**

> [!abstract] Overview
> The **Reloznoniz** are surface gnomes who have settled the hills and low mountains of the central Flanaess — masters of artifice, gemcraft, and illusion. Their cunning minds resist hostile magic, their small frames make them exceptional scouts, and their alliances with dwarves and humans have turned their warrens into some of the most prosperous communities in the west.

---

## Physical Description

- **Skin:** Wood-toned; from light ash to dark oak.
- **Hair:** Brown, blond, or auburn; males wear prominent beards trimmed to precise geometric shapes.
- **Eyes:** Brown, blue, or hazel.
- **Build:** Sturdy and proportional; 3 to 3½ feet tall, around 40 lbs.
- **Dress:** Stolid browns, greens, and tans — knee-length breeks, a tan blouse, and a dark jacket. Heavy accessorizing with gems, jewelry, and feathered hatbands.

## Rock Gnome Traits

As a Rock Gnome, you have the following racial traits.

- **Ability Score Increase.** Your **Intelligence** score increases by 2 and your **Constitution** score increases by 1. However, your **Strength** decreases by 1, reflecting your small frame and scholarly disposition.
- **Age.** Gnomes mature at the same rate as humans, and most are expected to settle down into adult life by around age 40. They can live 350 to almost 500 years.
- **Size.** Rock gnomes average 3 to 3½ feet tall and weigh around 40 pounds. Your size is **Small**.
- **Speed.** Your base walking speed is **25 feet**.
- **Darkvision.** Accustomed to life underground and in dimly lit burrow warrens, you have superior vision in dark and dim conditions. You can see in dim light within 60 feet as if it were bright light, and in darkness as if it were dim light. You can't discern color in darkness, only shades of gray.
- **Gnome Cunning.** You have Advantage on all Intelligence, Wisdom, and Charisma saving throws against magic.
- **Illusion Affinity.** You know the _Minor Illusion_ cantrip. Intelligence is your spellcasting ability for your innate spells.
- **Rock Gnome Heritage.** Choose one specialized lineage:
    - **Kron Hills Artificer:** You are a natural tinkerer, known across the Flanaess for craft and ingenuity. You gain proficiency with **Tinker's Tools** and **Mason's Tools** (you may substitute **Jeweler's Tools** for Mason's Tools if you hail from the gem-mining Cairn Hills tradition). You can use your tools to construct a Tiny clockwork device (AC 5, 1 hp) in 10 minutes. The device functions for 24 hours, then falls apart. You can have up to three at a time.
    - **Verbobonc Illusionist:** Raised in the gnomish warrens of the Gnomeburg district, you have refined your people's innate magical gifts. When you cast an illusion spell, add **+1 to the spell's saving throw DC**. Your _Minor Illusion_ cantrip can produce both a sound and an image simultaneously, rather than one or the other. You also know one additional cantrip from the Wizard spell list (Illusion school only).
    - **Lortmil Tunnel-Fighter:** Forged in the same underground wars as their dwarven neighbors, the gnome militias of the Flinty Hills and Lortmils are battle-hardened fighters. You gain proficiency in the **Stealth** skill. You gain a **+1 bonus to attack rolls** against kobolds and goblinoids (goblins, hobgoblins, and bugbears). You also have Advantage on Dexterity saving throws against attacks, traps, and area effects made or triggered by creatures of **Large** size or bigger.
- **Languages.** You can speak, read, and write **Common**, **Gnomish** (Neblin), and one extra language of your choice.
