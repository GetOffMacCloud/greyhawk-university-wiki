## 🌟 Overview

Trithereon is the god of individual liberty and its defense, the deity who specifically champions those who have been enslaved, imprisoned, or oppressed, and who teaches that every living being has the inherent right to be free and the right, even the duty, to fight for that freedom. He is Chaotic Good, and his chaos is specifically the chaos of authentic individuality: the refusal to fit neatly into institutional structures that would define you rather than let you define yourself.

His symbol is the triskelion, three spirals joined at the center, representing the three core principles of his faith: personal freedom, the right to retribution against oppressors, and the chaos of authentic life. He appears in imagery as a tall, wild-haired figure in traveling clothes, spear in hand, eyes bright with the particular intensity of someone who has committed to a cause completely. He is never depicted in institutional settings, always outdoors, always moving.

The church of Trithereon has a complicated relationship with Heironeous's church, both are Chaotic/Lawful Good respectively, both actively fight evil, but their methods and priorities create tension. Trithereon's faith does not build institutions, does not establish chains of command, does not submit individual judgment to collective decision. Heironeous's faith does all three. They fight on the same side of most conflicts and argue about everything else.

## 📖 Dogma & Core Teachings

### The Three Tenets of the Summoner

1. **No being may rightfully own another.** Slavery in any form, not just literal enslavement, but any system that removes a person's fundamental agency, is a violation that Trithereon's faith is committed to undoing. Every slave freed is an act of worship.
2. **The right to self-defense is absolute.** A person who is attacked has the right to respond with whatever force is necessary to stop the attack. A person who is oppressed has the right to respond to that oppression with whatever resistance is available to them.
3. **Retribution is justice.** Oppressors who are not punished continue to oppress. The faith specifically teaches that making oppressors experience the consequences of their oppression is a religious act, not merely a practical one.

### For Ordinary Worshippers

The faith's message is: you have the right to be free, and if you are not free, you have the right to fight to become free. This is a message with obvious appeal to those who are not free. The church actively recruits in areas under oppressive governance, in slave populations, and among those who have been imprisoned for activities that the faith considers legitimate resistance.

---

## ⛪ The Clergy

Trithereon's clergy are called Summoners, a title that reflects the faith's practice of calling individuals to their own liberation, summoning the courage required for self-defense, and calling oppressors to answer for what they have done. Summoners operate primarily in the field, not in temples; they travel to places where liberation is needed and provide both spiritual and practical support for those seeking freedom.

The church has no institutional hierarchy, individual Summoners recognize each other's experience and defer to seniority informally, but there is no chain of command that can be captured and forced to issue orders. This makes the faith extraordinarily resistant to suppression by governments that are its adversaries.

### Vestments & Appearance

Travel clothes suitable for combat, practical and non-restrictive. The triskelion symbol appears on cloaks, shields, and weapons. No elaborate ceremonial vestments; the faith does not have much use for occasions that require them.

## 🕯️ Prayers, Rituals & Holy Days

**The Day of Liberation (spring):** A celebration of historical and ongoing liberation, communities under Trithereon's influence commemorate specific acts of self-liberation (escaped slaves, successful revolts, communities that freed themselves from tyrannical rulers). New Summoners are formally recognized. The day also includes practical activities: training communities in self-defense, providing resources to those planning escape from oppressive situations, and demonstrating combat techniques.

**The Reckoning (autumn):** A day focused on retribution, the accounting of specific oppressors for specific acts. The Reckoning is not a festival; it is a formal process in which the faith identifies oppressors who have not faced consequences and commits resources to making those consequences happen. It is the most morally complex of the faith's observances and the one that creates the most friction with lawful-aligned good faiths.

---

## 🏛️ Sacred Sites

Trithereon's church does not maintain permanent sacred sites, fixed locations are targets for suppression by the governments that most frequently oppose the faith. The faith's sacred sites are places where significant liberations occurred: a crossroads where a slave caravan was ambushed and the slaves freed, a prison that was broken open, a field where a tyrant was brought to account.

## ⚖️ Divine Relations

**Allies:** Kord (individual courage and the right to fight back are deeply compatible), Pelor (the Sun Father's care for the oppressed overlaps significantly), Ehlonna (freedom of wild creatures connects to freedom of persons).

**Tense:** Heironeous (both good; fundamentally different approaches to how good operates, the Archpaladin's institution versus the Summoner's chaos), St. Cuthbert (law enforcement and liberation are in inherent tension; the Cudgel thinks some laws should not be broken; Trithereon thinks some laws should not exist).

**Enemies:** Hextor (organized tyranny), Iuz (slavery and oppression as governance), the Scarlet Brotherhood (human supremacy as a form of radical oppression).

## 🎲 Player's Guide to This Faith

### Seeking Help From This Church

Trithereon's church is generous when you're fighting oppression, specifically, when you are helping someone gain or maintain their freedom, fighting against slavery or forced service, or bringing an oppressor to justice. The church provides combat training, safe houses for those in flight from oppressive situations, and the practical support of experienced liberation operatives.

It will not help you if your goal is to establish or maintain a hierarchical institution that governs others without their consent, even a good-aligned one. This puts it in conflict with good-aligned characters who want to build things, which is productive table friction.

### What the Church Wants

- **Liberating slaves or prisoners** in territory under oppressive governance.
- **Protection of individuals** fleeing persecution.
- **Retribution against specific oppressors**, not assassination for ideology, but consequences for demonstrable harm.

### Clerical Advice

The Trithereon Summoner is the chaotic good character who actually has coherent reasons for the chaos, not disorder for its own sake but the principled refusal of hierarchy that removes individual agency. The most interesting version has genuinely thought through the faith's philosophy and applies it consistently: they will not follow orders they haven't agreed with, will not subordinate their judgment to institutional authority, and will not participate in anything that imposes control on others without consent. This creates genuine table tension with more institutionally-oriented characters and is wonderful when everyone is on board.
