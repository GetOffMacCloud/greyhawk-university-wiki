## 🌟 Overview

Brandobaris is the halfling rogue-god — the patron of the halflings who went on the adventure, who took the coin that was insufficiently guarded, who slipped past the guard and made it look easy, and who told the story afterward with appropriate embellishment. He is the halfling adventuring spirit personified: the willingness to do dangerous things for the thrill of it alongside the pragmatic self-preservation that keeps halflings alive while doing so.

He appears as a young halfling with quick eyes and an expression of someone who just did something clever and is still deciding whether to tell you about it. He is perpetually in the middle of an adventure that he appears to be enjoying more than is strictly rational. His mythology is full of impossible thefts, narrow escapes, and situations that should have ended badly but didn't — because Brandobaris's divine character is specifically the quality that makes the improbable not just possible but likely.

His relationship with Yondalla is warm but slightly complicated — Yondalla is the goddess of staying home and keeping the community safe; Brandobaris is the god of absolutely not staying home. They are not in conflict, but they represent genuinely different pulls on the halfling character, and most halflings feel both.

## 📖 Dogma & Core Teachings

1. **The close call is the best story.** Brandobaris's theology does not actually encourage unnecessary danger — unnecessary is the key word. The risk that produces no story and no gain is wasteful. But the risk that produces a spectacular story, even if it also produces a scar, is the faith's ideal.
2. **Be quick, be quiet, be gone.** The practical theology of the rogue tradition. Not violence, not confrontation — the elegant exit before the situation requires either. Brandobaris is not a war-god; he is a stealth-god, and his theology places the successful non-confrontational completion of a dangerous task above the successful fight.
3. **The scamp is never the villain.** The faith distinguishes between what it endorses (daring, mischief, the taking of things from those who have too much and are insufficiently careful with it) and what it does not (cruelty, harm to the genuinely vulnerable, betrayal of companions). Brandobaris is neutral, not evil; his theft is art, not malice.

## 🎲 Player's Guide

Brandobaris is the direct patron of halfling rogue PCs — his faith explains and endorses the character archetype in explicit theological terms. His clergy are the informal social network of halfling rogues in any community with halfling presence, and an introduction through the faith opens doors (and windows, and locked chests) that would otherwise be closed. A Brandobaris halfling is someone whose religious life and professional life are identical — their worship consists of doing what they do, doing it well, and telling the story afterward.
