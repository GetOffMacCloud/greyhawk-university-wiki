# Perrenland — A Traveller's Overview

*The Concatenated Cantons of Perrenland — a nation walled by mountains and lake, where the law is strict, the borders are guarded, and a sell-sword with a clean contract is more welcome than a king.*

> [!tip] Why this matters to you
> Ritterstadt and the Sepia Uplands sit within Perrenland's northern reach. Understanding how Perrenlanders think — about law, about outsiders, about mercenaries — is the difference between a warm hearth and a night in a canton lockup.

---

## The Land

Perrenland is one of the most isolated nations of the Flanaess, ringed on nearly every side:

- **West and south — the Yatil Mountains.** Towering, forest-cloaked peaks of 10,000–15,000 feet. Home to cave bears, mountain lions, the great **ernes** (giant eagles) of the Cloudpeaks, and worse. A few high passes pierce them, but winter snows close the roads for months.
- **East — the Clatspur Range.** Lower but even rougher, with no true passes. Snakes, giant spiders, and roving tribes of gnolls and bugbears make it no place to wander.
- **North — Lake Quag.** The third-largest lake in the Flanaess, rich with fish and waterfowl. Sailors keep the shore in sight: tales speak of a great whirlpool, the **Jaws of Quag**, that swallows boats caught in open water.
- **The foothills** — the Mounds of Dawn in the north and the **Sepia Uplands** in the northeast — are wild frontier where Perrenland's law runs thin. This is your country. See Regional Guide - The Sepia Uplands & Lake Quag.

## The People

Perrenlanders are, above all, **law-abiding**. Generations of invasion and occupation have made them **insular and fiercely proud of their homeland**. Outsiders — especially foreign **wizards** and **merchants** — are met with suspicion, sometimes open hostility.

There is one great exception: **mercenaries**. Every Perrenlander serves in the military, and many go on to form sell-sword companies. Because of this, foreign mercenaries are treated with grudging respect, so long as they keep the law. Perrenland is declared **neutral ground** — a place where rival companies set aside their feuds to trade war stories over a tankard. Sell-swords across the Flanaess treat the land as a second home and an attack upon it as an attack upon themselves.

The **highlands** keep the old Flannish ways: sheep-herders and farmers, matriarchal in custom, guided by the priestesses of the Oerth Mother. The **lowlands** are a mix of Oeridian and Flan, home to superb craftsmen and the finest merchants in the Flanaess — traders said to be able to "sell salt to a man in the Sea of Dust." Honesty and hard work are prized everywhere; laziness is despised.

## The Law (read this before you travel)

Perrenland is a confederation of semi-autonomous **cantons**, each with **near-absolute authority over its own affairs**. What is legal in one valley may be a crime in the next, and **ignorance of local law is never accepted as an excuse.** When you enter a new canton, ask what its customs are before you act.

Presiding over the whole is the **Voorman of All Perrenland** — currently **Karenin** — supported by three co-equal branches of government and advised by the guilds, the mercenary companies, and the priests and scholars.

You will most often meet Perrenland's law in two forms:

- **Canton constables** — small groups of lightly armored staff-fighters, watching for suspicious outsiders.
- **Border patrols** — mounted troops, often ten to forty strong, armed for missile and melee and usually accompanied by a priest. Expect to be questioned closely at any frontier.

## Faith

Perrenlanders are religious, though not zealots. At the heart of their faith stands **Beory, the Oerth Mother**, venerated as the mother of all living things — said to appear as a great **black she-bear** who leads lost children to safety. She is served by druid circles and by ranger-knights who guard the wild lands.

Alongside her, travelers will find shrines to the nature powers: **Atroa** of the spring winds, **Velnius** master of the north wind and the ernes, and **Ulaa** of the mountains in the highlands; **Berei** of the harvest and **Obad-Hai** in the lowlands. Merchants keep **Zilchus**, god of trade, and mercenaries toast **Kord** the Brawler.

## A Brief History

Long ago these lands were said to be a colony of a forgotten mountain empire, whose ruins still surface — and vanish — in the northern hills. After that empire fell, the Flan folk lived as herders and fishers, allied with the dwarves of the Yatils, who taught them metalwork, the pike, and the crossbow.

The nation itself was forged by the hero **Vartum Perren**, a ranger-knight who led the **Yatil Uprising** against a cruel foreign governor and united the cantons by 400 CY. Perrenland has since weathered conquest — most bitterly the "Decade of Tears" under a dread witch-queen — and emerged, after the recent Greyhawk Wars, as one of the strongest and most carefully neutral nations of the Flanaess. Its borders closed to all but mercenaries generations ago, and that welcome is why sell-swords still call it home.

## Travellers' notes
- **Mind the seasons.** The mountain passes close under winter snow and spring avalanche. Plan your crossings.
- **Respect the mercenary code.** If you travel as hired blades, keep your contracts to the letter — Perrenland remembers those who don't.
- **Ask before you act.** In a strange canton, custom is law.
