# Racial Relationship Matrix

*How people see each other in the Sepia Uplands — which is not necessarily how they see each other elsewhere in the Flanaess. The frontier has a way of blurring some distinctions while sharpening others.*

The relationships below describe **social assumptions and common attitudes**, not universal truths. Individual characters will vary. But if you are playing a character of a given ancestry, this is the ambient social weather you are walking into.

---

## Human Ethnicities

Five waves of migration produced the ethnic patchwork of the Flanaess. The northwestern corridor was one of the primary channels. The legacies of that forced mixing — resentment, assimilation, erasure, survival — are still present in 576 CY, expressed mostly in subtle social friction rather than open conflict.

### The Oeridians — *The Conquerors Who Stayed*

The dominant human ethnicity of the Sepia Uplands, as in most of the Flanaess. Oeridians brought the organizational structures that produced the Cantons, the knighthood tradition, and the organized religions. On the frontier, this cultural dominance is somewhat muted — when everyone is equally cold and equally subject to wolf attacks, ancestry matters less than it does in Schwungdorf — but it does not disappear.

**How Oeridians see others:** Tend to view Flan as quaint, superstitious, and in need of the progress that Oeridian civilization represents. Regard Suel with a mixture of wariness and intellectual respect. Consider Baklunish vaguely exotic and politically remote. Are deeply uncomfortable with Rhennee and generally show it.

**How others see Oeridians:** As the people who arrived several centuries ago and organized everything around themselves. Flan, in particular, have long institutional memories about this. On the frontier, where Oeridian institutions are thinner, this is more negotiable than in the south.

---

### The Flan — *The First People*

The oldest human presence in the Flanaess, displaced from their ancestral territories by the Great Migrations, absorbed into mixed communities, or driven into nomadism in the remote north and east. The Wolf Nomads are the most visible surviving Flan cultural tradition in this region. In Ritterstadt itself, most residents with Flan ancestry are two or three generations removed from any active cultural practice — but the blood is there, and occasionally so are the old reflexes.

The druids at Ehlonna's Mount are predominantly of Flan descent. Their relationship to the land and its spiritual geography is not superstition — it is knowledge, accumulated across generations, about things that are actually present and active in the soil underfoot.

**How Flan see others:** Oeridians are the people who built governments on top of Flan burial mounds. The relationship is complicated. Suel are seen as alien and fundamentally incomprehensible. Baklunish are fellow victims of catastrophe, which creates an unexpected solidarity in some contexts. Demi-humans, especially elves, are old enough to remember the world before the Migrations and are treated with corresponding respect.

**How others see Flan:** Common folk tend to view Flan ancestry as rustic, earthy, possibly superstitious. Those same common folk then knock on the tree-shrine at the forest edge during Growfest because they've lived in the Sepia Uplands long enough to take the Flan's spiritual instincts seriously.

---

### The Suel — *The Survivors*

The Suel Imperium was annihilated in the Rain of Colorless Fire in -422 CY. The survivors fled east, carrying their libraries, their genetics, and a racial trauma of sufficient magnitude that it has not fully resolved in nearly a thousand years. The Suel present in the northern Flanaess are typically scholars, isolated monastery communities, or individuals with conspicuously vague reasons for being so far from anywhere.

Suel are recognizable: pale skin ranging from alabaster to snow, hair from platinum white to pale blonde, blue or violet eyes. In a region of olive-skinned Oeridians and bronze-skinned Flan, a Suel stands out.

**How Suel see others:** A complex mix of intellectual superiority (the Suel Imperium was genuinely sophisticated before it destroyed itself), survivor's guilt, and the particular arrogance of people who know they are impressive and suspect they do not deserve to be. Baklunish are the people who fired the Invoked Devastation in response to the Suel's Rain of Colorless Fire; the historical hostility is technically mutual and practically old enough that most people on both sides have moved past it, mostly.

**How others see Suel:** Unsettling. Too pale, too quiet, and with a habit of knowing things they shouldn't. Respected for their scholarship; approached cautiously for everything else.

---

### The Baklunish — *Traders at the Edge*
Rare in the Sepia Uplands — most Baklunish presence in the northwestern Flanaess is commercial. Golden-hued skin, dark eyes and hair, associated with elemental magical traditions and extensive trade networks. Their heartlands are far to the west, and a Baklunish character in Ritterstadt will be understood as someone whose reason for being this far east is probably interesting.

**How Baklunish see others:** Vary by origin and guild affiliation. Generally pragmatic, commercially oriented, and inclined to see the world in terms of what can be traded and with whom.

**How others see Baklunish:** Exotic, wealthy (or assumed to be), probably useful, possibly suspect. The assumption that a Baklunish trader has access to resources and networks that local people do not is generally accurate.

---

### The Rhennee — *The River People*
The Rhennee are a mystery: a small, dark, wiry people who arrived in the Flanaess from somewhere else, live primarily on barges, and maintain their own secret laws and customs. They are widely distrusted. This is partly prejudice, partly the documented fact that Rhennee social norms around property and obligation do not map cleanly onto settled folk assumptions.

A Rhennee PC in Ritterstadt is unusual — the uplands are not prime barge country — and will carry the weight of their people's reputation. This can be played. It is not a comfortable position.

## Demi-Human Peoples

### Dwarves — *Respected, Autonomous, Impenetrable*

The dwarven clanholds of the Clatspurs are the most enduring political fact of the region. Dwarves are respected by common folk as master craftspeople and reliable (if not warm) trading partners. Hogar Irongrip's presence in Ritterstadt is considered an asset by everyone in the hamlet, even those who find him difficult to read.

Dwarves in the Sepia Uplands view the human settlement with the patient wariness of people who have watched settlements come and go. A dwarf who has chosen to be in Ritterstadt — as opposed to passing through or doing business — has made a significant cultural statement.

**Dwarf → Human:** Pragmatic cooperation. Trust is built slowly and lasts. Patience for human short-termism is limited but professionally maintained.
**Dwarf → Elf:** Ancient, complicated, and not discussed in mixed company.
**Dwarf → Gnome:** Commercial partners; occasional intellectual rivalry about engineering approaches.
**Dwarf → Halfling:** Underestimated, which is the halfling's advantage — dwarves know this and find it irritating.

---

### Elves — *Ancient, Watching, Selective*

Elves are rare in the Sepia Uplands proper. Their presence is felt more than seen: in the Vesve forest to the east, where Grey and Sylvan elves maintain border watch; in occasional scholars passing through who are either very young (and doing something they'll regret telling their elders) or very old (and doing something no one else understands yet). High elves represent the Greyhawk University's academic establishment in elven culture — rare, prestigious, and slightly insufferable about it.

Elves remember the pre-Migration world. This gives them context that humans lack and patience that humans find either reassuring or deeply aggravating depending on the situation.

**Elf → Human:** Watching. Not hostile. Aware that humans make large decisions quickly and live with the consequences briefly. Finding this simultaneously admirable and exhausting.
**Elf → Dwarf:** Ancient professional friction with deep mutual respect beneath it. They have been doing this for a very long time.
**Elf → Gnome:** Warm. Gnomish curiosity and elven long-view align more comfortably than either admits.

---

### Gnomes — *Useful, Underestimated, Quietly Correct*

Rock Gnomes dominate the eastern Sepia Uplands valleys. They know the mineral geography of the region better than anyone. They are natural illusionists, engineers, and collectors of information. The common assumption that gnomes are harmless eccentrics is one that gnomes have been actively cultivating for centuries because it is operationally useful.

Do not be condescending to a gnome prospector who knows the specific rust-red mineral composition of a particular hex better than your field report does.

**Gnome → Human:** Tolerant amusement. Humans try hard and move fast and occasionally stumble onto useful things.
**Gnome → Dwarf:** See above: friendly rivalry about engineering.
**Gnome → Halfling:** Natural allies. Both groups have mastered the advantages of being underestimated.

---

### Halflings — *Present, Practical, Underrated*

Valley halfling communities occupy the most sheltered and productive terrain in the eastern Sepia. They are superb scouts, traders, and information carriers. Their knowledge of valley paths is worth more than most folk realize. They also have a centuries-old relationship with gnome communities that makes them part of the most useful commercial-intelligence network in the region.

A halfling who has decided to operate out of Ritterstadt rather than a valley community has usually made this choice deliberately and has reasons.

**Halfling → Everyone:** Cheerfully unpredictable. What halflings actually think of you will be revealed incrementally and may surprise you.

---

### Goliaths — *Unknown, High, Cold*

Goliaths of the Clatspur heights are encountered rarely — less than once a year in most Ritterstadt residents' experience. They are not unfriendly. They are also not interested in most of what lowland communities are doing. A Goliath student at the University has made an unusual choice that will not be fully explained and does not need to be.

Common folk in Ritterstadt have no inherited opinions about Goliaths because they have barely encountered them. This is, in its way, a clean slate.

## The Short Matrix

| **If you are...** | **Oeridian** | **Flan** | **Suel** | **Dwarf** | **Elf** | **Gnome** | **Halfling** |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Oeridian sees you as:** | Peer | Rustic | Eerie | Useful ally | Unsettling elder | Clever odd-job | Overlooked |
| **Flan sees you as:** | The occupiers | Kin | Alien | Old enough to remember | Old enough to *really* remember | Practical friend | Shrewd equal |
| **Suel sees you as:** | The administrators | Simple but grounded | Fellow exile | Stubborn craftspeople | Distant peers | Amusing tinkerers | Irrelevant |
| **Dwarf sees you as:** | Short-term thinker | Original people | Strange survivors | Clan or not-clan | Ancient complicated thing | Friendly rival | Annoyingly good at hiding |
| **Elf sees you as:** | Brief | Original | Tragic | Ancient professional | Kin or rival | Curious companion | Surprisingly wise |
| **Gnome sees you as:** | Useful | Lore-keeper | Interesting library | Business partner | Valuable perspective | Colleague | Natural ally |
| **Halfling sees you as:** | The default | Home-keeper | Mysterious | Reliable customer | Impressive | Best friend | Family |

*These are ambient attitudes, not destinies. Play against them or into them as your character demands.*
