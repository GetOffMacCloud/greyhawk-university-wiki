# West Marches Hex Map
## The Ritterstadt Frontier

*A local coordinate grid for wilderness navigation from Ritterstadt. Assembled from field surveys, old Perrenish cartography, and hard-won expedition reports.*

> [!info] Reading This Map
> **Columns** run A–J, west to east. **Rows** run 1–8, north to south.
> **Each hex = approximately 6 miles** across (one hard day's travel through open terrain; two through marsh or highlands).
> Ritterstadt sits at **Hex D5**, on the northeastern shore of Lake Quag.

## Notes on Unmapped Territory

The current survey covers a **48 × 48 mile radius** around Ritterstadt — sufficient for most expeditions at the current tier of play. Beyond this grid:

- **North of row 1**: The deep Quag Mires, rumoured to extend 80 miles before giving way to the Wolf Nomads' steppes. Far beyond — some 250 miles northeast, past the nomad lands toward the Burneal Forest — lie the true **Cold Marshes** of the north, a different and darker country entirely.
- **East of column J**: The high Sepia Uplands, eventually approaching the Vesve Forest.
- **South of row 8**: The Clatspur Mountain passes and, beyond them, the southern reaches of the Flanaess.
- **West of column A (across Lake Quag)**: Perrenland's Kantons — a different political and cultural sphere.
