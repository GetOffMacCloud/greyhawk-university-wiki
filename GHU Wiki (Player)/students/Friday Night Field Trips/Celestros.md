### Class Features

**Spellcasting** *(CHA)* — Spell Save DC 14 | Spell Attack Bonus +6. Known-spell caster; arcane focus.

**Sorcerous Origin: Wild Magic**

- **Wild Magic Surge** — Once per turn, after casting a sorcerer spell of 1st level or higher, the DM can have Celestros roll a d20; on a 1, roll on the Wild Magic Surge table.
- **Tides of Chaos** *(1 / Long Rest)* — Advantage on one attack roll, ability check, or saving throw. Before it's regained, the DM can trigger a surge roll after a leveled sorcerer spell, which restores it.

**Font of Magic** — 3 sorcery points per long rest. Convert points ↔ spell slots as a bonus action.

**Metamagic**

- **Quickened Spell** *(2 SP)* — Cast a 1-action spell as a bonus action.
- **Twinned Spell** *(SP = spell level; 1 for cantrips)* — Target a second creature with a single-target spell.
