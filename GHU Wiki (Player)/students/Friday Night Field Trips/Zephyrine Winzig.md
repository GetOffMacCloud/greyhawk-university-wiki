## 🧑‍🎓 Student Profile

### Background
*Criminal / Spy — A black sheep of her fey court, Zephyrine ("Zeffy") was always getting herself into dicey situations. Thinking herself quite the brawler, she picked a fight as a youngster with a much older and more powerful fairy — and lost. The older fairy left her with a scar, stole her weapons and treasures, and told her she would never be strong enough to amount to anything. Zeffy swore she'd get them back one day, with interest. She has been hunting that fairy ever since, hoping to redeem her younger self.*

*Always itching for a new adventure or skill, she doesn't sit still for long. A treasure to swipe or a shiny trinket to pocket is paramount to her — wherever the loot is, she follows.*

*On record: female, Small, 2'5", 20 lb. Long cobalt-blue hair, braided and woven with small non-magical trinkets and charms. A scar runs from her right temple to near her jaw. Large silver-gray eyes, pale skin. Wears muted greys, blacks, and browns to aid her sneaking.*
