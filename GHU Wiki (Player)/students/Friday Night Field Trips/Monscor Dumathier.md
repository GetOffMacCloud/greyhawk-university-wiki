## 🧑‍🎓 Student Profile

### Background
*Acolyte — Descended from gold dwarves, Monscor has served as a Life Cleric of Dumathoin and hails from Clan Hedelstone. The mountains were home for most of his life, and he worked as a stonemason. As a young adult his fascination with precious gems led him to Dumathoin. On a mining expedition his craft led him to a long-lost tunnel and an ancient gate, which opened as he approached: a robed stone arm reached through the portal, its hand holding a small, glowing rough red stone. A voice called, "Claim your Birthright and follow your destiny in the light of Dumathoin." The stone dropped into his hand, a familiar peace washed over him, and his path was clear.*

*(Original names the Galena Mountains and Ironspur mine — see Import Review.)*

*On record: male, 80 years, 4'0", 155 lb. Tan skin, hazel eyes, grey/silver hair.*
