### Background
*Far Traveler — Sybil spent a lifetime in the shadows, navigating bustling cities and desolate villages and teaching herself to steal — not out of malice, but necessity. Her skills grew sharp enough to catch the eye of Violet, an older, seasoned thief whose reputation chilled those who crossed her. Violet took Sybil under her wing and taught her lockpicking, tailing marks, gathering information, and the thief's mindset: anticipate others like a game of chess, where each move means freedom or capture.*
