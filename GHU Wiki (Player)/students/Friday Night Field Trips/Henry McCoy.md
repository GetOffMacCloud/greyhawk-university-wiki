### Class Features

**Circle Spells** — Your mystical connection to the hunt infuses you with the ability to cast certain spells. At 3rd, 5th, 7th, and 9th level you gain access to the spells listed for that level in the Tiger Nomad Hunt Master Spells table.

**Bonus Proficiencies** — When you choose this circle at 2nd level, you gain proficiency in two of the following skills: Stealth, Survival, and Perception.

**Soul of the Predator** — Starting at 2nd level, you can expend one use of your Wild Shape as a bonus action to take on a hybrid predator form — part druid, part great cat. This is not a full beast transformation: you keep your own game statistics, hit points, and equipment, and you can cast spells and maintain concentration normally.

**Druidic** — You know Druidic, the secret language of druids. You can speak the language and use it to leave hidden messages.

**Spellcasting** — Drawing on the divine essence of nature itself, you can cast spells to shape that essence to your will. See Spells Rules for the general rules of spellcasting and the Spells Listing for the druid spell list.

**Wild Shape** — Starting at 2nd level, you can use your action to magically assume the shape of a beast that you have seen before. You can use this feature twice.

**Druid Circle** — At 2nd level, you choose to identify with a circle of druids: the Circle of the Land detailed at the end of the class description or one from the Player's Handbook or other sources. Your choice grants you features at 2nd level and again at 6th, 10th, and 14th level.

### Species Traits (Baklunish)

**Pious Culture** — You have proficiency in the Religion skill.

**The Hakima’s Sight** — You possess a fragment of the prophetic "Sight" granted by Istus to the wise women of your people. You have a supernatural knack for anticipating danger.

**Baklunish Heritage: Jinn-Touched Lineage** — You know one cantrip of your choice from the Druid spell list (Elemental only: *Control Flames, Gust, Mold Earth,* or *Shape Water*). Wisdom is your spellcasting ability for it.

**Wanderer** — You have an excellent memory for maps and geography, and you can always recall the general layout of terrain, settlements, and other features around you. In addition, you can find food and fresh water for yourself and up to five other people each day, provided that the land offers berries, small game, water, and so…
