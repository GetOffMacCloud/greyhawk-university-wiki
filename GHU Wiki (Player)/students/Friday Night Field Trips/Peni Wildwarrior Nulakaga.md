**Action Surge** *(1 / Short Rest)* — One additional action on her turn.

**Martial Versatility** *(TCoE)* — At an ASI level, may swap a fighting style or a known maneuver.

**Ability Score Improvement** *(4th)* — ⚠️ not recorded.

**Martial Archetype: Blade Breaker** *(GHPP ⚠️)*

- **Martial Maneuvers** *(STR)* — Maneuver DC 14. **Maneuver Points:** 8 / Long Rest. One maneuver per turn.
- **Stance of the Blade Breaker** — Enter a known stance when rolling initiative, or switch stances as a bonus action. Known stance: **Steel Serpent** — reach +5 ft. on her turn.
- **Maneuvers known:**
  - **Arcing Iron** *(1+ pts · Bonus Action)* — Empower next melee attack this turn; one additional attack per point against each additional creature within reach.
  - **Iron Tempest** *(4 pts · Action)* — Until end of turn, weapon hits deal +2d6 and she may make 2 more attacks, each against a different target. *(Listed twice on the sheet.)*
  - **Refusal to Bleed** *(1 pt · Reaction)* — On taking damage, reduce it by 1d10+4. If from an attack, spend 3 more points to reduce all damage from that creature by the same total until the start of her next turn.
  - **Wall of Blades** *(2 pts · Reaction)* — When hit by a weapon attack, make a weapon attack against the attacker and may replace AC with that attack roll result.
