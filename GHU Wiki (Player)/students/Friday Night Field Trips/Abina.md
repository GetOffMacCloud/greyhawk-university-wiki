### Class Features

**Bonus Proficiencies** — When you take this archetype at 3rd level, you gain proficiency in Survival and Stealth. If you are already proficient in either, you may select an additional skill of your choice.

**Guerrilla Tactics** — You excel in hit-and-run tactics — striking from nowhere and vanishing before the enemy can respond.

**Iron Resolve** — Your dedication to the Iron League's cause has strengthened your resolve against magical control.

**Expertise** — At 1st level, choose two of your skill proficiencies, or one of your skill proficiencies and your proficiency with thieves’ tools. Your proficiency bonus is doubled for any ability check you make that uses either of the chosen proficiencies.

**Sneak Attack** — Beginning at 1st level, you know how to strike subtly and exploit a foe’s distraction. Once per turn, you can deal an extra 1d6 damage to one creature you hit with an attack if you have advantage on the attack roll.

**Thieves’ Cant** — During your rogue training you learned thieves’ cant, a secret mix of dialect, jargon, and code that allows you to hide messages in seemingly normal conversation. Only another creature that knows thieves’ cant understands such messages.

**Cunning Action** — Starting at 2nd level, your quick thinking and agility allow you to move and act quickly. You can take a bonus action on each of your turns in combat.

**Roguish Archetype** — At 3rd level, you choose an archetype that you emulate in the exercise of your rogue abilities: Thief, detailed at the end of the class description, or one from another source. Your archetype choice grants you features at 3rd level and then again at 9th, 13th, and 17th level.

### Species Traits (Wild Elves (Grugach))

**Ability Score Increase (Elf)** — Your Dexterity score increases by 2. However, your Constitution decreases by 1 (due to your slender, light-boned frame).

**Fey Ancestry** — You have Advantage on saving throws against being Charmed, and magic can’t put you to sleep.

**Trance (Reverie)** — You don’t need to sleep. Instead, you meditate deeply, remaining semiconscious, for 4 hours a day.

**Keen Senses** — You have proficiency in the Perception skill.

**Feral Instinct** — You have Advantage on Wisdom (Survival) checks to track creatures and Intelligence (Nature) checks to identify poisonous plants or animals.

**Trapmaster** — You gain proficiency with Thieves' Tools (used here as Trapping Tools). When you spend 10 minutes setting a trap in a natural environment, the DC to detect or disarm it increases by your Proficiency Bonus.

**Primitive Weapon Mastery** — You are proficient with Spears, Short swords, Longbows, and Nets. When using a spear, you deal an extra 1d4 damage on a successful hit.

**Illiterate** — You cannot read or write any language, including Common or Elven. You can learn to read and write only through a Downtime Activity spent studying under a tutor of adequate skill.

**Grugach Heritage: Deepwood Stalker** — You are a master of the silent kill. You can take the Hide action as a bonus action while in a forest or jungle.

**Bad Reputation** — No matter where you go, people are afraid of you due to your reputation. When you are in a settlement, you can get away with minor criminal offenses, such as refusing to pay for food at a tavern or breaking down doors at a local shop, since most people will not report your activity to the authorities.
