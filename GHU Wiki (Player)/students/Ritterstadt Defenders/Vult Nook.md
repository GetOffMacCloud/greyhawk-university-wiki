## 🧑‍🎓 Student Profile

### Background
*Sage — For centuries Vult's family kept archives in a secret royal library; Vult himself was transcribing a diary of a marked noble line when the catastrophe came. A deadly mist rolled in, screams first. His brother Nymm rushed out sword in hand and has not been seen since. In the crush to evacuate, Vult was trampled and the family heirloom — a headband of intellect — was broken. He still wears it; he feels his god's favor through it. He snatched up a discarded sword, found he had gained abilities like his brother's, and began directing the fleeing to safety as the mist swept in behind them.*

*His brother and his queen are missing. Knowledge is lost. He will collect it again. The pen is mightier than the sword — except when it isn't.*

*(Original backstory is Eberron-specific; see Import Review.)*

*On record: male, bald. Name: "Vult" for December; "Nook" for the book nook he was named in, reading on a cold winter's day.*

**Divine Sense** *(3 / Long Rest · Action)* — Until the end of next turn, sense celestials, fiends, and undead within 60 ft. not behind total cover, and consecrated/desecrated places.

**Lay on Hands** *(Pool: 15 HP / Long Rest · Action)* — Restore HP from the pool, or spend 5 HP to cure one disease or neutralize one poison.

**Fighting Style: Dueling** — +2 damage with a one-handed melee weapon and no other weapons.

**Spellcasting** *(CHA)* — Spell Save DC 12 | Spell Attack Bonus +4. Holy symbol as focus.

**Divine Smite** — On a melee weapon hit, expend a slot for +2d8 radiant (+1d8 per slot level above 1st, max 5d8; +1d8 vs. undead or fiends).

**Divine Health** — Immune to disease.

**Harness Divine Power** *(TCoE · 1 / Long Rest · Bonus Action)* — Spend a Channel Divinity use to regain one expended spell slot of up to 1st level.
