## 🧑‍🎓 Student Profile

### Background
*Gladiator — Ryth was born into one of the many aarakocra clans of the Skywatch Cliffs, where fledglings fly before they can write their names and grow into aerial duelists, messengers, or precision strike units. Ryth excelled at all three. While others studied strategy and patience, Ryth soared not just swiftly but beautifully, and the clan militia noticed — granting him the low rank of Wind-Talon, a scout and combat specialist.*

*He fought in the gladiatorial bouts his clan used to demonstrate dueling, recruit, and court sponsors, and showboating was fun — but he craved more. Reflecting on what was missing, he realized he didn't just want to win battles; he wanted witnesses, tales, legends carried not by guards and guild-masters or even kings, but by the world. He left his post — not dishonorably, but not silently either — to chase adventure and the fame of a renowned hero, wherever the wind takes him.*

*On record: Medium, 5'7", 90 lb.*

### Class Features

**Unarmored Defense** — AC = 10 + DEX + WIS without armor or shield.

**Martial Arts** — DEX for unarmed strikes and monk weapons; d4 Martial Arts die; bonus-action unarmed strike after the Attack action.

**Dedicated Weapon** *(TCoE)* — After a rest, designate one proficient simple/martial weapon (no heavy/special) as a monk weapon.

**Ki** *(3 / Short Rest · Ki save DC 14)*

- **Flurry of Blows** *(1 ki)* — Two unarmed strikes as a bonus action after the Attack action.
- **Patient Defense** *(1 ki)* — Dodge as a bonus action.
- **Step of the Wind** *(1 ki)* — Disengage or Dash as a bonus action; jump distance doubled.

**Unarmored Movement** — +10 ft. speed without armor or shield.

**Ki-Fueled Attack** *(TCoE)* — After spending ki as part of an action, make one unarmed strike or monk-weapon attack as a bonus action.

**Deflect Missiles** *(Reaction)* — Reduce ranged weapon damage by 1d10 + 6; at 0, spend 1 ki to throw it back (20/60).

**Monastic Tradition: Way of the Astral Self**

- **Arms of the Astral Self** *(1 ki · Bonus Action)* — Summon spectral arms for 10 minutes. On summoning, creatures of his choice within 10 ft. make a DEX save (DC 14) or take 2d4 force. While active: use WIS for STR checks/saves; unarmed strikes with the arms get +5 ft. reach and use WIS for attack and damage, dealing force.
