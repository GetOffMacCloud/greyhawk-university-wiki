### Class Features

**Spellcasting** *(CHA)* — Spell Save DC 13 | Spell Attack Bonus +5. Phin casts known Bard spells using Charisma. He can use a musical instrument as a spellcasting focus, and may cast known bard spells as rituals if they have the ritual tag.

**Additional Bard Spells** *(TCoE)* — Phin's Bard spell list includes the additional spells designated in *Tasha's Cauldron of Everything*.

**Bardic Inspiration** *(d6, 3 / Long Rest, Bonus Action)* — As a bonus action, grant a creature within 60 ft. that can hear him a d6 inspiration die. For 10 minutes, it can add the die to one ability check, attack roll, or saving throw, after seeing the roll but before knowing the outcome.

**Magical Inspiration** *(TCoE)* — If a creature has a Bardic Inspiration die from Phin and casts a spell that restores HP or deals damage, it can roll that die and add the result to the healing or damage. The die is then lost.

**Jack of All Trades** — Add half proficiency bonus (+1), rounded down, to any ability check that doesn't already include it.

**Song of Rest** *(d6)* — At the end of a short rest, friendly creatures who can hear his performance and spend Hit Dice each regain an extra 1d6 HP.

**Bard College: College of Lore**
- **Expertise** — Doubled proficiency bonus on two chosen proficiencies: **Performance** (+7) and **Stealth** (+8).
- **Bonus Proficiencies** — Proficiency in three additional skills of choice.
- **Cutting Words** *(Reaction)* — When a creature within 60 ft. that he can see makes an attack roll, ability check, or damage roll, expend one Bardic Inspiration die and subtract it from the creature's roll. He may do so after the roll but before knowing the result.

**Vetha-Touched** — Phin carries the Second Sight of the wise women. He knows the **Guidance** cantrip (Wisdom is his spellcasting ability for it).
