## 🧑‍🎓 Student Profile

### How he arrived
*Sunsebb 576 C.Y., first bell, the common room of the Krone und Kohl in Königsruh, with snow on the windows.* Every cup on the long table rang at once, the fire leaned sideways, and Ivan Lennon was standing on the end of the table in the butter with a letter from the Chair of Flan Antiquities in his hand.

He was the nearest enrolled student, in **Traft** on his family's business, when the Silbertal posting lost [[students/Ritterstadt Questers/Tuldrim Hammerfall|Tuldrim Hammerfall]] to a recall. Magistra Brantwald would not send five where six had gone. Nobody on Ritterstadt's faculty can cast *teleport*, so **the Ritterstadt council signed a Schuldbrief** to a Traft wizard to put him there in a single morning. Ivan didn't sign it. Ritterstadt will remember whose arrival it bought.

### Background
*Noble: Position of Privilege.* People assume Ivan has the right to be wherever he is. Common folk try to accommodate him; other nobles treat him as one of their own.
