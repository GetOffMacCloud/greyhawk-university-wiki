## 🧑‍🎓 Student Profile

### Background
*Courtier — Celenin moves easily among the powerful. He knows how to address a lord, navigate a bureaucracy, and read the currents of a crowded hall. Half-elven and raised near the Phostwood, he carries himself with a diplomat's grace and a soldier's discipline in equal measure — a rider and a retainer as comfortable in the saddle as at court.*

*No backstory has been written yet. What brought Celenin to Greyhawk University? Whom does he serve, and what does he seek in the Sepia Uplands? Fill this in after your first session.*

### Research Focus
During his first adventure, Celenin found a Dire Wolf pup. He has tamed it and it is now his ally. The end goal is that he would like to train the pup to grow up and eventually become his mount once its full grown.

**Action Surge** *(1 / Short Rest)* — Take one additional action on your turn.

**Martial Archetype: Cavalier**
- **Bonus Proficiency** — Proficiency in an additional skill of choice.
- **Born to the Saddle** — Advantage on saves to avoid falling off a mount. If you fall and descend no more than 10 ft., you can land on your feet (if not incapacitated). Mounting or dismounting costs only 5 ft. of movement.
- **Unwavering Mark** *(4 / Long Rest)* — When you hit with a melee weapon attack, you can mark the creature until the end of your next turn. While within 5 ft. of you, a marked creature has disadvantage on any attack roll that doesn't target you. If the marked creature deals damage to anyone other than you, on your next turn you can make a special melee attack against it as a bonus action with advantage and +1 extra damage.
