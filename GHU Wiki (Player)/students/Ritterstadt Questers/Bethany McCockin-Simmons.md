### Class Features

**Divine Sense** *(3 uses / long rest)* — As an action, detect good and evil. Know the location of any celestial, fiend, or undead not behind total cover within 60 ft., and anything affected by the *hallow* spell. Lasts until the end of next turn.

**Lay on Hands** *(20 HP pool / long rest — 5 × paladin level; **was 15, a Level 3 value**)* — Touch a creature to restore HP from the pool (1 HP per point spent), or expend 5 HP to cure one disease or poison.

**Fighting Style: Defense** — +1 bonus to AC while wearing armor.

**Spellcasting** — CHA modifier | Spell DC 12 | Spell Attack +4 | 3× 1st-level slots

**Divine Smite** — On a melee hit, expend a spell slot to deal 2d8 radiant damage +1d8 per slot level above 1st (max 5d8), +1d8 vs. undead or fiends (max 6d8 total).

**Divine Health** — Immune to disease.

**Sacred Oath: [[start-here/character-options/Oath of Trithereon|Oath of Trithereon]]** — *Summoner of the Triskelion*

*Tenets: Break the Chain · The Right to Answer · Retribution Is Justice · **Never Build the Cage***

- *Channel Divinity (1/short or long rest):*
  - **Sunder the Chain** — As an action, touch a willing creature (or yourself). End one of: **charmed, frightened, grappled, paralyzed, restrained**; for 1 minute it has advantage on saves/checks to resist that same condition. *Alternatively:* instantly break one set of **mundane bonds** you touch — manacles, rope, a lock, a collar — regardless of construction.
  - **The Reckoning** — Bonus action, name a creature within 30 ft; marked for 1 minute. First time each turn you hit it with a melee weapon attack it takes **+1d6 radiant**, or **+1d10** if it has reduced a creature *other than you* to 0 HP in the last minute. *Speak the accounting aloud — an unspoken Reckoning is only a grudge.*
- *Oath Spells (always prepared, don't count against prepared spells):*
  - **Compelled Duel** — 1BA, 30 ft, Concentration 1 min. WIS save or disadvantage on attacks against anyone but you; can't move away from you willingly.
  - **Heroism** — 1A, Touch, Concentration 1 min. Immune to frightened; temp HP equal to CHA mod (+2) each turn.
