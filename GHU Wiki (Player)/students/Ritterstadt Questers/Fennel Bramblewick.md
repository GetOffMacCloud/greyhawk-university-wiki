## 🧑‍🎓 Student Profile

### Background
*Outlander — Fennel emerged from the Underdark into a world that moves too slowly and keeps too many things nailed down. He comes from unspoiled underground wilderness that few surface-dwellers will ever see, and fewer still would survive. The natural world — above or below — is sacred to him in a way that paved roads and stone walls can never be.*

*He cannot stop touching things. He picks them up, turns them over, studies them, fiddles with them — and sometimes, regrettably, breaks them. He means no harm. He is simply a creature of intense curiosity living at slightly the wrong speed for delicate objects.*

### Class Features

**Spellcasting** *(INT)* — Spell Save DC 13 | Spell Attack Bonus +5. Fennel casts prepared Wizard spells using Intelligence. He can use an arcane focus or a spellbook as his spellcasting focus. Rituals can be cast from the spellbook without preparation.

**Arcane Recovery** *(1 / Short Rest)* — Once per day on a short rest, Fennel recovers expended spell slots with a combined level of 2 or lower (no 6th level or higher).

**Arcane Tradition: School of Divination**

- **Divination Savant** — The gold and time to copy divination spells into the spellbook is halved.
- **Portent** *(2 / Long Rest)* — On a long rest, roll two d20s and record the results. Once per turn, Fennel may replace any attack roll, saving throw, or ability check made by himself or any creature he can see with one of these foretelling rolls — declared *before* the roll is made.

**Background Feature: Wanderer** — Fennel has an excellent memory for maps and geography and can always recall the general layout of terrain, settlements, and other features. He can also find food and fresh water for himself and up to five others each day, provided the land offers such resources.
